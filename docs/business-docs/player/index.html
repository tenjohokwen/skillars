Player

An adult (18+) athlete who trains directly with a coach through Skillars — browsing coaches, doing assigned homework, and tracking their own skill development.

Who this persona is

A Player is the person actually being coached. Skillars supports two very different kinds of player account: an adult self-registered Player (this page) who manages their own account end-to-end, and a minor player profile created and supervised by a Parent (no login of their own — see the Parent page). Sports coverage centers on football/soccer today — the player profile asks for a playing position (Goalkeeper, Defender, Midfielder, Forward).

The one thing to know up front: booking a session is not something a Player does themselves today. See the callout in "Browsing the marketplace" below.

1. Registering as a player

Why it matters: this is Skillars' self-serve acquisition path for adult athletes — no coach or parent has to invite them first.

  1. Go to /player-register (not the generic /register page — that's a separate legacy form used for admin-style accounts, and using it will not create a usable Player account).
  2. Fill in: First name, Last name, Email, Phone, Date of birth, Password. Ticking the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy checkboxes is required before the "Create Account" button becomes clickable.
  3. The Date of birth field is validated live: if you enter a birth date under 18 years ago, a banner appears ("You must be 18 or older to register as a player") with a link to /parent-register instead — self-registration is adults-only by design; minors are onboarded by a parent.
  4. First/Last name are also scanned for accidental contact info (e.g. a phone number typed into the name field) and warn you before submitting.
  5. On submit, you land on /player/email-pending, which tells you to check your email and offers a "Resend email" button (60-second cooldown between resends).

In local/dev environments, no real email is sent. The dev-mode email service only logs the subject line and suppresses the body — this is a known, common point of confusion, not a bug specific to registration.

2. Verifying your email and phone

  1. Clicking the verification link in the email opens /player/verify-email?token=..., which auto-submits the token — you'll briefly see a spinner, then get redirected automatically.
  2. You land on /player/verify-phone: a 6-digit one-time-passcode entry (auto-advances between digit boxes, and auto-submits once all 6 digits are filled — pasting a 6-digit code also works).
  3. On success, you're taken to /player/profile-builder.

3. Finishing your profile

  1. At /player/profile-builder, the only required field today is Position (Goalkeeper, Defender, Midfielder, or Forward).
  2. Clicking "Finish Setup" creates your player profile and takes you straight to your Locker Room.

4. Your home base: the Locker Room

Why it matters: this is where a coach's homework assignments actually reach the player between live sessions — it's the retention mechanic that keeps a player training on their own time.

  1. Logging in always sends you through /player/home, which silently resolves your own player ID and redirects you to /player/locker-room/:playerId.
  2. If you have assigned homework, drills are grouped by the coach who assigned them, each shown as a drill card with a "Mark done" checkbox you tick off yourself (once checked, it can't be unchecked).
  3. If nothing's been assigned yet, you'll see an empty state instead.

5. Browsing the marketplace

Why it matters: this is how a player evaluates and chooses a coach to work with.

  1. Go to /marketplace (also reachable while logged out — it's public).
  2. You must type a city and search before any results show — there's no default/nearby listing.
  3. Once a city is entered, filters appear: District, Age group, Language, and a Sort option; results are shown as a grid of coach cards with a "Load more" button for pagination.
  4. Clicking a coach card opens /coaches/:coachId — their public profile: photo, verification badge, availability status, star rating (or "No reviews yet"), bio, specialties, languages, and price.

As of the current build, a Player cannot actually complete a booking from this screen. The profile page's call-to-action button only has real behavior for Parent accounts (it routes them to a buy-sessions or booking-request page). For anyone who is not a Parent — including a fully logged-in adult Player — the button reads "Sign up to book" and pushes you to /login with a return URL. Since you're already logged in, that's a dead end that loops you back to where you started. Booking today is a Parent-initiated action even when the person training is an adult self-registered player. If you want to actually get booked with a coach right now, that has to happen outside this screen (e.g. the coach books you directly, or a parent account requests it on your behalf).

6. Tracking your development

Why it matters: this is Skillars' core value proposition beyond just scheduling — a visible, ongoing record of skill growth.

  1. Go to /player/development/:playerId (linked from your Locker Room).
  2. You'll see a Skills Radar chart (multi-axis ability ratings your coach has scored you on) — read-only for players — with an optional toggle to compare against your original baseline.
  3. Below that, a weekly Skill Exposure chart shows which skills you've actually been training, highlighting any the system flags as neglected.

7. Uploading drill videos

  1. Go to /player/videos.
  2. A quota bar shows your storage/bandwidth usage before you upload.
  3. Click "Upload", pick a video file — a progress bar tracks the upload, and once processed it appears in your video list with a status card (this is where content moderation and, for minors, a parental-approval gate would apply — as an adult player that gate doesn't block you).
  4. Each video can be deleted from the same list.

8. Messaging your coach

  1. Go to /messaging: a two-panel screen — conversation list on the left, message thread on the right.
  2. Selecting a conversation loads its message history; type in the box at the bottom and press Enter or click send.
  3. Conversations only exist against a real, booking-linked coach relationship — you can't cold-message an arbitrary coach.

Conventions & Gotchas

Booking is not player-initiated in the current UI — see the marketplace section above. Don't promise a prospective player "browse and book" as a complete self-serve loop until this is wired up.

Use /player-register, never the generic /register page — they lead to different account types.

There's no player-facing "leave a review" screen. Ratings shown on a coach's profile are real, but nothing in the current UI lets a player submit one.

Related Personas

Parent

Books sessions and pays on behalf of players under 18 — and is currently the only account type that can actually complete a booking through the UI.

Coach

The other side of every booking, messaging thread, homework assignment, and development assessment a player sees.

Trust & Safety
