Player

An adult (18+) athlete who trains directly with a coach through Skillars — browsing coaches, doing assigned homework, and tracking their own skill development.

Who this persona is

A Player is the person actually being coached. Skillars supports two very different kinds of player account: an adult self-registered Player (this page) who manages their own account end-to-end — booking, paying, confirming sessions — and a parent-managed player profile created and supervised by a Parent (no login of their own — see the Parent page). Sports coverage centers on football/soccer today — the player profile asks for a playing position (Goalkeeper, Defender, Midfielder, Forward).

As an adult self-registered player you book and pay for your own sessions — no parent account required. Several of your pages live under /parent/... URLs; that prefix is historical, the pages are shared with parents.

1. Registering as a player

Why it matters: this is Skillars' self-serve acquisition path for adult athletes — no coach or parent has to invite them first.

  1. Go to /player-register ("Create Player Account") — not the generic /register page, which is a separate legacy form and will not create a usable Player account.
  2. Fill in: First Name, Last Name, Email, Phone Number, Date of Birth, Password. Ticking "I accept the Terms of Service" and "I accept the Privacy Policy" is required before "Create Account" becomes clickable.
  3. Date of Birth is validated live: under 18 shows "You must be 18 or older to register directly." with an "Ask a parent to register instead" link to /parent-register — self-registration is adults-only by design.
  4. First/Last name are scanned for accidental contact info (e.g. a phone number in the name field): "Contact details will be removed on save".
  5. On submit you land on /player/email-pending ("Check your email"), with a "Resend email" button (60-second cooldown).

In local/dev environments, no real email is sent. The logging email transport only records each send in the application log; the rendered email (with the verification link) is written to disk only when app.email.log.dump-dir is configured. This is a common point of confusion, not a registration bug.

2. Verifying and finishing your profile

  1. The verification link opens /player/verify-email?token=..., which submits the token automatically.
  2. By default (security.registration.phone-otp-required is seeded false) you are then sent to /login to sign in. When phone OTP is switched on you go to /player/verify-phone first (6-digit code, auto-advance/auto-submit, paste works) and from there to the profile builder.
  3. Signing in always routes through /player/home. If you have no player profile yet it sends you to /player/profile-builder ("One Last Step"): choose a Position and click "Finish Setup".
  4. From then on, /player/home resolves your player ID and opens your Locker Room.

3. Your Locker Room and dashboard

Why it matters: the Locker Room is where a coach's homework reaches the player between live sessions — the retention mechanic that keeps a player training on their own time.

  1. /player/locker-room/:playerId ("Player Locker Room") shows assigned drills as cards, labelled "Assigned by {coach}" when more than one coach has set homework. Tick "I've done this" to mark one complete; it then reads "Done!" and can't be unticked.
  2. With nothing assigned you see "No homework yet".
  3. /player/dashboard ("My Dashboard", the drawer's Dashboard entry) shows three tiles: Upcoming Sessions ("{n} upcoming"), Credit Wallet (your balance, opens the Credit Wallet page) and Pending Approvals ("{n} awaiting coach response" — your requests the coach hasn't answered).
  4. The drawer also lists Find a Coach, My bookings, Messages, Locker room, My videos, Credit wallet, Credit Statement, Your session packs and Player Development.

3a. Proving you are 18 or older (first chat or first booking)

Why it matters: the date of birth you type at signup is only a claim, and Skillars is for adults — players under 18 are served through a parent account. So that a minor cannot slip in with a made-up birthday, a self-registered player verifies their age once, with an ID document, the first time they try to book a session, buy a pack or chat with a coach. Nothing is asked at signup.

  1. Trying to book (/parent/coaches/:id/request-booking), buy a pack or message a coach before you are verified sends you to /player/age-verification ("Verify your age"), with a note that you need to verify first. The Player dashboard also has an "Age verification" tile showing your status.
  2. "Verify my age" opens Stripe's hosted page, where you photograph a passport, ID card or driving licence. When Stripe returns you to Skillars the page shows "Checking your document" and updates by itself, usually within a minute.
  3. Verified: you can chat and book, and "Continue where you left off" takes you back to what you were doing. If the date of birth on the document differs from the one you registered with, the document wins and your profile is corrected.
  4. Could not verify (blurry, covered or expired document): "Try again". You get a limited number of attempts per day (3 by default); when they are used up the page says to try again tomorrow.
  5. If the document shows you are under 18, your account is blocked and your sessions are ended. Nothing is cancelled or refunded automatically; support can review it, and an under-18 player can be registered by a parent instead.
  6. Skillars keeps only the result and your date of birth. The document images stay with Stripe and are deleted right after the check; deleting your account removes the record.

When verification is switched off (identity.ageVerification.enabled, off until an operator turns it on, and always off in dev/UAT) booking and paying work as before, but chat is closed in both directions until you have at least one completed session with that coach. A coach who tries to message an unverified player sees only that the player has not completed age verification. Players who registered before this feature are treated as unverified and are asked at their next chat or booking.

4. Finding a coach and booking a session

  1. Go to /marketplace ("Find a Coach", also public). Type a city and search — there is no default/nearby listing. Filters (District, Age group, Language, Sort) then appear; results are coach cards with "Load more".
  2. A card opens /coaches/:coachId: photo, verification badge, availability, rating and reviews, bio, specialties, languages, per-session price and the coach's session pack.
  3. The main button reads "Book a session" whenever you hold credits with the coach or the coach has a per-session price — no pack needed → /parent/coaches/:coachId/request-booking: pick a slot under "Select a time slot" (shown in the coach's time zone), choose a pack or "Per session" under "Use a session pack" (without credits it defaults to "Per session": you pay the coach's price when they accept, wallet credit first, then your saved card), check your "Payment Method" card, add optional notes and press "Request session". "Multi-select" lets you request several slots at once (paid by credit/card, never from a pack).
  4. Optional: if the coach sells a pack, an outlined "Buy sessions" button (the main button only for a coach with a pack and no per-session price) → /parent/coaches/:coachId/purchase-sessions: select the "{n}-session bundle" (it shows "Valid for {days} days from purchase") and "Confirm purchase" (a saved card is required — "Add card" if prompted).
  5. The same request limits as for parents apply: up to 10 requests waiting on one coach, at most 120 days ahead, and the price is locked when you request. Nothing is charged until the coach accepts.

5. Managing your bookings

/parent/bookings ("My bookings") gives you exactly the parent's actions on your own bookings: Confirm / Decline a session your coach proposed (payment is taken on Confirm), "Withdraw request" while the coach hasn't answered, "Request Change", "Cancel", "Request for the coach", "Message coach", "Coach didn't show" (from start + 15 minutes, until 5 hours after the start), "Confirm session" after the coach ends it, "Raise a dispute" (within 14 days, while a started session is in progress, or while a session waits for your confirmation) and "Receipt". The refund and forfeit rules are the same as for parents — 24 hours' notice measured from the original start, forfeits pay the coach, the coach may report you as a no-show from start + 15 minutes — see the table on the Parent page.

Money pages you can use: "Your session packs" (/parent/players/:playerId/packs, including "Pause pack"), the "Credit Statement" (/parent/credit-statement) with receipts, and the player subscription page (/parent/player/:playerId/subscription, reachable from the storage-quota bar on /player/videos). You also have the Credit Wallet (/parent/credit-wallet, the drawer's "Credit wallet" entry or the dashboard tile): your balance, your card, and "Cash Out" under exactly the parent's rules — 2.5% + €0.25 fee, refunded to the card payment that funded the credit (or your latest one), blocked while a dispute on your payments is open, and booking-only credit never cashable. This works because you pay for your own sessions, so your refunds land in your own wallet.

If the coach reports you as a no-show, or you cancel inside 24 hours, you get an email and an inbox notice saying the session is forfeited and no refund is due, with 14 days to dispute it.

6. Tracking your development

Why it matters: this is Skillars' core value proposition beyond scheduling — a visible, ongoing record of skill growth.

  1. Open "Player Development" from the drawer (/player/development/:playerId).
  2. The Skills Radar (scored by your coach, read-only) has a "Compare to baseline" toggle. Below it: Skill Exposure for "This Week" with neglected skills highlighted, a "Weekly Trend" chart, Skill Trends, performance reports and your Player Timeline.

7. Uploading drill videos

  1. Go to /player/videos ("My Videos"). A quota bar shows your storage usage.
  2. Click "Upload Homework Video" and pick a file (max 500 MB). A progress bar tracks the upload; the video then appears with a status card while it is processed and moderated. Only adult accounts can upload — a minor's profile is refused.
  3. Each of your videos can be deleted from the list.
  4. Below your own videos, "From the coach" lists session videos a coach recorded of you — watch only.

8. Reviewing a coach

  1. On /coaches/:coachId, "Write a review" appears once you have a completed session with that coach at least 7 days old and no open dispute with them; otherwise the reason is shown.
  2. Give a 1–5 star "Rating" and an optional "Comment" (max 1000 characters), then "Submit review". It shows "Your review is awaiting moderation…" until approved.
  3. "Edit your review" requires a new session since you last wrote it (30-day cooldown). Coaches can add a "Coach's response"; you can "Flag this review" on other reviews.

9. Messaging your coach

  1. Start a conversation with "Message coach" on a booking in "My bookings" or on the coach's profile — it only appears once a booking links you to that coach. As a self-registered player you must be age-verified first (step 3a), or, when verification is off, have a completed session with that coach.
  2. /messaging ("Messages") is a two-panel screen: conversation list on the left, thread on the right; press Enter or click send.

10. Your account and data

/profile ("My Profile") has a Privacy & data section ("Request export" of your personal data, and "Delete my account") plus your email notification preferences; /notifications is the full notification inbox behind the bell icon.

Conventions & Gotchas

Use /player-register, never the generic /register page — they lead to different account types.

Players must finish the profile-builder step before anything else works. Without a player profile there is no player ID, so the Locker Room, packs, development links and booking can't resolve — /player/home keeps sending you back to /player/profile-builder.

Your money pages live under /parent/... URLs (bookings, packs, statement, receipts). That is intentional reuse: the same pages, wallet cash-out included, serve both a parent and a self-registered adult player who pays for themselves.

Related Personas

Parent

Books and pays on behalf of players under 18 (and can manage an 18+ child too). Uses the same booking, pack and refund rules you do — the full refund/forfeit table lives there.

Coach

The other side of every booking, messaging thread, homework assignment, review, and development assessment a player sees.

Trust & Safety

As an adult player you sit in the unrestricted age tier — this explains what changes for the minors you'll see referenced everywhere.
