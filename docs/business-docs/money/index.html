Money & Commercial Model

Every rule that moves money in Skillars, in one place — how a session gets paid for, when the coach is paid, what the platform takes, and what happens on cancellation, expiry, dispute and refund.

Figures on this page are the current defaults. Most are runtime-tunable by an admin on the /admin/config page without a redeploy, so treat them as current defaults rather than constants. A few are fixed in code and need a release to change: the 24-hour refund window and the 30-day / 14-day pack-extension rule. Pack validity (90 / 180 / 365 / 548 days by pack size) is tunable there, but only for packs bought after the change.

How a session gets paid for

A family pays for a session in one of two ways: per session at the coach's listed price (from their credit wallet first, then their saved card), or by spending one credit from a session pack they bought from that coach in advance. Either way, money is taken by the platform when the booking is confirmed, and the coach is paid only after the session has happened:

  • The price is locked when the request is made. If the coach changes their price later, the family still pays what they were quoted. A coach with no per-session price set cannot be booked per session at all; only pack bookings work for them, and the booking page warns "This coach can only be booked with a session pack. Buy one to request a booking." (The profile builder requires a price, so this is rare.)
  • No pack is needed to start. The coach profile's main button reads "Book a session" whenever the family holds pack credits with that coach or the coach has a per-session price. If the coach also sells a pack, an outlined "Buy sessions" button sits underneath. "Buy sessions" is the main button only for a coach with a pack and no per-session price, and a coach with neither shows a disabled "Not bookable yet". Without credits the booking request page defaults to "Per session" and says "No session pack needed: you pay this coach's per-session price when they accept your request (wallet credit first, then your saved card)."
  • Nothing is charged until the booking is confirmed. Requests are free. A request the coach never answers expires after 48 hours (or at its start time). A failed card charge declines the booking.
  • The coach is paid per completed session. This applies to card, wallet and pack sessions alike. For a pack, the coach receives that session's share of what the pack cost, as each session completes. The 48-hour hold gives a family time to raise a problem before the money leaves the platform.

A coach without Stripe Connect cannot be booked. Booking requests to a coach whose Stripe onboarding is incomplete are refused at request time. Stripe account status is re-checked daily, so a coach whose account Stripe restricts stops being bookable even if a notification was missed. Payouts that Stripe refuses go on hold and are retried automatically once the coach finishes onboarding (an admin can also release them from the coach's revenue view on /admin/finance).

Booking request rules that affect money

  • Coach-proposed sessions need the parent's consent. A coach's "repeat next week" creates a proposal. Nothing is charged until the parent presses Confirm on their bookings page, and the parent can decline it instead. A coach cannot accept their own proposal.
  • Parents can withdraw any request still awaiting an answer. Nothing has been charged, so nothing is refunded or forfeited.
  • Request limits: at most 10 open requests per family per coach, and no further ahead than 120 days. This stops one account blocking a coach's calendar with free requests.
  • Batch bookings (several slots in one go, up to booking.batch.maxSize, seeded at 5) are paid from the wallet and the card, never from a pack. The review dialog shows the price per session, the total, and the wallet/card split before the parent submits.

Pausing a pack

A parent can pause a pack from the pack dashboard to stop the expiry clock, for example for an injury or a holiday. The limits are tight:

  • Once per pack lifetime. There is no second pause.
  • The pause can last at most 90 days by default. It must start in the future and before the pack expires.
  • Expiry moves out by exactly the pause length. The pack can't be used during the pause, but it stays usable until a future pause starts.
  • Bookings on this pack that fall inside the pause must be cancelled first. The first attempt lists them without applying the pause; the parent confirms cancelling them, and the pause then applies. Each cancelled session goes back to the pack (or to the wallet if the pack has already expired). The coach gets no strike. This two-step confirm is intended behaviour, not an error.
  • A paid booking inside its 24-hour notice window cannot be cancelled by a pause. The pause is refused until it is moved after that session. Otherwise, pausing would turn a late-cancel forfeit into a refund.

What the platform takes

The coach's revenue dashboard (/coach/revenue) shows commission, the amount released to their Stripe account, the amount still pending release (inside the 48h hold), and the amount on hold (frozen by a dispute or refused by Stripe). "Released" means the transfer has started; Stripe then pays the coach's bank on its own schedule.

New coaches wait longer. A coach's first 5 payouts (payment.payout.newCoach.releasedCount; only payouts of at least €20, payment.payout.newCoach.minReleasedNet, that were not taken back count) are held 7 days (payment.payout.newCoach.holdHours, 168h) instead of 48h. A card chargeback can arrive well after 48h, and with a brand-new coach there is no payout history to recover it from. The revenue dashboard tells the coach while this applies ("Your first 5 payouts are held for 7 days … 3 remaining.") and shows each pending payout's release date.

Sessions with a linked account are held for 60 days. If the person who paid for a session appears to be connected to the coach it is booked with (the same card, the same device, or the same email or phone number once formatting is ignored), the booking still goes ahead and nobody is alerted, but its payout is held 60 days (payment.payout.relatedParty.holdDays, adjustable from 7 to 120). If the normal or new-coach hold is longer, that one applies. Such a payout does not count toward ending the new-coach hold. Only the coach and the account that paid are compared, so a coach who is also a parent of a child trained by someone else is not affected. A coach cannot book their own sessions.

Credit wallet

As well as pack credits, each payer has a euro balance with Skillars. Refunds land here and are never sent straight back to a card.

  • The ledger is append-only: a correction is a new entry, never an edit. The statement at /parent/credit-statement is a true audit trail with a running balance.
  • Wallet balance is used first on every per-session booking; only the remainder is charged to the card.
  • Cashable vs. booking-only credit. Most credit can be cashed out to the card (minus the fee above). Some credit is booking-only and can never be cashed out: the refund for a session drawn on an expired pack when the cancellation is not the coach's fault — the family cancelling with more than 24h notice or pausing the pack, or the coach cancelling for an excused reason (mutual agreement, weather, illness, family emergency) — including when the pack expires between the cancellation and the moment the session would have been given back. The wallet page shows both amounts separately, and booking-only credit is spent first. Booking-only credit stays booking-only: if it paid for a session that is later cancelled or credited after a dispute, that part comes back booking-only too (only the part paid by card or cashable credit is cashable). Unexcused coach cancellations, coach no-shows and Skillars cancellations of an expired-pack session refund cashable credit.
  • Cash-out is for the payer: a parent, or a self-registered adult player (no parent) who books and pays for themselves. Both use /parent/credit-wallet under the same rules. It is blocked while any dispute or chargeback involving that payer is open. The money goes back to the card charges that originally funded the credit — split across several of them if one can't take it all — and only what they can't take goes to the payer's latest card payment. If one part can't be refunded, that part goes back into the wallet and the payer is told; the rest still reaches the card. A card payment Stripe no longer knows is skipped and staff are alerted; one that is only temporarily unreachable makes the cash-out fail with nothing taken (try again later).

Subscriptions

There are two separate subscription products. Both are called "tiers", so they are easy to confuse:

Coach subscriptions

SCOUT → INSTRUCTOR → ACADEMY. Paid by the coach to unlock platform capability. The gating is real: a coach on the free SCOUT tier sees a locked overlay instead of the session builder, and has no video storage.

Player subscriptions

ATHLETE → SEMI_PRO → PRO. Bought by a parent for a child, or by an adult player, to unlock video and skills-radar features. SEMI_PRO and PRO are yearly billing only.

Upgrades and downgrades are deliberately asymmetric. An upgrade is charged immediately (prorated) and unlocks only once that charge succeeds. A downgrade takes effect at the end of the current billing period, so the customer keeps what they paid for. Downgrading to SCOUT means cancelling at period end. If a payment fails, the subscription keeps its features for a 7-day grace period and is then cancelled, dropping back to the free tier. Saving a new card moves the customer's subscriptions onto it, retries the overdue invoice, and resumes a paused subscription.

Cancellations, no-shows, and refunds

Who cancelled, and how close to the session, decides the refund, whether the coach is still paid, and whether the coach gets a strike. "Refund" always means what the family actually paid (the locked price or the pack's per-session value), returned as wallet credit or a pack session.

  • Rescheduling doesn't reopen the 24-hour window. Notice is measured from the earlier of the original and the new start time. The cancel dialog warns parents about this.
  • A session that has started can't be cancelled. The family raises a dispute instead (see below). A live session nobody ends is closed automatically 2 hours after its scheduled end, which opens the family's confirm/dispute window. A session that was never started is flagged to admins and is never paid or refunded automatically.
  • The family confirms anything that wasn't a full live session. A Quick Complete, or a live session ended in under booking.live.minDurationPercent (50%) of its booked length, waits for the family: they are emailed at once and can confirm it or dispute it. Nothing is paid to the coach while it waits; if they do nothing it confirms automatically.
  • Excused coach cancellations (mutual agreement, health/medical, family emergency, weather) still refund the family in full but don't count against the coach, up to 3 in any rolling 90 days. Any more excused cancellations in that window earn a strike, so that coaches can't avoid strikes by always picking an excused reason. Cancelling a never-started session at or after its start time is never excused (calling off a paused, actually-run session is): it is a coach no-show strike and does not use up the excused allowance.
  • A refunded pack session goes back to the pack, or to the wallet if the pack has expired since.

Reliability strikes

Unexcused coach cancellations, excused cancellations over the cap, and coach no-shows (reported, a coach cancel of a never-started session at or after its start, or a family cancel after the coach never started) each add a strike. Strikes count over a rolling 30-day window:

  • 3 strikes → visibility reduced: the coach is listed below all coaches in good standing in search results.
  • 5 strikes → pending review: hidden from search and sent to the admin queue. This is not an automatic suspension, and by design the coach stays bookable through a direct link while flagged. An admin decides on /admin/coaches whether to suspend, reinstate, or add or remove strikes.

Coaches see and acknowledge their strikes at /coach/reliability, and can appeal a strike within 14 days. Strikes drop out of the window after 30 days, but the coach's status does not recover on its own: an admin has to reinstate a coach who is reduced or pending review.

Disputes and chargebacks

In-app disputes. A parent, an adult player or the coach can raise a dispute on a finished booking within 14 days, and a family can also dispute a session that is in progress or awaiting their confirmation. While a dispute is open, the coach's payout for that session is frozen (a session completed meanwhile is paid out frozen). The coach and the family can each have one open dispute on a booking; the payout stays frozen until both are settled. Each side (coach, or the family) can raise at most 2 disputes on a booking (disputes.maxPerBookingPerRaiser). An admin resolves it on /admin/disputes:

ResolutionFamilyCoach
Full / partial creditWallet credit, capped at what was paid minus anything already refundedPayout cut by the same proportion (or partly reversed, if already paid)
No action / coach warningNothingPaid normally
Uphold coachAny refund they already received is taken back from the wallet, up to the current balance and never below zeroPaid the full amount, topped up if the payout had been cut. The platform absorbs any shortfall.

Card chargebacks (a family disputes the charge with their bank) freeze the coach payouts and any pack paid by that charge. Wallet credit the charge funded is taken back, up to the available balance. If the platform loses the chargeback, the coach's payout is cut and the pack's remaining sessions are revoked. If it wins, everything is restored. When Stripe refuses to take money back from a coach who has already been paid, the amount becomes coach debt, which is deducted from that coach's future payouts. Every one of these events raises a "payment integrity" alert in the admin queue.

Gotchas worth knowing before you quote a number

Forfeiture hits the family. A family can lose a paid session in three ways: pack expiry, a cancellation inside 24 hours, or a player no-show. Each one emails the family (the parent, or the adult player who booked) and adds an inbox notice. The late-cancel and no-show emails say the session is forfeited, no refund is due, and the family can raise a dispute from their bookings page within 14 days. A refunded cancellation or a withdrawn request sends the family nothing. Forfeits are still the most likely source of billing complaints, so be upfront about them in marketing copy.

A card must be saved before buying a pack or a subscription. Their confirm buttons stay disabled until a card is on file, so a parent who expects to type card details at checkout hits this gate earlier than expected.

Config changes are not instant. Commission rate, strike thresholds, grace minutes and pause limits can be changed at runtime, but the values are cached for up to five minutes. A change that "didn't work" has usually not refreshed yet. A commission change never applies retroactively.
