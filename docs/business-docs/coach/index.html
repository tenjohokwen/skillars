Coach

The football/soccer coach who lists on the marketplace, runs sessions, and gets paid — Skillars' supply side.

Who this persona is

A Coach is a paid supplier on the marketplace: they publish a bookable profile, set their own availability, run live sessions, plan training content, and get paid through Stripe. Everything a Parent or Player experiences as "booking a coach" is this persona's supply — availability windows, pricing, session content, and reliability all live on the coach's side.

1. Registering as a coach

  1. Go to /coach-register. Fields: First name, Last name, Email, Phone, Password, plus Terms/Privacy acceptance.
  2. Submitting takes you to /coach/email-pending (same resend-with-cooldown pattern used across all three registration flows).
  3. Email link → /coach/verify-email → /coach/verify-phone (6-digit SMS OTP).

2. Building your public profile

Why it matters: this is literally your storefront — an incomplete or unpublished profile means zero marketplace visibility.

Despite its filename, /coach/profile-builder is a fully built 5-step wizard (not a stub) that must be completed before you show up in the marketplace. It shows "Step n of 5" throughout, with an icon and short label per step:

  1. Step 1 — Profile. Split into three labelled sections: Basic info, Location, and Languages. Your time zone is captured here and must be a real IANA zone — it's what every booking slot a parent sees is rendered in.
  2. Step 2 — Specialties. Sport/coaching specialties.
  3. Step 3 — Pricing, including your session packs. As well as per-session pricing you add session packs here — each with a session count, a total price in EUR, and a label. The helper text reads "Offer discounted multi-session packages. Leave empty if you only want per-session pricing." Read the warning below before leaving it empty.
  4. Step 4 — Availability. Add weekly windows (day, start, end); helper text warns "Add at least one weekly window so parents know when they can book you." Fine-tuned later at /coach/availability.
  5. Step 5 — Photo, uploaded directly from this step (or skipped) via a presigned upload — same underlying mechanism as any other file upload on the platform. Guidance: "A clear headshot builds trust with parents. JPG or PNG, up to 5MB."

Leaving step 3's session packs empty makes you unbookable — despite what the helper text implies. That text ("Leave empty if you only want per-session pricing") describes an option the booking flow no longer supports as an entry point. A parent's only route to booking you is: buy your session pack, then spend a credit. With no pack published, the parent's call-to-action leads to a purchase page reading "This coach has no session pack available for purchase," and they cannot proceed.

You will still appear in marketplace search, which makes this fail silently — you simply never receive requests. If a coach reports "I'm listed but nobody books me," check step 3 first.

Completing step 5 publishes your profile and takes you straight to /coach/command-center. Coming back to this URL later, once your profile is already complete, redirects you straight to the command center instead of re-showing the wizard.

3. Your command center

Why it matters: this is the coach's single main hub — a week-by-week schedule plus the live-session tools that turn a booking into a delivered (and paid) session.

  1. /coach/command-center shows a week grid of your bookings (prev/next week navigation) plus a sidebar of currently active clients.
  2. When a booked session's time arrives, an "Active Session" overlay takes over the screen for running it live.
  3. Ending a session opens a "Wrap-Up" sequence, then a post-wrap-up summary showing a "Session DNA" chart — a snapshot of what was actually covered.

4. Setting your availability

  1. /coach/availability shows a weekly calendar (prev/next week navigation) of your recurring availability windows and any one-off blocked time.
  2. An always-visible add/edit panel below the calendar lets you pick a day of week, start time, and end time, then save — no modal, so you never lose your place on the calendar.
  3. Existing windows/blocks can be edited or deleted directly from the calendar.

5. Handling booking requests

  1. /coach/booking-requests is your inbox: individual requests, plus batch groups (several slots one parent requested together at once), each showing the player, requested time, and Accept/decline actions.
  2. An empty inbox shows a simple "nothing here yet" state.

6. Planning session content

Why it matters: this is what separates Skillars from a bare scheduling tool — structured, reusable training content tied to each session.

  1. /coach/drills: your drill library, split into a "Platform" tab (Skillars' own curated drills) and a "My Library" tab (drills you've authored yourself), with a filter button to narrow results.
  2. /coach/session-builder/:bookingId: a 3-column planner — drill library on the left, the session plan itself in the middle, template/save actions along the top ("Save Draft", "Save Session", "Save as Template", and a shortcut to browse existing templates).
  3. /coach/session-templates: your vault of saved, reusable templates — rename or delete from a per-template menu.

The session builder can be feature-gated by subscription tier. Coaches on the lowest ("Scout") tier see a locked overlay instead of the builder — this is the per-tier feature gating mentioned in the subscription section below, enforced live rather than just described in marketing copy.

7. Getting paid

  1. /coach/payment-settings: connect your Stripe account (the "Connect with Stripe" button starts Stripe's own onboarding flow) — required before you can actually receive payouts.
  2. /coach/revenue: a date-range filtered summary of gross earnings, commission deducted, and Stripe fees, so you can see your real take-home at a glance.
  3. /coach/subscription: your own subscription tier (separate from a parent/player's subscription) — upgrade or cancel from a set of tier cards.
  4. /coach/reliability: your reliability-strike history (late cancellations/no-shows) with an "acknowledge" action — repeated strikes reduce your marketplace visibility or suspend you, per the platform's enforcement rules.

8. Managing your reviews

Why it matters: coach ratings on the marketplace drive bookings — understanding how you're perceived and responding to feedback helps you build trust and improve your service.

  1. Once parents or players have trained with you and left reviews, they appear on your public profile on the marketplace.
  2. Reviews go through a moderation process — newly submitted reviews may show a "Pending moderation" status before being approved and made visible.
  3. You can respond to individual reviews with your own message — this appears directly on your profile below the original review so other potential clients can see your commitment to feedback.
  4. Your average rating and review count are displayed prominently on your coach card in the marketplace and on your full profile page.

9. Messaging clients

Same shared /messaging screen every persona uses. Conversations only exist against a real booking relationship — you can't message a parent or player you've never been booked with.

Conventions & Gotchas

Your profile isn't visible in the marketplace until all 5 profile-builder steps are complete — a coach who registers but abandons the wizard partway through will not appear in search results.

"Placeholder" in a filename doesn't mean unfinished — CoachProfileBuilderPlaceholderPage.vue is the real, fully-built 5-step wizard despite the name. The same is true of ParentDashboardPlaceholderPage.vue, which is now a real parent hub; there are no remaining genuinely-stub pages behind these names.

Your time zone is load-bearing. The zone you set in step 1 is the zone every booking slot is displayed in to parents — they book against your calendar, not their own local time. It is validated as a real IANA zone at submission, so a malformed value is rejected at the form rather than silently breaking slot rendering later.

You define one active session pack at a time. Publishing a new pack tier deactivates the previous one. Parents who already bought the old pack keep their credits — deactivating affects what is on sale, not what has been sold.

Session builder access is subscription-tier-gated. Don't promise full session-planning capability to a coach on the entry-level tier without checking current tier gates.
