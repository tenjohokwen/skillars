Coach

The football/soccer coach who lists on the marketplace, runs sessions, and gets paid — Skillars' supply side.

Who this persona is

A Coach is a paid supplier on the marketplace: they publish a bookable profile, set their own availability, run live sessions, plan training content, and get paid through Stripe. Everything a Parent or Player experiences as "booking a coach" is this persona's supply — availability windows, pricing, session content, and reliability all live on the coach's side.

Every one of these pages is in the left-hand menu for a coach, grouped as Schedule (Coach Command Center, Booking requests, Availability), Coaching (My Players, Drill library, Session templates, My videos, Messages) and Business & account (Revenue Dashboard, Session Pack Offer, Payment settings, Subscription, My Reviews, Reliability).

1. Registering as a coach

  1. Go to /coach-register. Fields: First name, Last name, Email, Phone, Password, plus Terms/Privacy acceptance.
  2. Submitting takes you to /coach/email-pending (same resend-with-cooldown pattern used across all three registration flows).
  3. The email link opens /coach/verify-email, which verifies you and sends you to /login. Sign in with your email and password.
  4. On first sign-in you land on the command center, which redirects you to /coach/profile-builder until your profile is complete.

There is no phone-code step today. A /coach/verify-phone page exists, but it is only used when the platform setting security.registration.phone-otp-required is on. It ships off, and the configuration editor refuses to turn it on because no SMS provider is integrated yet.

2. Building your public profile

Why it matters: this is your storefront. If the profile is incomplete or unpublished, you get no marketplace visibility.

/coach/profile-builder ("Complete Your Profile") is a 5-step wizard that must be completed before you show up in the marketplace. It shows "Step n of 5" throughout, with a progress rail:

  1. Step 1 — Profile. Four labelled sections: Basic info, Location, Languages and Timezone. Contact details typed into your bio are stripped ("Contact details will be removed on save"). The Timezone is the zone every booking slot a parent sees is rendered in.
  2. Step 2 — Specializations and Age Groups.
  3. Step 3 — Pricing. "Per Session Price (EUR)", "Session Length" (every bookable slot in your calendar is this long) and session packs, each with a session count, a total price in EUR and a label. The per-session price is required. Packs are optional; the helper text reads "Optional: offer discounted multi-session packages. Families can always book single sessions at your per-session price."
  4. Step 4 — Availability. Add weekly windows (day, start, end); helper text: "Add at least one weekly window so parents know when they can book you." The timezone shown here is read-only and comes from step 1. You can change your windows later at /coach/availability.
  5. Step 5 — Photo. Upload a photo or skip this step. Guidance: "A clear headshot builds trust with parents. JPG or PNG, up to 5MB."

How families see your booking buttons. Because you have a per-session price, the main button on your public profile is "Book a session", which goes to the booking request page; the family pays your per-session price when you accept (wallet credit first, then their saved card). If you also sell a pack, an outlined "Buy sessions" button underneath goes to /parent/coaches/:coachId/purchase-sessions. A family already holding your pack credits books against the pack.

If a coach reports "I'm listed but nobody books me," check their Stripe setup first (see below). A coach with neither a per-session price nor a pack shows a disabled "Not bookable yet" button, but the builder doesn't let you finish without a price.

Completing step 5 publishes your profile and takes you to /coach/command-center. If you return to the wizard after your profile is complete, it sends you to the command center instead.

3. Your command center

Why it matters: this is the coach's main hub. It holds your week's schedule and the live-session tools that turn a booking into a delivered, paid session.

  1. /coach/command-center ("Coach Command Center") shows a week grid of your bookings ("Previous week" / "Next week"), an "Active Clients" sidebar, a "Projected Revenue" panel and a "Booking requests" shortcut button.
  2. If your Stripe onboarding is not complete, a banner reads "Complete your Stripe setup to accept bookings" with a "Set up payments" button that opens /coach/payment-settings.
  3. If any completed sessions have no recap, a banner shows how many ("n sessions need a recap"). Click "Show" to list them, with an "Add recap" button on each.
  4. Each booking block shows buttons that depend on its status:
    • Upcoming: "Start Session", "Quick Complete", "Prep", "Report No-Show", "Cancel Session", "Propose New Time".
    • Confirmed: "Prep", "Cancel Session", "Propose New Time".
    • Completed: "Add recap" / "Edit recap" and "Repeat for next week".
    • Awaiting wrap-up: "Resume wrap-up".
    • Finished or cancelled: "Raise a dispute" / "View dispute".
    Empty availability windows have a "Share this slot" button, which copies a booking link for that slot.
  5. "Start Session" opens the full-screen live-session overlay. Ending the session opens the wrap-up sequence, followed by a summary with a "Session DNA" chart of what was covered.
  6. "Quick Complete" skips the live overlay. A quick-completed session waits for the parent to confirm it; if they don't, it confirms automatically after booking.quick_complete_timeout_hours (seeded at 24). The server decides which kind of wrap-up you are doing. It only counts as a live session if you pressed "Start Session", ended it yourself, and it ran for at least booking.live.minDurationPercent (50%) of its booked length, so you can't skip the parent's confirmation by claiming a live session or by pressing Start then End. The family is emailed as soon as a session starts waiting for them, and can confirm it or raise a dispute. If you Quick Complete a session you never started, the family can still report "Coach didn't show" from start + 30 minutes.

If you don't press Start. The family can claim a coach no-show from 15 minutes after the start until 5 hours after it (the claim is a report, or cancelling the session): they are refunded in full and you get a reliability strike. Press "Start Session" first, even late, and no claim is possible; the family can only dispute. If you Quick Complete without ever pressing Start and the family then reports a no-show, neither side can be proven: the fee is split 50/50 (you are paid your half through the normal payout, with commission on that half) and nobody gets a strike.

Time gates on the command center. You can't start or complete a session before its scheduled start time. "Report No-Show" asks you to confirm ("Confirm that the player did not attend this session. This cannot be undone."). It is only accepted from the start time plus booking.playerNoShow.graceMinutes (default 15 minutes). Before that you get "A player no-show can only be reported once the grace period after the scheduled start has passed." A recorded no-show forfeits the session: you are still paid, and the family is emailed that no refund is due and that they can dispute within 14 days.

Abandoned sessions are settled automatically. A session left running or paused more than booking.unresolvedSession.graceMinutes (default 120) after its scheduled end is ended for you. It then waits for the parent's confirmation like a Quick Complete. A paid session that was never started is not paid out automatically; it raises a "Session ended without being started" alert for Skillars staff.

4. Setting your availability

  1. /coach/availability ("Manage Availability") shows a weekly calendar of your recurring windows and any blocked time.
  2. Below the calendar there are two panels that are always visible. Add Recurring Window has Day, Start time and End time fields and a Save button. Block Personal Time has Start date, Start time, End date, End time and an optional Reason, with a "Block Personal Time" button.
  3. You can edit or delete existing windows and blocks directly from the calendar.

5. Handling booking requests

  1. /coach/booking-requests ("Booking requests") is your inbox. It shows single requests (player, parent, requested time, any notes) with "Accept" and "Decline" buttons. It also shows batch groups ("name — n sessions requested") with an "Accept all n sessions" button. An empty inbox reads "No pending booking requests".
  2. Accepting charges the family. A request can't be accepted after its start time has passed ("This request’s start time has passed, so it can no longer be accepted."). Unanswered requests expire automatically after booking.request_expiry_hours (48 by default). If a request has no agreed price you'll see "This request has no agreed price. Decline it and ask the family to send a new request."
  3. "Accept all" settles a batch one request at a time. If some fail, you see "x of y sessions accepted", and the ones that failed are marked. If two people try to accept the same batch at once, only one succeeds.
  4. A family can have at most booking.request.maxPendingPerCoach (10) unanswered requests with you at a time, and can request at most booking.request.maxHorizonDays (120) days ahead.

"Repeat for next week" sends a proposal; it does not create a booking. Clicking it on a completed session creates a coach proposal ("Repeat session proposed — parent will be notified"). In your inbox it appears as "Proposed by you — awaiting parent confirmation". It has no Accept button, only "Withdraw proposal". The family confirms it (they are charged at that point) or declines it. Nobody is charged until they confirm. Reschedules work the same way: you can accept or decline a time the parent proposed, but not a time you proposed yourself. A session can be repeated once: while the proposal (or a family's own request for that time) is open, a second "Repeat" is refused ("This time slot is no longer available."). If the family's session pack is paused that week, or every session left on it is already held by a pending booking, the repeat is refused with that reason instead of being charged per session. A reschedule can't move a pack session into the pack's pause, or further ahead than a family could request (120 days).

6. Your players

  1. /coach/players ("My Players") is your roster. Each player shows when they last trained with you, a "Gone quiet" badge if they have been dormant, a count of neglected skills, and a "Send message" button. Players whose access window has passed are listed separately under "Lapsed players".
  2. Click a player to open /coach/players/:playerId ("name's Development"): neglected skills, coach contributions, a progress summary, the homework you assigned, and your targets.
  3. The "Prep" button on an upcoming booking opens "Session Prep": what you worked on last time, what's next, the focus thread, a private plan note ("Your plan note (private)", up to 500 characters) and a "Plan this session" button.

7. Planning session content

Why it matters: structured, reusable training content tied to each session is what separates Skillars from a plain scheduling tool.

  1. /coach/drills ("Drill Library"): "Platform Library" and "My Library" tabs, a search box, a "Filters" button (skill, difficulty, equipment, weak foot only), "New drill" and "Clone a platform drill".
  2. /coach/session-builder/:bookingId ("Session Builder"): drill library on the left (including a "Suggested" tab), the session plan in the middle, and "Templates", "Save Draft", "Save Session" and "Save as Template" actions.
  3. /coach/session-templates ("Session Templates"): your saved templates. Each has "Rename", "Delete" and "Create session plan", which applies the template to one of your bookings.
  4. /coach/videos ("My videos"): upload and manage your own coaching videos.

The session builder depends on your subscription tier. Coaches on the entry-level Scout plan see a locked screen instead: "Session Builder is available on Instructor and Academy plans."

8. Your session pack offer

  1. /coach/session-pack-tiers ("My Session Pack Offer") shows "Your current offer", the offers it replaced, and a list of "Sold session packs" (bought by, sessions left, expiry date, status). Each offer shows "Valid for {days} days from purchase": 90 days for 1 session, 180 for 2–5, 365 for 6–10 and 548 for 11+.
  2. "Create a new offer" or "Replace your current offer" asks for a Label, a "Number of sessions" and a "Total price (€)". Replacing an offer deactivates the old one straight away. Parents who already bought under it keep their sessions. "Withdraw offer" stops new sales without creating a replacement.
  3. A sold pack's status is Active, Paused, Expired, "Used up", Frozen (a card dispute on its purchase is open) or Withdrawn (with the reason, e.g. the buyer won a card chargeback).
  4. Within the 14 days before a sold pack expires, you can extend it once by 30 days ("Extend"). A withdrawn pack can't be extended.

9. Getting paid

  1. /coach/payment-settings ("Payment Settings"): the "Connect with Stripe" button starts Stripe's own onboarding. The page then shows "Charges enabled" / "Payouts enabled", "Setup pending — follow Stripe instructions" or "Account restricted — contact Stripe support". If you disconnect Skillars from your Stripe account, it reads "Your Stripe account is disconnected from Skillars. Reconnect it to receive payouts." with the connect button again. Skillars also re-checks connected accounts with Stripe daily, in case Stripe's own notification was missed.
  2. /coach/revenue ("Revenue Dashboard"): choose a start and end date and click "Apply". The tiles show Gross, Commission, "Released to your Stripe account", Refunds, "On hold" and Sessions, followed by a transaction table with a "View Receipt" link per session (/coach/bookings/:bookingId/receipt). Stripe processing fees are paid by the platform, not by the coach. Your payout is the session amount minus the platform commission (platform.commission.rate, seeded at 8%).
  3. /coach/subscription ("Coach Subscription"): your own plan, separate from any parent or player subscription. Upgrade, downgrade or cancel from the plan cards.
  4. /coach/reliability ("Reliability Dashboard"): your strike history (Date, Reason, Booking), with "Acknowledge" and "Appeal" on each strike.

When you get paid. A completed session's payout is held for payment.payout.hold_hours (48), then transferred to your Stripe account. While your account is new, your first payment.payout.newCoach.releasedCount (5) payouts of at least payment.payout.newCoach.minReleasedNet (€20) are held payment.payout.newCoach.holdHours (168, i.e. 7 days) instead, so card chargebacks can land before the money leaves the platform; the Revenue Dashboard shows a notice with how many of those first payouts remain, and each pending payout's release date. You are paid your normal net amount for card-paid, credit-paid and pack-paid sessions. You are also paid it when a session is forfeited: a player no-show, or a parent cancelling with less than 24 hours' notice. Payouts are frozen while a dispute or card chargeback on that booking is open, and while your account is under a safety hold. If your Stripe account can't receive money, payouts go on hold. They are sent automatically once your onboarding completes, and staff can also release them manually.

Cancelling and strikes. "Cancel Session" asks you to pick a reason. Mutual Agreement, Health / Medical, Family Emergency and Weather are excused. Scheduling Preference and Other are unexcused and always give you a reliability strike. Excused cancellations are capped: more than coach.excusedCancellations.maxPerWindow (3) in a rolling coach.excusedCancellations.windowDays (90) days, and the next excused one strikes too. The family is always refunded in full when you cancel. Cancelling a session you never started at or after its start time is a "Coach no-show" strike whatever reason you pick, and does not count toward the excused cap. Calling off a paused session you actually ran follows the normal excused / unexcused rules. Not turning up yourself is a "Coach no-show" strike too — whether the family reports it, or cancels once start + 30 minutes has passed without you starting the session.

What strikes do. At 3 strikes in 30 days your marketplace visibility is reduced ("Your visibility has been reduced due to recent cancellations or no-shows."). At 5 your profile goes to "under review" ("Your account is under review. Please contact support."). Suspension is not automatic: a Skillars admin decides. While under review you are hidden from search but families can still book you through a direct link, by design.

10. Disputes and appeals

  1. On a finished or cancelled booking in the command center, "Raise a dispute" lets you dispute it yourself, within disputes.submissionWindowDays (14). Disputes raised by families against you are handled by Skillars staff. Your dispute and the family's can be open on the same booking at once (one each); the payout stays frozen until both are settled. Each side can raise at most disputes.maxPerBookingPerRaiser (2) disputes on one booking.
  2. Dispute outcomes that affect you: a credit to the family cuts your payout in proportion to the credit. "Uphold coach (reverse refund)" pays you your full net amount. If money already sent to you has to be taken back but Stripe refuses the reversal, the amount is recorded as a debt and deducted from your next payouts.
  3. Reliability strikes, a suspension, a removed review and a dispute decision can each be appealed once, within 14 days, using the "Appeal" button ("Appeal this decision"). The admin's decision is final. A suspended coach appeals from /profile. A child-safety suspension cannot be appealed.

11. Managing your reviews

Why it matters: ratings drive bookings, and how you respond to feedback is visible to future clients.

  1. /coach/reviews ("My Reviews") lists the reviews parents and players left you.
  2. A review held for moderation is marked "This review is awaiting moderation and is not yet publicly visible."; one that was removed reads "This review was removed by moderation."
  3. Reply to a review in the "Write a response (optional)" box and click "Submit response". Your reply appears under the review on your public profile, labelled "Coach's response".
  4. Your average rating and review count appear on your marketplace card and your public profile (/coaches/:coachId).

12. Messaging clients

This is the same /messaging screen every persona uses. Conversations only exist where there is a real booking relationship, so you can't message a parent or player who has never booked you.

Conventions & Gotchas

Your profile isn't visible in the marketplace until all 5 profile-builder steps are complete. A coach who registers but abandons the wizard partway through won't appear in search results.

Your time zone matters. The zone you set in step 1 is the zone every booking slot is shown in to parents: they book against your calendar, not their own local time.

You have one active session pack offer at a time. Publishing a new one deactivates the previous one. Parents who already bought the old pack keep their credits: deactivating changes what is on sale, not what has already been sold.

Without a working Stripe account, families can't book you. New booking requests to a coach whose Stripe account isn't payment-ready are refused. The banner says "to accept bookings", but the "Accept" button itself is not blocked: requests that arrived before your account lost its Stripe status can still be accepted and delivered, and their payouts sit on hold until onboarding completes.

If Skillars suspends you, your profile leaves the marketplace, your pending requests are cancelled, and (unless the admin opts out) so are your upcoming sessions. Families are refunded in full and the unused sessions on your sold packs are refunded to their wallets. Sessions that are already running are left to finish.

Related Personas

Parent

Books and pays for the sessions you deliver, and is the primary source of your booking requests and revenue.

Player

The athlete you assign homework to and assess via Skills Radar — whether self-registered or added by a parent.

Admin / Staff

Reviews strike thresholds, suspends or reinstates coaches, resolves disputes and decides appeals from the admin console.
