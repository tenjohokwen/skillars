Admin / Staff

Internal Skillars staff — operations, trust & safety, and platform partnerships. This page is scoped honestly: it covers what's actually clickable in the UI today, separately from what's real backend capability with no UI yet.

Who this persona is

Admin/staff accounts run the business behind the scenes. They keep the infrastructure healthy, moderate content, resolve disputes, enforce coach reliability, manage accounts, decide appeals, sort out stuck or problem bookings, watch the money, and track GDPR requests. Almost all of this now has a working admin console. Admins sign in at the normal /login page and land on /admin/health-dashboard. The left-hand menu has two groups: Admin (Health Dashboard, Disputes, Moderation, Coach enforcement, Users, Appeals) and Operations (Video review, Booking lookup, Finance, GDPR requests, Configuration).

1. System health — /admin/health-dashboard

"System Health" shows an overall UP/DOWN status pill and a card for each backend component (database, cache, external providers and so on), with status and detail rows.

2. Moderation queue — /admin/moderation

Why it matters: every report, flag, threshold breach and payment problem in the platform lands here as one deduplicated alert. This is the admin's inbox.

  1. The "Alert queue" tab shows open alerts with counts per type ("Total open", "Payment integrity"). Use the "Alert type" filter to narrow it ("All types" by default). Columns: Type, Summary, Raised, Actions.
  2. The action button depends on the alert type:
    • "Reported message" / "Held message": Review message opens "Message under review", with "Approve and deliver" or "Block". Blocking asks for a reason, and the sender is notified.
    • "Reported conversation": Review conversation, then "Unblock conversation".
    • "Flagged review": Open review queue, which switches to the Reviews tab.
    • "Coach strike threshold" / "Orphaned subscription": Open coach, which opens that coach in /admin/coaches.
    • "Dispute raised": Open disputes.
    • "Urgent: booking under a safety hold": Open booking and Mark handled.
    • Payment-integrity alerts — "Card dispute (chargeback)", "Refund made outside the app", "Coach payout on hold", "Coach payout reversal failed", "Booking needs manual action", "Session ended without being started": Mark handled once you have dealt with it, in Stripe or by contacting the parties. The two payout alerts also get Open booking when they are about a single booking.
    • "GDPR erasure overdue" and "Unconfirmed cash-out refund" show "No action here". The cash-out alert closes by itself once Stripe confirms.
  3. The "Reviews" tab lists reviews held for moderation (Coach, Author, Rating, Review, Flags, Submitted). Use "View" to open one, then "Approve" or "Block".

3. Disputes — /admin/disputes

  1. "Disputes" lists open disputes, oldest first (Dispute, Booking, Raised by, Reason, Status, Raised). Click "View" to see the coach, session date, booking status, "Credit debited", "Stripe charged", "Session price", the details, and the "Coach cancellation history".
  2. Resolve asks for a Resolution, plus a "Credit amount" for Partial credit (optional for Coach warning) and a "Resolution note". The resolutions are: Full credit, Partial credit, No action, Coach warning, and "Uphold coach (reverse refund)". Uphold coach takes the refund back from the parent's credit wallet (never below zero) or deducts the restored pack session again, and pays the coach in full. Anything that can't be recovered is shown as "Absorbed by Skillars".
  3. Dismiss asks for a reason and closes the dispute with no money movement.
  4. Rules to know: the coach's payout is frozen while the dispute is open. A dispute on a session that is still running can only be resolved once the session has completed ("This session has not completed yet…"). Full credit is refused on a booking that has already been fully refunded.

4. Coach enforcement — /admin/coaches

  1. The list defaults to "Under review or suspended". The Status filter also offers Pending review, Suspended, Reduced visibility and Active. Click "View" to open the "Coach enforcement profile": active strikes (30 days), open alerts, strike history and cancellation history.
  2. Suspend ("Suspend coach") asks for a reason and has two checkboxes: "Notify the coach by email" and "Also cancel the coach's upcoming sessions and fully refund the families" (ticked by default). Pending requests are always cancelled. Each booking is cancelled and refunded on its own, with no coach strike and a neutral "cancelled by Skillars" notice to the family. Sessions already running are left alone. A booking that is busy (for example, a card payment in progress) is retried once. If it still can't be cancelled, it raises a "Booking needs manual action" alert. Suspension also refunds the unused sessions on every pack the coach has sold to the families' wallets.
  3. Reinstate returns the coach to the marketplace, or to onboarding if their profile is incomplete. Issue strike needs a Booking ID belonging to this coach and a Strike reason. Delete strike re-evaluates the coach's status.
  4. "Verification tier": set Basic / Trusted / Featured with an optional note and click "Save tier". The tier shows as a badge on the coach's public profile and in search.

5. Users — /admin/users

  1. Search by email or name, then "View" an account to see its role, verification, registration date, failed logins and the history of admin actions on it.
  2. Suspend signs the user out everywhere and blocks sign-in. For a coach account it also has the "Also cancel this coach's upcoming sessions…" checkbox. Reactivate reverses a suspension. Unlock login clears failed sign-in attempts but does not lift a suspension.
  3. Administrator accounts and your own account can't be changed here. Accounts erased under GDPR, and accounts under a child-safety suspension, can't be reactivated from this page.

6. Appeals — /admin/appeals

Users can appeal a reliability strike, a coach suspension, a blocked review or a dispute decision, once, within 14 days. Filter by "Appeal pending" / "Appeal rejected" / "Appeal accepted", then click "View". "Uphold" leaves the action in place. "Overturn" reverses it automatically where possible: the strike is deleted, the suspension lifted or the review published. A dispute decision is not reversed automatically; you must apply any correction by hand. Add a "Decision note (shown to the appellant)". Decisions are final.

7. Booking lookup — /admin/bookings

  1. Enter a Booking ID and click "Look up" to see the session, coach, player profile, parent user, status and "Session wrap-up recorded".
  2. Force-complete is for a booking stuck awaiting a wrap-up the coach never submitted. It moves the booking to Completed with a placeholder wrap-up (player marked as not attended) and releases the coach's payout. It can't be undone from the console.
  3. Cancel booking cancels the booking and refunds the parent in full: a pack session goes back to the pack, and a card or credit payment is credited to their Skillars balance. The coach gets no strike. Both parties are emailed that the Skillars team cancelled the session, and any open safety-hold alert for the booking is closed. It refuses a running session ("Not cancelled: the session is in progress or paused.") and a booking with a card payment in progress. Both actions ask for a note (for example, the support ticket), which is written to the audit log.

8. Finance — /admin/finance

  1. Set "From" / "To" dates (UTC, inclusive; leave both empty for the current month) and click "Apply". The tiles show Gross volume, Commission, Stripe fees (estimated), Refunds issued as credit, Credit cash-outs, and active coach and player subscriptions. There is also a warning line for cash-outs still awaiting Stripe confirmation.
  2. "Coach revenue": pick a coach by status or Coach ID and click "View revenue" to see gross earnings, commission deducted, net payout, refunds issued, sessions, amounts and sessions pending release, and reliability strikes (30 days).
  3. Release held payouts (in the coach revenue dialog, then "Release") re-checks each of that coach's held payouts. Payouts still frozen by an open dispute or safety hold stay frozen. The rest are queued for transfer, but not before their original release date. Payouts held because of a Stripe transfer mismatch are skipped. The result reads "Held payouts: x queued for transfer, y still frozen, z skipped". It is safe to repeat.

Manual refunds are not available from this console (the page says so). Subscription revenue isn't tracked yet, and commission and Stripe fees are calculated from the configured rates.

9. GDPR requests — /admin/gdpr

A read-only tracker of export and erasure requests, filterable by "Request type" and "Status" (Pending / Processing / Completed / Failed). Open requests older than 30 days are flagged "Over 30 days". Requests are made by users themselves (or by a parent for a child's profile) and are processed automatically. Failed erasures are retried daily. There is nothing to approve here.

10. Video review — /admin/videos

"Video review" shows videos needing attention. The "Show" filter offers All needing attention, Locked by moderation, Failed, and Playback blocked. To find a specific video, enter its Video ID and click "Open". On a video you can "Block playback", "Restore playback", "Reconcile with provider" or "Delete", and load its upload sessions.

11. Platform configuration — /admin/config

"Platform configuration" lists every stored setting (Setting, Value, Type, Allowed range, Last changed), with a "Search settings" box. Click "Edit" to change a value. Changes take effect at once on the server that handled the change, and on all other servers within five minutes. This is where policy numbers like strike thresholds, grace periods, request limits and the commission rate are tuned. A setting that has never been stored shows "Not set (default n)".

What's real but API-only (no admin UI yet)

Every /api/admin/** endpoint the backend exposes now has a caller in the admin console. What remains without a screen:

Monitoring alert rules

Create, list and update threshold alert rules (/v1/admin/alerts), operated directly against the API.

Email (SES) preflight

A send test for the email provider switch-over (/v1/admin/ses/preflight). It only exists when SES is the active email transport.

Manual refunds & payment fixes

No console action. Chargebacks, external refunds and payout problems are raised as alerts, but the fix happens in Stripe (following the runbook), and then the alert is marked handled.

Admin-initiated GDPR actions

Admins can only view requests. Starting an export or erasure on someone's behalf is not possible.

Conventions & Gotchas

"Mark handled" only closes the alert. For payment-integrity and safety-hold alerts, the console has no workflow behind the button. Do the real work first (in Stripe, or by contacting the parent or coach), then mark the alert handled. "Booking needs manual action" and "Session ended without being started" have no "Open booking" button: copy the booking ID from the summary into Booking lookup.

Strike thresholds don't suspend anyone automatically. 3 strikes in 30 days reduces the coach's visibility. 5 strikes moves the coach to Pending review and raises a "Coach strike threshold" alert. Suspension is always an admin decision.

A child-safety hold is different from a suspension. When a coach's upload matches a known-abuse hash, the coach is suspended automatically, their not-yet-started bookings are cancelled with full refunds, and their payouts are frozen. Bookings that can't be cancelled raise "Urgent: booking under a safety hold" alerts. The hold can't be appealed. An admin can still reinstate the coach from Coach enforcement.

There is no multi-tenancy in Skillars. Every coach, player and parent shares the same platform. The old B2B "tenant" API-partner feature and its admin screens have been removed entirely, frontend included.

Related Personas

Coach

Strikes, suspensions, verification tiers, disputes and held payouts all act on coach accounts from this console.

Parent

GDPR requests, disputes and content reports most often originate from a parent's concerns about their child's data, safety or money.
