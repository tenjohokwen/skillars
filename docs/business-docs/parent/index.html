Parent

The account holder and legal guardian who manages one or more minor players — discovering coaches, booking and paying for sessions, and overseeing what their child does on the platform.

Who this persona is

A Parent is Skillars' primary paying customer for anyone under 18. Their child (the Player) has no login of their own — the Parent account browses, books, pays, and even reviews video content on the child's behalf. This makes Parent the busiest and most commercially important persona in the product: nearly every payment flow and most booking flows in Skillars are Parent-initiated. Parents can also book coaches on behalf of adult players if they choose to.

1. Registering as a parent

Why it matters: this is the account that legally consents to a minor's participation and holds payment responsibility — Skillars' consent flow here is deliberately heavier than the Player/Coach registration forms.

  1. Go to /parent-register. Fields: First name, Last name, Email, Phone, Password.
  2. Below the form are three separate policy boxes — Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, and Parental Consent — each in its own scrollable text box. Each corresponding checkbox stays disabled until you've scrolled that specific box to the bottom. All three must be checked before "Create Account" is enabled.
  3. On submit you land on /parent/email-pending (same resend-with-cooldown pattern as the player flow).

2. Verifying and adding your child

  1. Email link → /parent/verify-email (auto-verifies from the URL token) → /parent/verify-phone (6-digit SMS code, same auto-advance/auto-submit UX as player verification).
  2. You land on /parent/create-player: Name, Date of birth, Position (Goalkeeper/Defender/Midfielder/Forward).
  3. As soon as a date of birth is entered, an age-tier preview appears live (U10, 10–12, 13–17, or Adult) — this tier drives messaging permissions and content rules elsewhere in the product.
  4. If the computed age is under 18, a fourth scroll-gated consent box appears specifically for that child ("I am the legal parent/guardian and consent to..."), stamped with a policy version — you must scroll and check it before "Create" is enabled.
  5. Submitting takes you to /parent/dashboard.

You can add more than one child by returning to /parent/create-player again later — or from the dashboard's "Add another player" card.

2b. Your dashboard

Why it matters: this is now the parent's actual hub. Earlier versions of this documentation described it as a placeholder stub; that is no longer true.

  1. /parent/dashboard opens on "Welcome to Skillars" and a Your Players section — one card per child showing their name, age-tier label, and position — plus an "Add another player" card that routes to /parent/create-player.
  2. Below that, a Quick Links grid of four tiles, each showing live data rather than just a link:
    • Upcoming Sessions → /parent/bookings, showing a count of bookings in CONFIRMED/UPCOMING state.
    • Browse Coaches → /marketplace.
    • Credit Wallet → /parent/credit-wallet, showing your current balance.
    • Pending Approvals → /parent/approvals, showing a count awaiting review, or "All caught up".
  3. A tile whose data fails to load degrades to "Unavailable" rather than breaking the page.

3. Finding a coach and booking a session

Why it matters: this is the core transaction in the whole business — a paid coaching session.

  1. Go to /marketplace, enter a city, optionally filter by district/age group/language, and open a coach's profile at /coaches/:coachId.
  2. If you don't have any session credits with that coach yet, the call-to-action button reads "Buy sessions" and takes you to /parent/coaches/:coachId/purchase-sessions. You'll see the coach's single active session pack — a session count, total price, and per-session price — as one selectable card. Select it and press "Confirm purchase". If the coach hasn't published a pack you get "This coach has no session pack available for purchase" and cannot proceed at all.
  3. A saved payment card is required. With no card on file the confirm button stays disabled behind an "Add a payment card to continue" banner; the "Add card" action opens a Payment Method dialog (Stripe Elements) inline, and the purchase becomes available as soon as the card saves. The same card-on-file requirement applies to player subscriptions and appears on the credit wallet page.
  4. Once you hold credits with that coach, the button instead reads "Book session" and takes you to /parent/coaches/:coachId/request-booking: pick an open time slot from the coach's published availability, optionally add a note, and submit. A "batch mode" toggle lets you select several slots at once and submit them together as a batch of requests instead of one at a time.
  5. On a single booking request you can choose which pack to draw from, or pick "Per session" to pay for that one session outside a pack. Batch requests never carry a pack — they settle against your credit wallet/card instead.
  6. Track everything booked so far at /parent/bookings — each entry shows the coach, which child it's for, the time (with a toggle between your local time and the session's own time zone — useful for touring/travel coaches), and how many credits you have left with that coach. Pending reschedule requests from the coach also surface here.
  7. An empty /parent/bookings list shows a "Find a coach" button straight back to the marketplace.

4. Paying — wallet, packs, subscriptions, receipts

  1. /parent/credit-wallet: shows your cash balance and lets you cash out unused funds back out (amount field, capped at your current balance).
  2. /parent/credit-statement: a transaction-level ledger of credit activity.
  3. /parent/players/:playerId/packs: a dashboard of session packs you've purchased for that child, including a pause option. A pack can be paused once in its lifetime; pausing pushes its expiry out by the pause length, and if any of your existing bookings fall inside the pause window you are shown them and must confirm their cancellation before the pause applies. Full rules on Money & Commercial Model.
  4. /parent/player/:playerId/subscription: manage a recurring subscription plan/tier for that child.
  5. /parent/bookings/:bookingId/receipt: a formatted payment receipt for a specific booking.

5. Keeping an eye on your child

  1. /parent/players/:playerId/sessions: your child's upcoming schedule, with a direct link through to their Locker Room (homework hub) so you can see what they've been assigned.
  2. /parent/players/:playerId/development: the same Skills Radar and skill-exposure view the player themselves sees, from the parent's vantage point — plus a narrative summary paragraph parents get that players don't.
  3. /parent/approvals: this is specifically about video content, not booking approval despite the generic name — it's the parental-approval queue for videos your child has uploaded (drill clips, homework submissions). Each pending item shows the player, video type, and date, with Approve/Reject buttons. This is the "minor safety gate" that sits between a child uploading a video and it becoming visible/usable.

6. Reviewing a coach

Why it matters: your feedback as a parent and payer helps other parents make informed choices and helps coaches improve their service.

  1. After you've booked and experienced a coach, visit their profile again at /coaches/:coachId.
  2. Scroll to the "Write Review" section (visible only if you're logged in as a parent and haven't already reviewed this coach).
  3. Click "Write Review" to open a dialog where you can rate the coach 1–5 stars and write up to 1000 characters of feedback.
  4. Once submitted, your review goes into moderation — it may initially show a "Pending moderation" status. Once approved, it appears on the coach's profile where all parents and players can see it.
  5. You can edit your existing review by clicking "Edit Review" next to it; coaches can also respond to your review with their own message.

7. Messaging a coach

Same /messaging screen as every other persona — conversation list plus thread. As the guardian of a minor, your exact role in a given conversation (primary participant vs. silent observer, and whether your name or your child's is shown) depends on your child's age tier, computed automatically from their date of birth.

Conventions & Gotchas

You cannot book a coach who hasn't published a session pack. Booking requires credits, credits come only from that coach's pack, and a coach who skipped the pack step in their profile builder has none to sell. The coach still appears in marketplace search, so this looks like a broken booking button rather than missing coach setup. It is the first thing to check when a parent reports being unable to book.

Booking slot times are shown in the coach's time zone, not yours. On the booking-request screen every slot is rendered in the coach's canonical zone with the zone abbreviation shown, deliberately — you are booking against their calendar. This differs from /parent/bookings, which offers a toggle between your local time and the session's own zone. Don't assume a time you see on one screen is in the same zone as the other.

A card must be on file before any purchase. Session-pack purchase and player subscriptions both gate their confirm button on a saved card. This is a change from earlier builds where payment details were collected at the point of charge.

Parent registration has four separate scroll-gated consent checkboxes across two pages (ToS, Privacy, Parental Consent at registration; a second child-specific consent when the added player is a minor) — all deliberately un-checkable until the corresponding policy text has been scrolled to the end.

"/parent/approvals" means video approvals, not booking approvals — a naming trap worth knowing before you go looking for it.
