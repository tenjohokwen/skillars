Parent

The account holder and legal guardian who manages one or more minor players — discovering coaches, booking and paying for sessions, and overseeing what their child does on the platform.

Who this persona is

A Parent is Skillars' primary paying customer for anyone under 18. Their child (the Player) has no login of their own — the Parent account browses, books, pays, confirms sessions and watches the child's videos on the child's behalf. This makes Parent the busiest and most commercially important persona in the product: most payment and booking flows are Parent-initiated. A Parent can also add and book for a child who is 18+, although some oversight features (for example messaging the coach on that child's behalf) switch off for adult children.

1. Registering as a parent

Why it matters: this is the account that legally consents to a minor's participation and holds payment responsibility — Skillars' consent flow here is deliberately heavier than the Player/Coach registration forms.

  1. Go to /parent-register ("Create Parent Account"). Fields: First Name, Last Name, Email, Phone Number, Password. Contact details typed into a name field are flagged ("Contact details will be removed on save").
  2. Below the form are three separate policy boxes — Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, and Parent Consent — each in its own scrollable text box. Each checkbox ("I accept the Terms of Service", "I accept the Privacy Policy", "I accept the Parent Consent Policy and confirm I am the legal guardian") stays disabled until you've scrolled that specific box to the bottom. All three must be checked before "Create Account" is enabled.
  3. On submit you land on /parent/email-pending (same "Resend email"-with-cooldown pattern as the player flow).

2. Verifying and adding your child

  1. The email link opens /parent/verify-email, which verifies the token from the URL automatically.
  2. What happens next depends on the platform setting security.registration.phone-otp-required. It is seeded false, so by default you are sent straight to /login to sign in. When it is switched on you go to /parent/verify-phone first (6-digit code, auto-advance/auto-submit; the code is sent by email because no SMS provider is integrated) and from there to /parent/create-player.
  3. After signing in you land on /parent/dashboard; use the "Add another player" card to reach /parent/create-player ("Add Player Profile"): Player name, Date of birth, Position (Goalkeeper/Defender/Midfielder/Forward).
  4. As soon as a date of birth is entered, an "Age tier" preview appears (U10, 10–12, 13–17, or 18+) — this tier drives messaging permissions and content rules elsewhere in the product.
  5. If the child is under 18, a fourth scroll-gated "Parent Consent" box appears for that child, stamped with a policy version. You must scroll it and tick "I consent on behalf of this minor player" before "Create Profile" is enabled.
  6. Submitting takes you back to /parent/dashboard. Repeat for each additional child.

2b. Your dashboard and child hub

  1. /parent/dashboard opens on "Welcome to Skillars" and a Your Players section — one card per child (name, age tier, position) with shortcut chips Development, Session packs and Locker room — plus the "Add another player" card.
  2. A Quick Links grid of three tiles shows live data: Upcoming Sessions ("{n} upcoming" — bookings in CONFIRMED/UPCOMING) → /parent/bookings; Browse Coaches → /marketplace; Credit Wallet (your balance) → /parent/credit-wallet. A tile whose data fails to load shows "Unavailable".
  3. Clicking a child's card opens that child's Player hub at /parent/players/:playerId/sessions: buttons for Development, Session packs, Subscription, Locker room and Videos, the child's "Upcoming sessions" list, and a Privacy & data panel (see section 6).
  4. The left drawer offers Dashboard, Bookings, Credit wallet, Credit Statement, Messages and a "My players" list; a child switcher in the header sets which child the coach-profile buttons act for.

3. Finding a coach and requesting a session

Why it matters: this is the core transaction in the whole business — a paid coaching session.

  1. Go to /marketplace, enter a city, optionally filter by district/age group/language, and open a coach's profile at /coaches/:coachId.
  2. The profile's main button is "Book a session" whenever you hold credits with that coach or the coach has a per-session price — you don't need a pack to book. It opens /parent/coaches/:coachId/request-booking ("Book a session with {coach}") for the child selected in the header. Under "Select a time slot", pick a slot from the coach's availability (slots you already requested show "You have already requested this slot"). Under "Use a session pack", choose a pack ("{remaining} of {total} sessions left") or "Per session". Without credits it defaults to "Per session" with the note "No session pack needed: you pay this coach's per-session price when they accept your request (wallet credit first, then your saved card)." Add "Notes for coach (optional)" and press "Request session".
  3. Optional: buy a session pack. If the coach also sells a pack, an outlined "Buy sessions" button sits under "Book a session" (it is the main button only for a coach with a pack and no per-session price). It opens /parent/coaches/:coachId/purchase-sessions ("Buy sessions with {coach}") for the selected child. You see the coach's single active pack as one card ("{n}-session bundle", total and per-session price, "Valid for {days} days from purchase"). Select it and press "Confirm purchase". A suspended coach's pack is refused before any charge.
  4. A saved payment card is required to buy a pack. With no card on file the button stays disabled behind "Add a payment card to continue"; "Add card" opens the "Payment Method" dialog (Stripe Elements) and the purchase unlocks as soon as the card saves.
  5. The "Multi-select" toggle lets you pick several slots (up to booking.batch.maxSize, seeded at 5), then "Review requests" → "Confirm requests". The review shows price per session, total, and how much comes from your credit versus your card. Batch requests never use pack credits, and payment is taken when the coach accepts.
  6. Request limits: at most 10 requests may await one coach at once (booking.request.maxPendingPerCoach; coach proposals don't count, and a batch counts in full), and a session can be requested at most 120 days ahead (booking.request.maxHorizonDays) — the same limit applies to a new time you propose with "Request Change". Only the slots shown can be requested: a start time between them is refused ("That start time is not one of the coach’s bookable slots…") and the slot list reloads. A pack can back only as many open requests as it has sessions left — sessions are taken from the pack when the coach accepts, so pending requests hold them ("Every remaining session in this pack is already held by a pending booking."). The price is locked when you request. If the coach has no session price set, the page warns "This coach can only be booked with a session pack. Buy one to request a booking." and only a pack booking is possible.
  7. The coach answers in their inbox. Nothing is charged while a request is "Awaiting coach response". On acceptance the session is paid from the pack, or from wallet credit and then your card. A failed payment declines the booking. A request still unanswered at its start time is declined automatically.

4. Managing bookings at /parent/bookings

"My bookings" lists every booking with coach, child ("Player: …"), time (toggle between your time and the session's time zone) and a status chip. The actions offered depend on the status:

  • Coach proposals (e.g. the coach's "repeat next week"): chip "Awaiting your confirmation" and the note "Your coach proposed this session. Nothing is charged until you confirm." Confirm opens "Confirm this session?" and takes payment then (pack if the coach used one, otherwise credit and card). Decline drops it. The coach cannot accept their own proposal.
  • Your own pending request: "Withdraw request" — allowed until the coach answers.
  • Confirmed/Upcoming: "Request Change" (dialog "Propose a new time" → "Send request"; the end time follows automatically because the length can't change), "Cancel" (dialog "Cancel this session?" → "Cancel session"), "Request for the coach" (a 500-character prep note), and "Message coach". If the coach proposes a new time, "Reschedule requested" appears with Accept / Decline.
  • After the start time: "Coach didn't show" appears from start + 30 minutes (before that: "You can report a coach no-show from {time}."). Once the coach has started the session (In progress / Paused) it can no longer be cancelled — use "Raise a dispute".
  • Awaiting confirmation (the coach marked the session complete without running it live, ended it in under half its booked length, or it was closed automatically): you get an email and an inbox notice straight away, and the row offers "Confirm session" and "Raise a dispute". If the coach never pressed Start, "Coach didn't show" stays available too (from start + 30 minutes) — a coach can mark a session complete at its start time, before you could report anything. If you confirm before the coach has written the recap, the coach adds it afterwards and it still counts towards your child's development record. If you don't respond, it auto-confirms after booking.quick_complete_timeout_hours (seeded at 24) once the coach has written the recap; a dispute you raised stays open and holds the coach's payout until an admin decides it.
  • Finished or cancelled: "Raise a dispute" (reason: Coach no-show, Session quality, Safety concern, Unauthorised charge, Other) within 14 days of the booking reaching that status; afterwards "View dispute". Your family (you and your child's own login together) can raise at most 2 disputes per booking (disputes.maxPerBookingPerRaiser) — past that, appeal the decision instead. A dispute the coach raised on the same booking does not stop you raising your own. "Receipt" appears on any booking with a captured payment.

5. What happens to your money

SituationOutcome
You cancel more than 24h before the originally booked startRefunded: a pack session goes back to the pack; a card/credit payment becomes cashable wallet credit. Rescheduling never restarts the 24h clock.
Same, but the pack has already expiredRefunded as booking credit only — usable for bookings, never cashed out.
You cancel inside 24h, or the coach reports your child as a no-show (allowed from start + 15 min)No refund; the coach is paid as normal. You get an email and an inbox notice saying the session is forfeited, with a 14-day window to dispute it.
Coach hasn't started by start + 30 minCancelling is refunded, or report "Coach didn't show" — either way the coach gets a reliability strike. Also open when the coach marked the session complete without ever starting it.
Coach cancelsRefunded (pack session restored, or wallet credit). A coach cancelling a session they never started, at or after its start time, gets a reliability strike whatever reason they give.
Coach marks the session complete, or runs it for under half its booked lengthYou are asked straight away to confirm — or dispute. The coach is not paid while it waits for you.
Coach starts, then abandons the sessionTwo hours after the scheduled end it is closed automatically and comes to you to confirm — or dispute.
Dispute decided for the coach ("uphold coach")A refund you already received is taken back: from your wallet down to €0 at most, or the pack session is re-deducted.

6. Paying — wallet, packs, subscriptions, receipts

  1. /parent/credit-wallet ("Credit Wallet"): "Current Balance"; if part of it is booking credit, it is split into "Can be cashed out: €…" and "Booking credit only: €…". It also holds your "Payment Method" (card on file / "Replace card") and the "Cash Out" form ("Amount to cash out (EUR)"). Cash-outs are refunded to your most recent card payment minus a processing fee. They are paused while any dispute on your payments or bookings is open.
  2. /parent/credit-statement ("Credit Statement"): a ledger with Start/End Date filters and a "Receipt" link per booking line.
  3. /parent/players/:playerId/packs ("Your session packs"): each pack's sessions remaining, expiry and status badge — Active, Paused ("Paused until {date}"), Frozen, Withdrawn (with the reason), Expired or Exhausted. A pause that hasn't started yet keeps the pack Active with a "Pause scheduled" note. A pack is valid for 90 days (1 session), 180 (2–5), 365 (6–10) or 548 (11+) from purchase, and you get one warning email 14 days before it expires. "Pause pack" (once per pack, offered only on an Active pack): pick a "Pause start date" (tomorrow at the earliest) and a "Pause duration (days)" (1–90). The expiry moves out by the same length. Only bookings drawn on this pack inside the pause conflict: they are listed and cancelled when you press "Cancel sessions & apply pause". A paid session within 24h of its start blocks the pause, and so does one whose payment is still being processed (try again a few minutes later). No pack session can take place inside the pause: requesting, rescheduling into it, confirming a coach's "repeat" for that week, or the coach accepting a request inside it is refused ("This session pack is paused…"). While a card dispute on the pack's payment is open the pack is Frozen (no booking, spending or pausing). If the dispute is lost, the payment is refunded outside Skillars, or the coach is suspended, the remaining sessions are Withdrawn — for a suspended coach they are refunded to your wallet as cashable credit. A withdrawn pack can't be paused or extended.
  4. /parent/player/:playerId/subscription ("Player Subscription"): choose or change the child's plan (shown by its raw code: ATHLETE free, SEMI_PRO, PRO — the paid tiers bill yearly) or "Cancel Plan". Saving a new card on the wallet page retries a failed subscription payment or resumes a paused subscription with that card.
  5. /parent/bookings/:bookingId/receipt: a printable "Session Receipt".

7. Keeping an eye on your child

  1. Player hub (/parent/players/:playerId/sessions): the child's upcoming sessions with remaining credits per coach, and links to everything below. The Locker room button opens the child's homework at /player/locker-room/:playerId.
  2. /parent/players/:playerId/development ("Development Portal"): Skills Radar (with "Compare to baseline"), a narrative summary only parents get, Session Credits, coach contributions, Skill Exposure with a neglected-skills alert, Skill Trends, performance reports and the Player Timeline.
  3. /parent/players/:playerId/videos: videos on the child's own account (you can watch or delete them) and, under "From the coach", session videos a coach recorded of your child (watch only). Children under 18 cannot upload; there is no approval queue.
  4. Privacy & data on the hub: "Request export" (profile, bookings, development record) and "Delete child's data" (type DELETE to confirm; your own account is unaffected). Your own export/deletion is on /profile.

8. Reviewing a coach

  1. Open the coach's profile at /coaches/:coachId. "Write a review" appears only once you have a completed session with that coach at least 7 days old (reviews.minSessionAgeDays) and no open dispute with them; otherwise the reason is shown instead.
  2. The dialog takes a 1–5 star "Rating" and an optional "Comment" (max 1000 characters); press "Submit review".
  3. Under "Your review" you see "Your review is awaiting moderation and isn't publicly visible yet." until it's approved. "Edit your review" needs a new session since you last wrote it and is limited by a 30-day cooldown. Coaches can post a "Coach's response".
  4. You can "Flag this review" on other people's reviews (Fake review, Offensive content, Conflict of interest, Other).

9. Messaging a coach

Use the "Message coach" button on a booking, or on the coach's profile ("Message coach about {child}"). It appears only once a booking links that coach to the selected child. Conversations live at /messaging ("Messages"). For a minor, your role in the conversation (primary participant or silent observer, and whose name is shown) follows the child's age tier. For an 18+ child the button is hidden — the adult manages their own conversations.

Conventions & Gotchas

A pack is optional. "Book a session" works without one: you pay the coach's per-session price (wallet credit first, then your saved card) when they accept. A coach whose profile shows a disabled "Not bookable yet" has set neither a price nor a pack. When a parent reports being unable to book, check that first, then whether the coach's Stripe setup is complete (requests to a coach without it are refused).

Booking slot times are shown in the coach's time zone, not yours. On the request page every slot is in the coach's zone, deliberately. /parent/bookings, by contrast, offers a toggle between your local time and the session's zone. Don't assume a time you see on one screen is in the same zone as the other.

The 24-hour clock runs from the original start time. Moving a session later with "Request Change" does not create a fresh refund window, and cancelling between the start time and start + 30 minutes is still a forfeit.

A card must be on file before buying a pack or a subscription, and nothing is charged for a request until the coach accepts it — or, for a coach proposal, until you press Confirm.

Parent registration has four separate scroll-gated consent checkboxes across two pages (ToS, Privacy, Parent Consent at registration; a child-specific consent when the added player is a minor) — all deliberately un-checkable until the policy text has been scrolled to the end.
