Skillars — Business Documentation

What Skillars is, who it's for, how it makes money, and exactly how to execute each persona's real flows in the live product.

What Skillars is

Skillars is a marketplace that connects private football/soccer coaches with players for one-on-one and small-group training, built around a Parent account that supervises and pays for a minor child's participation. Coaches list themselves publicly, publish availability and prices, and get booked and paid through the platform; players (adult self-registered, or added as a child profile by a parent) receive assigned homework drills, get assessed on a multi-skill "Skills Radar," and can upload homework video for review (adults only — minors never upload).

The four personas

Player

The athlete being coached — self-registers if 18+ and can then book and pay like a parent; otherwise added by a parent with no login of their own.

Parent

The account that books, pays, and consents on behalf of a minor child — Skillars' primary paying customer.

Coach

The paid supplier — publishes a profile, sets availability and prices, delivers sessions, plans training content, gets paid through Stripe.

Admin / Staff

Internal operations — a working admin console for moderation, disputes, appeals, coach enforcement, users, bookings, finance, GDPR, videos, config and system health.

How the business makes money

  • Commission on every paid session — Skillars keeps an 8% commission (platform.commission.rate) of the session price and pays the coach the rest after the session is completed and a 48-hour hold has passed. Stripe card fees are paid by the platform, not deducted from the coach.
  • Per-session payment — the default way in: every coach sets a per-session price, and a family can book a single session without buying anything first. It is paid wallet balance first and then the saved card, charged when the coach accepts (or when a parent confirms a coach's proposal).
  • Session packs (optional) — a coach can also publish one active pack (a session count at a discounted total price). The family pays up front; the coach is paid each session's share as it completes. A pack is valid for 90 / 180 / 365 / 548 days from purchase for 1 / 2–5 / 6–10 / 11+ sessions (admin-tunable, extendable once by the coach), and sessions left unused when it expires are kept by the platform.
  • Subscriptions — coaches sit on tiers (Scout / Instructor / Academy; Scout is feature-gated, e.g. no session builder and no video storage), and a family can buy a player subscription (ATHLETE / SEMI_PRO / PRO) for video and development features. Upgrades unlock only once the payment succeeds.
  • Credit wallet — refunds land as wallet credit. Cashable credit can be cashed out by the payer (a parent, or a self-registered adult player) for a 2.5% + €0.25 fee; "booking credit only" (e.g. a refund from an expired pack) can only be spent on bookings.
  • Forfeits still pay the coach — on a player no-show or a parent cancelling inside 24 hours, the family gets no refund and the coach receives their normal net amount; the platform keeps only its commission.

An adult self-registered Player books and pays through the same booking-request and (optional) pack-purchase pages as a Parent, and cashes out their own wallet credit the same way. Phone verification is off by default (security.registration.phone-otp-required), so every persona goes from the email link straight to login.

What makes a coach unbookable — check this first when someone reports "nobody can book me."

  • No working Stripe account. Booking requests to a coach whose Stripe Connect account isn't payment-ready are refused. The coach sees a "Complete your Stripe setup" banner in their Command Center.
  • Neither a per-session price nor a session pack. The coach profile then shows a disabled "Not bookable yet" button with the hint "This coach hasn't set a session price or a session pack yet, so they can't be booked right now." The profile builder requires a per-session price, so a coach who finished it never lands here.

Not having a session pack is not a blocker: the profile's main button is "Book a session" whenever the family holds pack credits with the coach or the coach has a per-session price, with an outlined "Buy sessions" button underneath when the coach also sells a pack. "Buy sessions" is the main button only for a coach with a pack and no per-session price. Signed-out visitors see "Sign up to book".

Known gaps (read this before promising a flow)

These are real product behaviours in the current build, not bugs in this documentation. Details are on the linked pages.

  • Admin alerts are not pushed (except video moderation alerts, which are emailed) — staff have to check /admin/moderation. There is also no admin screen for manual refunds or alert rules. Admin / Staff
  • Teen (13–17) independent messaging can't happen — minors have no login, so every conversation about a minor goes through the parent's account. Trust & Safety

By design, not a gap: a coach in "pending review" (5 strikes) is hidden from search but can still be booked through a direct link. The spec says the coach keeps operating while flagged for admin attention, with no automatic suspension without an admin's decision. Reduced-visibility and pending-review status also never recover on their own: an admin reinstates the coach on /admin/coaches. Trust & Safety

Previously listed here and now resolved: the admin console (moderation, disputes, GDPR, coach enforcement and more) exists; the broken /admin/tenants screens were removed; review submission, player self-booking and the parent dashboard hub all work. Since then: a new family can book a coach per session without buying a pack; families are emailed when a session is forfeited; self-registered adult players can cash out their wallet credit; pack badges show Paused, Frozen and Withdrawn correctly; and pack validity follows the per-size tiers instead of a flat 60 days.
