    Payment

    Moves money between parents, coaches, and the platform: card charges on the platform account, completion-gated Stripe Connect transfers to coaches, the credit wallet, subscriptions, session packs, finance reporting, and the reliability-strike system that keeps coach supply honest.

    Business Overview

    Skillars is a two-sided marketplace: parents (or self-registered adult players) pay for coaching sessions, and coaches need to get paid reliably without the platform touching card numbers or bank details. The payment module is where money actually moves. It owns five related concerns:

    Charges & coach payouts

    Since skillars-deferred-106 the platform uses separate charges and transfers, not destination charges. A booking's card portion is charged to the platform Stripe account at acceptance. The coach's net share is only transferred to their Connect account after the session completes and a hold window (default 48h) has passed. That leaves time for a dispute to cancel the payout before any money reaches the coach.

    Credit wallet

    Refunds for cancellations never go back to a card. They land in an append-only parent_credit_ledger as euro credit. A payer spends that credit first on later bookings. A parent (not a player) can also "cash out" credit back to their card, minus a Stripe processing fee.

    Subscriptions & session packs

    Coaches subscribe to tiers (SCOUT/INSTRUCTOR/ACADEMY) that unlock marketplace features. Players subscribe to tiers (ATHLETE/SEMI_PRO/PRO) that unlock video and skill-radar features. A payer can also prepay a coach-defined session pack, which is bulk sessions at a fixed per-session price.

    Reliability strikes

    Coaches who cancel late or don't show up get strikes. The strike count over the last 30 days drives two marketplace consequences: first reduced search visibility, then suspension pending admin review.

    Finance reporting

    This module owns the coach revenue dashboard and transaction list, coach and payer receipts, the payer's credit statement with running balance, and the admin finance overview with per-coach revenue drill-down. All of these are read-only and come from RevenueReportingService.

    Key business rules found in the code

    Commission is locked at capture and applied at payout. The PaymentIntent no longer carries application_fee_amount or transfer_data. When the payment is persisted, BookingPaymentPersistenceService stamps the live platform.commission.rate into booking_payments.commission_rate. On completion, CoachPayoutOutboxSupport.enqueuePayout computes commission = gross × rate (HALF_UP to the cent) and net = gross − commission, and never re-reads the live rate (a NULL stamp falls back to the live rate). Every funding source is paid through the same row, held payment.payout.hold_hours from completion:

    • Card and/or wallet credit: gross = stripe_charged + credit_debited at booking_payments.commission_rate. A mixed booking is one row and one transfer, so the card part and the credit part can't be paid twice.
    • Session pack: gross = this session's share of session_pack_purchases.amount_charged: amount_charged / session_count rounded down to the cent, with the last (session_count-th) session taking the remainder. The rate is session_pack_purchases.commission_rate, stamped at pack purchase (V186). The running payout_sessions_allocated / payout_gross_allocated is updated under the pack row lock, so the sum never exceeds what the pack was charged. Unused sessions of an expired pack never complete, so the platform keeps that money.

    If nothing was charged, coach_payouts gets a row written directly as CANCELLED with last_error = NOTHING_CHARGED (PACK_FULLY_ALLOCATED / PACK_AMOUNT_UNKNOWN for a pack that can't cover the session). Payouts made before V186 for pack or credit sessions were never paid and are not paid retroactively.

    Cash-out fees. The fee on a cash-out is amount × payment.stripe.feeRate + payment.stripe.feeFixed. Both values are platform_config keys read through ConfigService, with seeded values 0.025 and 0.25 (there are no application.yaml equivalents). The fee comes out of the requested amount: the wallet is debited exactly the amount asked for (CASH_OUT_DEBIT = net + STRIPE_FEE_DEBIT = fee) and the card gets the net. The cash-out is refused with payment.cashOutTooSmall if the net would be ≤ 0, and with payment.cashOutExceedsRefundable if the net is more than the payer's last PaymentIntent has left to refund. The same two keys produce the estimated Stripe-fee line on the admin finance overview.

    Cancellation refund matrix. A parent cancellation is refund-eligible if the booking had been paid (CONFIRMED/UPCOMING) and the cancellation is either more than 24h before the start (measured from the earlier of the original and current start, so a reschedule cannot reopen the window) or the session is still unstarted booking.lateCancel.graceMinutes (default 30) after its start (Deferred-64, narrowed by fix/booking-cancel-policy-loopholes). A coach's excused cancellations beyond coach.excusedCancellations.maxPerWindow (3) per windowDays (90) strike as COACH_CANCELLATION_EXCUSED_LIMIT; the count-and-strike runs under a per-coach transaction-scoped advisory lock (CoachCancellationHistoryRepository.acquireCoachCancellationLock), so two concurrent excused cancels cannot both count before the other commits. It is an advisory lock and not the coach row because ReliabilityStrikeService.issue (REQUIRES_NEW) locks that row on a second connection. That is BookingService's refundEligible flag on the event. A booking paid by card or credit is refunded as a BOOKING_REFUND wallet credit for the full session price. A pack-funded booking gets its session restored instead. Refunds only ever give back money actually taken: a card booking is charged, and a pack booking's session deducted, only at settle, so nothing is owed before CONFIRMED. A coach cancellation refunds only a captured booking (CONFIRMED/UPCOMING/PAUSED; the event's paymentCaptured, decided under the booking row lock) — an ACCEPTED booking the coach drops mints no wallet credit and restores no pack session (C6; it used to do both). Coach no-shows (only from UPCOMING) always refund. An admin cancellation (coach suspension, child-safety hold, or one booking cancelled from the admin bookings page, BookingCancelledByAdminEvent) refunds only a booking whose payment was captured (CONFIRMED/UPCOMING; the event's refundEligible), never strikes the coach and writes no cancellation history. Every refund is what was actually paid (BookingService.resolveAmountPaid: the booking_payments credit + card, or the pack's per-session price), never the coach's current price, which is wrong once the coach re-prices. If the pack had already expired when a coach cancelled, no-showed, or was suspended, the refund goes to the wallet instead of the pack. Coach cancellations and no-shows also issue a reliability strike, unless the cancel reason is MUTUAL_AGREEMENT, HEALTH_MEDICAL, FAMILY_EMERGENCY or WEATHER. No-shows always get a strike. A player no-show forfeits the fee, with no refund and no strike.

    Reliability thresholds. Strikes are counted over the trailing 30 days. Reaching reliability.strike.visibilityThreshold (default 3) sets the coach's status to REDUCED. Reaching reliability.strike.suspensionThreshold (default 5) sets it to PENDING_REVIEW. Each call applies at most one of these, and suspension is checked first. The defaults live in ReliabilityStrikeConfig, which is shared with admin's AdminCoachEnforcementService. A coach who is already SUSPENDED or DEACTIVATED still gets the strike row recorded, but their status is never changed (skillars-deferred-123).

    Session packs expire after 60 days and can be extended once. The coach can extend a pack by 30 days, but only once (extendedAt must be null) and only within the 14 days before expiry (SessionPackPaymentService.EXTENSION_WINDOW, which CoachSoldPackQueryService also uses to compute its extendable flag). An extension that loses a race gets 409 payment.packAlreadyExtended. One outside the window gets 422 payment.packExtensionNotEligible. A daily job sends one warning email 14 days before expiry if sessions remain, and stamps expiryWarnedAt so it isn't sent again. Packs that have already been extended are not warned again.

    A pack can be paused exactly once in its lifetime. pausePack refuses if pausedUntil is already set. The pause length must be between 1 and pack.pause.maxDays days. The pause must start today or later in the coach's timezone, and before the pack expires. Pausing pushes expiresAt out by the pause length. Any of the payer's bookings with that coach inside the pause window are handled by a deliberate two-step confirm flow, not an error. The first call returns them without applying the pause (PauseConflictResponse.applied = false). The client then re-submits with confirmedCancellationIds, which must cover every conflict. If it covers only some of them, the server returns the conflict list again (skillars-deferred-136).

    Unused sessions are forfeited at expiry, not refunded. SessionPackForfeitureScheduler stamps expiredNotifiedAt and fires SessionPackExpiredEvent carrying the remaining count. No credit is written back.

    Subscription tier changes are asymmetric. Upgrades take effect immediately with Stripe proration. Downgrades are written to *_subscription_changes and applied at the end of the billing period by a nightly job. A newer downgrade voids the older one by setting voided_at. Player SEMI_PRO/PRO tiers are billed yearly only: the tier catalogue returns no monthly price for them. A PAST_DUE subscription is downgraded once subscription.pastDue.gracePeriodDays has passed (coach → SCOUT, status CANCELLED).

    Key services

    ServiceOrchestrates
    StripeClientThin, mockable wrapper around static Stripe SDK calls: PaymentIntent, Refund, SetupIntent, Customer, PaymentMethod, Subscription, Transfer, TransferReversal, and the paginated subscription list. It is the only class that calls the Stripe Java SDK, apart from StripeOnboardingService's OAuth calls and the webhook signature check.
    StripePaymentGatewayImplements PaymentGateway. chargeAndCapture charges the platform account and sets transfer_group = reference id. Before charging it fails fast if the coach's Connect account isn't ready. transferToCoach (Stripe key transfer-{bookingId}), reverseTransfer (key reversal-{transferId}), refund (caller-supplied key: refund-{paymentIntentId} for pack-purchase compensation, cashout-{cashOutId} for a cash-out), getRefundableAmount (latest charge's captured minus refunded), SetupIntent, and customer creation.
    PaymentLifecycleServiceAFTER_COMMIT listener for BookingAcceptedEvent/BatchBookingAcceptedEvent. Decides pack vs credit vs card, reserves a capture before every Stripe call, charges, and hands off to persistence. Batch bookings settle in a separate REQUIRES_NEW transaction per booking.
    BookingPaymentPersistenceServicereserveCapture writes a CAPTURE_PENDING row in its own REQUIRES_NEW transaction under a booking-row lock. persistPaymentSuccess atomically writes the BOOKING_DEDUCTION, the CAPTURED row and stamped commission rate, the booking transition, and BookingConfirmedEvent. Also handles failure, decline and batch confirm. Its REQUIRES_NEW paths are routed to a dedicated connection pool (see gotchas).
    CoachPayoutEnqueueListener / CoachPayoutOutboxSupportA BEFORE_COMMIT listener for BookingCompletedEvent. Writes the coach_payouts row (PENDING_RELEASE, release_after = now + payment.payout.hold_hours, bounded 0–336) and a COACH_PAYOUT_TRANSFER outbox message with notBefore = release_after, atomically with the completion write. enqueueReversal is called by admin's DisputeService.
    CoachPayoutTransferHandler / CoachPayoutReversalHandlerOutbox handlers. The transfer handler takes a locked read of the payout row, acts only on PENDING_RELEASE after release_after, and moves the row to RELEASED. A non-retryable Stripe error moves it to HOLD and increments the coach.payout.held metric. The reversal handler acts only on RELEASED and moves the row to REVERSED, or REVERSAL_FAILED plus coach.payout.reversal_failed.
    StripeTransferErrorClassifierSorts StripeExceptions from transfers and reversals into retryable (outbox backoff) and non-retryable (HOLD / REVERSAL_FAILED).
    CancellationRefundServiceAFTER_COMMIT, REQUIRES_NEW listeners for cancellations and no-shows. Restores pack sessions, records cancellation history, and issues strikes. A PessimisticLockingFailureException from strike issuance is caught here so it can't affect anything else. This class no longer writes refunds; see the next row.
    RefundEnqueueListener / RefundOutboxSupport / CreditWalletRefundOutboxHandlerA BEFORE_COMMIT listener that enqueues a CREDIT_WALLET_REFUND outbox message in the same transaction that writes the booking as CANCELLED. The handler writes the BOOKING_REFUND ledger row and does nothing if one already exists for the booking. uq_pcl_booking_refund is the backstop if two run concurrently.
    CreditWalletServiceBalance (sumByParentId, empty → 0) and writeLedgerEntry, which rejects a zero amount.
    CashOutServiceNot transactional. Fee/net → refundable-amount check on the payer's lastPaymentIntentId → CreditWalletService.debitForCashOut (locked balance check, CASH_OUT_DEBIT net + STRIPE_FEE_DEBIT fee, both tagged with a fresh cash-out id) → Stripe refund of the net under key cashout-{cashOutId}. If Stripe rejects the refund (4xx), it writes compensating CASH_OUT_REVERSAL rows with the same id in one transaction. If the outcome is unknown (network failure, 5xx, 409 idempotency conflict), it retries under the same key and then keeps the debit and marks the reconciliation record UNKNOWN (payment.refundOutcomeUnknown, admin alert) rather than restore credit for a refund that may have gone through. See the cash-out gotcha below.
    CashOutReconciliationServiceOwns the cash_out_reconciliations transitions (each a short transaction under the row lock, only from an open status, so credit is restored at most once) and the re-check job that retries open refunds under their original key.
    CoachPayoutFreezeServiceThe safety-hold payout freeze: on AccountSuspensionRequestedEvent for a coach, activates the freeze and flips PENDING_RELEASE payouts to FROZEN; on CoachReinstatedEvent (BEFORE_COMMIT), lifts it and re-enqueues each frozen payout not before its release_after.
    BatchPaymentQuoteService / CreditCardSplitPre-submit cost preview for a multi-session request. CreditCardSplit.of(balance, amount) is the one rule ("use as much credit as covers it, charge the rest"), shared with batch settlement so the quote can't drift from the actual charge.
    PackSessionServiceThe pack credit engine. Deducts or restores a session under a pessimistic row lock (publishing SessionPackExhaustedEvent at zero), answers active-pack lookups (hasActivePack, hasActivePackForAnyOf, getActivePackId, findActivePackId), and runs the pause flow (pausePack → pausePackTransactional, publishing PackPausedEvent).
    SessionPackPaymentServicePack purchase: Stripe charge, then persist, then a compensating refund if persisting fails, then a stripe_refund_failures record if the refund also fails. Also extend (locked), tier create/deactivate, saved-card summary, and computeStatus (EXHAUSTED → PAUSED → EXPIRED → ACTIVE).
    CoachSoldPackQueryServicePaged list of the packs a coach has sold, with batched tier/player/buyer name lookups. Computes status, expiringSoon and extendable using the same window and precedence as extend and the buyer's view (#312).
    RefundReconciliationServiceWrites or increments a stripe_refund_failures row in its own REQUIRES_NEW transaction.
    PaymentPendingSweeperRecovers bookings stuck in PAYMENT_PENDING, deciding by the payment row, not the funding type. See the "stuck payments" flow below.
    SubscriptionServiceTier catalogue, subscribe/upgrade/downgrade/cancel for coaches and players, the webhook-driven status sync, and the bodies of four nightly jobs: applyPendingChanges, checkPastDueGracePeriod, reconcileMarketplaceTiers and reconcileStripeSubscriptions. The last one only alerts and never auto-heals.
    StripeWebhookServiceVerifies signatures, enforces idempotency, and routes the account, subscription, invoice, dispute and refund events listed below. Raises CoachSubscriptionOrphanedEvent when Stripe bills a coach who has no local subscription row.
    StripeOnboardingServiceBuilds the Connect OAuth URL (state = coach id) and exchanges the code (OAuth.token) for the account id. It upserts the account as PENDING.
    ReliabilityStrikeServiceissue runs in REQUIRES_NEW. It inserts the strike, locks the coach row, counts strikes over 30 days, and changes the coach to REDUCED or PENDING_REVIEW. Also handles acknowledge and paged history.
    RevenueReportingServiceRead-only. Coach summary and transactions come from the coach_payouts ledger (RELEASED, plus PENDING_RELEASE as a separate line). Coach receipts require a RELEASED payout and payer receipts a CAPTURED payment; anything else returns 404. Also the credit statement with running balance, the admin overview, and per-coach admin revenue.

    Key Flows

    Coach Stripe Connect onboarding

    sequenceDiagram
  participant Coach as Coach (browser)
  participant API as StripeOnboardingResource
  participant Svc as StripeOnboardingService
  participant Stripe as Stripe OAuth
  participant DB as coach_stripe_accounts

  Coach->>API: GET /coaches/me/stripe/onboard
  API->>Svc: generateOnboardingUrl(coachId)
  Svc->>Stripe: OAuth.authorizeUrl(state=coachId)
  Svc-->>Coach: StripeOnboardingUrlResponse
  Coach->>Stripe: redirected, approves Connect access
  Stripe-->>API: GET /coaches/me/stripe/callback?code, state
  API->>API: validate state == coachId (CSRF guard)
  API->>Svc: handleOAuthCallback(coachId, code)
  Svc->>Stripe: OAuth.token(code)
  Stripe-->>Svc: stripe_user_id
  Svc->>DB: upsert CoachStripeAccount(status=PENDING)
  API-->>Coach: 302 to frontend coach payment-settings page

    Onboarding uses Stripe's OAuth Connect flow. Stripe redirects back to a backend callback (not the frontend) with a one-time code and the original state. Before exchanging the code, the controller checks that state equals the authenticated coach's id. This prevents CSRF account-linking. The final redirect goes through FrontendLinkBuilder.resolve(callbackSuccessUrl), producing an absolute frontend URL with the hash router's #/; on failure, ?stripe_error= is appended. The account starts as PENDING and only becomes COMPLETE later, through the account.updated webhook. Charges and transfers both require COMPLETE with charges_enabled.

    Stripe webhook: idempotent, signature-verified event processing

    sequenceDiagram
  participant Stripe
  participant WH as StripeWebhookResource
  participant Svc as StripeWebhookService
  participant Idem as stripe_webhook_events
  participant Sub as SubscriptionService

  Stripe->>WH: POST /webhooks/stripe (Stripe-Signature)
  WH->>Svc: processWebhook(payload, sigHeader)
  Svc->>Svc: Webhook.constructEvent (outside TX)
  Svc->>Idem: INSERT eventId ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING (in TX)
  alt duplicate (0 rows)
    Idem-->>Svc: no-op
  else account.updated
    Svc->>Svc: set flags, status COMPLETE or RESTRICTED
    Svc-->>Svc: publish CoachStripeOnboardingCompleteEvent after commit
  else customer.subscription.updated / deleted
    Svc->>Sub: handleSubscriptionWebhook(...)
    Svc->>Svc: live status with no local coach row: CoachSubscriptionOrphanedEvent
  else invoice.payment_failed
    Svc->>Svc: orphan check first, then
    Svc->>Sub: mark PAST_DUE, stamp pastDueSince (first failure only)
  else invoice.paid
    Svc->>Sub: PAST_DUE/UNPAID/INCOMPLETE back to ACTIVE, clear pastDueSince
  else charge.dispute.created / closed
    Svc->>Svc: freeze (or, if lost, cut / reverse pro rata) the coach payouts the charge funded, admin alert
    Svc->>Svc: take back wallet credit the charge funded (restored if won), cash-out refused while open
  else charge.refunded
    Svc->>Svc: refund not issued by the app: admin alert only
  end
  WH-->>Stripe: 200 (500 on failure so Stripe retries)

    Signature verification happens outside any transaction. The idempotency insert and the event handling then run together in handleEventAtomically, which is @Transactional and called through a self-injected proxy. A deserialization failure re-throws, so the idempotency row rolls back and Stripe retries the event rather than it being lost. The orphan check skips coaches whose coach_subscriptions row was touched within a short grace window, so a subscribe that is still being set up doesn't trigger a false alert. Subscription and invoice events carry the Stripe event's created time, and an event older than the last one applied to that subscription row (last_stripe_event_at) is ignored, so late out-of-order deliveries cannot overwrite newer state. Every other event type (payment_intent.*, transfer.*, ...) is ignored (logged at DEBUG).

    Booking settlement: reserve, then charge the platform account

    sequenceDiagram
  participant Booking as BookingService
  participant Life as PaymentLifecycleService
  participant Persist as BookingPaymentPersistenceService
  participant Wallet as CreditWalletService
  participant Gateway as StripePaymentGateway

  Booking-->>Life: BookingAcceptedEvent (AFTER_COMMIT)
  Life->>Life: terminal row exists? ignore. CAPTURE_PENDING row? abort (CAPTURE_UNCONFIRMED)
  alt pack-funded
    Life->>Life: PackSessionService.deductSession (row lock)
    Life->>Persist: persistPaymentSuccess(0, 0)
  else credit / card
    Life->>Wallet: getBalance(payerId)
    Life->>Life: credit = min(balance, price), card = price - credit
    opt card > 0
      Life->>Persist: reserveCapture (REQUIRES_NEW, payment pool): CAPTURE_PENDING row
      Life->>Gateway: chargeAndCapture (platform account, transfer_group = bookingId)
    end
    Life->>Persist: persistPaymentSuccess(credit, card, intentId)
  end
  Persist->>Persist: BOOKING_DEDUCTION + CAPTURED row + commission_rate
  Persist->>Booking: transition(PAYMENT_CAPTURED)
  Persist-->>Booking: BookingConfirmedEvent

    Credit is applied first, and only the remainder is charged to the card. A booking fully covered by credit or a pack never reserves. That keeps the UAT.3 invariant: for a booking in PAYMENT_PENDING, no booking_payments row proves no Stripe call was attempted. The Stripe call is made outside any local transaction. If it throws, persistPaymentFailure marks the booking failed and no credit is debited. While a CAPTURE_PENDING row exists, BookingService refuses a parent cancel with 409 booking.paymentInProgress. For batch bookings, every card-funded booking is reserved first, then priced over the reserved set, using CreditCardSplit against the wallet. There is one batch BOOKING_DEDUCTION and one Stripe charge for the batch. Each booking is then confirmed in its own REQUIRES_NEW transaction. If the charge fails, the credit is restored with BOOKING_DEDUCTION_REVERSAL and every reserved booking is declined.

    Coach payout lifecycle (completion-gated transfer)

    stateDiagram-v2
  [*] --> PENDING_RELEASE: BookingCompletedEvent, card plus credit or pack share above 0
  [*] --> CANCELLED: nothing charged (NOTHING_CHARGED, PACK_FULLY_ALLOCATED)
  [*] --> FROZEN: coach already under a safety hold
  PENDING_RELEASE --> FROZEN: CSAM safety hold (freeze or release-time check)
  FROZEN --> PENDING_RELEASE: admin reinstates coach, one message re-enqueued
  FROZEN --> CANCELLED: dispute upheld
  PENDING_RELEASE --> RELEASED: transfer ok after release_after
  PENDING_RELEASE --> HOLD: non-retryable transfer error
  PENDING_RELEASE --> CANCELLED: dispute upheld inside hold
  HOLD --> CANCELLED: dispute upheld
  HOLD --> PENDING_RELEASE: operator, coach reconnected
  RELEASED --> REVERSED: dispute upheld, reversal ok
  RELEASED --> REVERSAL_FAILED: non-retryable reversal error
  HOLD --> FAILED_PERMANENT: operator SQL only
  REVERSAL_FAILED --> FAILED_PERMANENT: operator SQL only

    The row is written in the completion transaction. The outbox message is delayed to release_after. If it fires early, the handler throws so the outbox retries later. DisputeService.reconcileCoachPayout (admin) takes the same PESSIMISTIC_WRITE lock as the transfer handler (findByIdForUpdate). A dispute and a transfer happening at once therefore can't both win: the handler re-reads CANCELLED and does nothing. After release, a dispute reverses the coach's proportional share of the parent's refund. Nothing moves a row to FAILED_PERMANENT automatically. HOLD and REVERSAL_FAILED are resolved by hand per docs/deployment/runbook.md Scenario 5 (watch the coach.payout.held and coach.payout.reversal_failed metrics).

    Safety-hold freeze. Only a CSAM safety hold freezes payouts (normal suspensions don't). Three gates read coach_payout_freezes: the hold itself flips pending rows to FROZEN, a completion during the hold writes the row FROZEN, and the transfer handler checks the freeze under the payout row lock right before calling Stripe. The last one is the gate the money actually passes, and it catches a row the freeze UPDATE could not see yet. A transfer that fired before the hold landed is not clawed back automatically. A retryable transfer failure is counted in coach.payout.transfer_retry{reason}. A credit-funded payout has no charge behind it and draws on the platform's available balance, so BALANCE_INSUFFICIENT is retried until charges settle.

    Stuck PAYMENT_PENDING recovery

    PaymentPendingSweeper.sweepOne takes the booking row lock (the same lock reserveCapture takes), re-checks PAYMENT_PENDING, and then decides based on the payment row:

    Payment rowAction
    NoneNo money moved, whatever the funding type. Writes CHARGE_FAILED, transitions PAYMENT_FAILED, publishes BookingDeclinedEvent, and frees the coach's slot.
    CAPTURE_PENDING, younger than booking.payment_pending.capture_pending_max_hours (default 72, bounded 6–720) or without reserved_atERROR log + booking.payment_pending.unrecoverable{reason=CAPTURE_UNCONFIRMED}. The booking is left alone for an operator to reconcile against Stripe (runbook Scenario 4).
    CAPTURE_PENDING, older than thatMoved to CAPTURE_ABANDONED (keeping stripe_charged), the booking is released, BookingPaymentUnresolvedEvent is published, and {reason=CAPTURE_TIMEOUT} is counted. An operator still reconciles the Stripe side.
    Any terminal status{reason=PAYMENT_ROW_PRESENT}: a data-integrity error, not a stuck booking.

    Cancellation refund (durable)

    sequenceDiagram

    Conventions & Gotchas

    Never skip webhook signature verification or idempotency. /api/payment/webhooks/stripe has to be permitAll (Stripe sends no session cookies), so Webhook.constructEvent(...) is the only protection against forged events. Any new handler must go through StripeWebhookService.handleEventAtomically.

    Stripe SDK calls stay outside @Transactional, and the split is done with a self-proxy. Services separate "call Stripe" from "persist the result". The persist half is reached through an @Autowired @Lazy self-reference so Spring's proxy applies; a direct this.foo() call skips it. SessionPackPaymentService.purchasePack is intentionally not transactional for the same reason.

    reserveCapture deadlocks with no timeout if its caller holds the booking row. It runs in REQUIRES_NEW on a second connection and locks booking.bookings. Both callers in PaymentLifecycleService carry a "DEADLOCK CONSTRAINT" comment: their own transaction must have no lock and no uncommitted write on that row. The NOWAIT hint applies only to the locked SELECT, not to a plain UPDATE. Check this before adding a write above a reservation.

    Payment's REQUIRES_NEW writes use a dedicated Hikari pool. reserveCapture, persistPaymentFailure and declineBatchBooking call RoutingDataSourceContext.set(PAYMENT_REQUIRES_NEW_DATASOURCE_KEY) in a non-transactional wrapper and then call self.xxxTransactional(...). The routing key has to be set before the proxy opens the connection, so setting it inside the @Transactional body would be too late (skillars-deferred-137). The pool is payment-requires-new-pool in infrastructure.config.DataSourceConfig.

    Stripe idempotency keys. Charges use pi-{referenceId}-{payerId}-{1-minute bucket}. The bucket exists because pack purchases pass the tier id as referenceId, so without it a parent's second purchase of the same tier would replay the first. Transfers, reversals and refunds use deterministic keys (transfer-{bookingId}, reversal-{transferId}, refund-{paymentIntentId} for a pack purchase's compensating refund), so a re-run outbox row repeats the original operation instead of performing a second one. A key names one logical operation: a cash-out uses cashout-{cashOutId}, because a payer can cash out several times against the same PaymentIntent and a shared key would make Stripe replay the first refund instead of issuing the second.

    The credit ledger is append-only. Rows are never updated or deleted. A failed operation is undone by writing a new row (CASH_OUT_REVERSAL, BOOKING_DEDUCTION_REVERSAL). The ledger type and sign CHECKs reject anything else. Type names are constants in CreditLedgerEntryType; JPQL in ParentCreditLedgerRepository concatenates them rather than repeating literals.

    Cash-out is serialized per payer on the stripe_customers row. The ledger is append-only, so there is no balance row to lock. CreditWalletService.debitForCashOut takes StripeCustomerRepository.findByIdForUpdate (NOWAIT, inside PessimisticLockRetryer.withBoundedRetry), re-reads the balance and writes both debit rows in one transaction, so a second concurrent cash-out sees the first one's debits and fails with payment.insufficientCredit. The Stripe refund runs after that transaction commits, never inside it. CashOutConcurrencyIT pins this. Booking settlement takes the same lock: when the payer has credit, PaymentLifecycleService re-reads the balance through CreditWalletService.lockAndGetBalance (a blocking lock, not NOWAIT: settlement runs in an AFTER_COMMIT listener with no retry, so a second settlement for the same payer waits instead of being stranded in PAYMENT_PENDING) and holds the lock until its transaction writes the BOOKING_DEDUCTION, so a settlement and a cash-out (or two settlements) can't spend the same credit (SettlementCashOutRaceIT). The trade-off: on a mixed credit and card settlement the lock is held across the Stripe charge, so a cash-out at that moment may get a retryable 409. Every cash-out writes a cash_out_reconciliations row in the debit's transaction, so a crash between the debit and the refund, or an unknown refund outcome, leaves a durable record. An unknown outcome raises a CASH_OUT_UNRESOLVED admin alert (after the status commits), the re-check job retries it under the same key and the alert resolves itself when the cash-out settles, and the admin finance page shows the count and amount of unknown or stale-pending cash-outs (CashOutReconciliationIT).

    A payer with no ledger history has no balance row, so treat that as zero. Both ledgerRepository.sumByParentId and the payment.parent_credit_balance view (GROUP BY parent_id) return zero rows for that payer; the view's COALESCE only covers a null SUM. CreditWalletService.getBalance maps empty to BigDecimal.ZERO. Any new reader must do the same. CreditWalletZeroHistoryBalanceIT pins this behaviour.

    A coach can't be charged for, or paid out, until Stripe onboarding is COMPLETE. StripePaymentGateway.resolveCoachStripeAccountId throws payment.coachStripeNotConfigured (422) unless onboarding_status = COMPLETE and charges_enabled is true. It runs at charge time as a fail-fast check and again at transfer time, where the error is non-retryable and moves the payout to HOLD. This is the most common "why did payment fail" support case.

    Pack deduction, restore, extend and pause lock the purchase row. They read with findByIdForUpdate inside PessimisticLockRetryer.withBoundedRetry. Without the lock, two devices booking against the same pack could both decrement the same remainingSessions. pausePackTransactional holds the lock for the whole method and re-runs the conflict query just before writing (D8). If a new booking appeared, it throws so the cancellations it already made roll back.

    Pack- and credit-funded sessions are paid out per completed session. Pack purchases are charged to the platform account at purchase time, and each completed pack session pays the coach its share (see the business rule above). CoachPayoutPackCreditIT pins the exact allocation under concurrent completions. A dispute on a pack session treats the pack share as the session price, so the parent is credited it and the payout is cancelled or reversed like a card payout. Transfers do not set source_transaction (card payouts never did either); they are plain transfers from the platform balance with transfer_group = booking id.

    Admin finance figures. getAdminOverview calculates:

    • gross volume as SUM(stripe_charged + credit_debited) over CAPTURED booking rows (booking value before refunds, which are a separate line; wallet credit spent on a booking counts too, so this is not cash charged to cards) plus session_pack_purchases.amount_charged (stamped at purchase, V181; legacy rows were backfilled from the tier's current price);
    • commission as the sum of coach_payouts.commission_amount over payouts RELEASED in the period (dated by released_at): what was actually deducted from coach transfers, for card, wallet-credit and pack sessions alike, at the rate stamped on each row. Pending, held and frozen payouts haven't earned it yet. Cancelled or reversed ones (a dispute refunded the parent) earned none. A partially reversed payout drops its whole commission, which errs on the conservative side;
    • Stripe fees as an estimate from config over card money only (card-charged booking money and pack purchases), with one fixed fee per distinct charge;
    • cash-outs as CASH_OUT_DEBIT rows not reversed by a CASH_OUT_REVERSAL with the same reference_id. Rows from before the fix hold the gross requested amount and have no reference id.

    There is no subscription-revenue figure, because no subscription price amounts are stored locally. The coach summary's netPayout is the ledger net that was actually transferred. It carries no Stripe-fee line, because under separate charges the platform pays the processing fee, not the coach.

    String status constants, not enums. BookingPaymentStatus, CoachPayoutStatus and CreditLedgerEntryType are final classes of String constants that match DB CHECK constraints. Adding a value needs a migration that widens the CHECK. For BookingPaymentStatus, also revisit isTerminal: anything that isn't CAPTURE_PENDING counts as "settled".

    Local development: Stripe webhooks without a public URL

    Stripe can only deliver webhooks to a publicly reachable URL, and localhost isn't one. For local work, use the Stripe CLI to forward test-mode events to your machine:

    stripe listen --forward-to localhost:9990/api/payment/webhooks/stripe

    The first run prints a whsec_... signing secret. Set it as APP_PAYMENT_STRIPE_WEBHOOK_SECRET; the CLI signs forwarded events with its own key, which differs from any Dashboard secret. Set APP_PAYMENT_STRIPE_API_KEY to a sk_test_... key (a live key refuses to boot outside the prod profile). Set APP_PAYMENT_STRIPE_PUBLISHABLE_KEY to the matching pk_test_... key, which the frontend reads from GET /api/payment/stripe/config. The handled events are account.updated, customer.subscription.updated/deleted, invoice.payment_failed, invoice.paid, charge.dispute.created/closed and charge.refunded, so trigger those, e.g. stripe trigger customer.subscription.updated or stripe trigger charge.dispute.created. A deployed webhook endpoint must be subscribed to all of them.

    Use the standard secret key (sk_test_...), not a Restricted key (rk_test_...). Stripe only accepts OAuth.token (Connect onboarding) with the account's standard secret key.

    This only works on your machine. A deployed environment such as UAT (see uat-deployment.md) needs a real webhook endpoint registered in the Stripe Dashboard.
