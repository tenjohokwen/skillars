    Payment

    Moves money between parents, coaches, and the platform: card charges on the platform account, completion-gated Stripe Connect transfers to coaches, the credit wallet, subscriptions, session packs, finance reporting, and the reliability-strike system that keeps coach supply honest.

    Business Overview

    Skillars is a two-sided marketplace: parents (or self-registered adult players) pay for coaching sessions, and coaches need to get paid reliably without the platform touching card numbers or bank details. The payment module is where money actually moves. It owns five related concerns:

    Charges & coach payouts

    Since skillars-deferred-106 the platform uses separate charges and transfers, not destination charges. A booking's card portion is charged to the platform Stripe account at acceptance. The coach's net share is only transferred to their Connect account after the session completes and a hold window (default 48h) has passed. That leaves time for a dispute to cancel the payout before any money reaches the coach.

    Credit wallet

    Refunds for cancellations never go back to a card. They land in an append-only parent_credit_ledger as euro credit. A payer spends that credit first on later bookings. A parent (not a player) can also "cash out" cashable credit back to a card, minus a Stripe processing fee. Some credit is spend-only (bookable, never cashed out, V208).

    Subscriptions & session packs

    Coaches subscribe to tiers (SCOUT/INSTRUCTOR/ACADEMY) that unlock marketplace features. Players subscribe to tiers (ATHLETE/SEMI_PRO/PRO) that unlock video and skill-radar features. A payer can also prepay a coach-defined session pack, which is bulk sessions at a fixed per-session price.

    Reliability strikes

    Coaches who cancel late or don't show up get strikes. The strike count over the last 30 days drives two marketplace consequences: first reduced search visibility, then suspension pending admin review.

    Finance reporting

    This module owns the coach revenue dashboard and transaction list, coach and payer receipts, the payer's credit statement with running balance, and the admin finance overview with per-coach revenue drill-down. All of these are read-only and come from RevenueReportingService.

    Key business rules found in the code

    Commission is locked at capture and applied at payout. The PaymentIntent no longer carries application_fee_amount or transfer_data. When the payment is persisted, BookingPaymentPersistenceService stamps the live platform.commission.rate into booking_payments.commission_rate. On completion, CoachPayoutOutboxSupport.enqueuePayout computes commission = gross × rate (HALF_UP to the cent) and net = gross − commission, and never re-reads the live rate (a NULL stamp falls back to the live rate). Every funding source is paid through the same row, held payment.payout.hold_hours (48) from completion, or payment.payout.newCoach.holdHours (168) while the coach has fewer than payment.payout.newCoach.releasedCount (5) payouts released — status RELEASED with a kept net of at least payment.payout.newCoach.minReleasedNet (20, whole units of the platform currency), so a few tiny sessions don't count (fix/audit3-wallet-cashout-integrity: the 48h hold is shorter than the card-chargeback window, so a brand-new coach paid out at once left every chargeback to the platform; never shorter than the normal hold; both keys are ConfigBounds code defaults, no seed row). The coach revenue page shows the rule (RevenueSummaryDto.payoutHold) and each pending payout's releaseAfter:

    • Card and/or wallet credit: gross = stripe_charged + credit_debited at booking_payments.commission_rate. A mixed booking is one row and one transfer, so the card part and the credit part can't be paid twice.
    • Session pack: gross = this session's share of session_pack_purchases.amount_charged: amount_charged / session_count rounded down to the cent, with the last (session_count-th) settled session taking the remainder. A session refunded to the payer as wallet credit (a booking on an expired pack cancelled by the parent more than 24h ahead or by the coach, a coach no-show, admin-cancelled or paused) settles on the same ledger (refund_sessions_issued / refund_gross_issued, one pack_session_refunds marker per booking, V200, PackRefundAllocationService), so payouts plus refunds of a fully settled pack equal amount_charged to the cent. An UPHOLD_COACH clawback un-settles the refund before the coach is paid, and payInFull never recomputes a pack payout whose share was already allocated (only a PACK_AMOUNT_UNKNOWN / PACK_FULLY_ALLOCATED skip is), so a session is never settled twice. The rate is session_pack_purchases.commission_rate, stamped at pack purchase (V186). The running payout_sessions_allocated / payout_gross_allocated is updated under the pack row lock, so the sum never exceeds what the pack was charged. Unused sessions of an expired pack never complete, so the platform keeps that money.

    If nothing was charged, coach_payouts gets a row written directly as CANCELLED with last_error = NOTHING_CHARGED (PACK_FULLY_ALLOCATED / PACK_AMOUNT_UNKNOWN for a pack that can't cover the session). Payouts made before V186 for pack or credit sessions were never paid and are not paid retroactively.

    Forfeits pay the coach (feat/payment-forfeit-payouts-and-clawback, owner decision). CoachPayoutEnqueueListener writes the same payout row, at the same locked rate and with the same hold, BEFORE_COMMIT in the forfeiting transaction for a PlayerNoShowEvent and for a BookingCancelledByParentEvent whose isForfeit() is true (captured money the parent doesn't get back, a pack session stays consumed). Nothing captured, nothing paid. An open dispute still freezes these payouts.

    A refund costs the coach the same share whatever the timing. CoachPayoutAdjustmentService.applyRefund (in-app dispute credit or lost card dispute) cuts a pending payout's net by refund / price of its original net (original_net_amount, V205) and releases it, or enqueues a reversal of that share of a released one. Only a cut of the whole net cancels. A partial reversal keeps the row RELEASED and adds to reversed_amount (V193); reporting counts net − reversed. An admin UPHOLD_COACH pays the coach the full original net: a cut pending payout is restored, a released or reversed one gets a COACH_PAYOUT_TOPUP transfer for the shortfall, and the booking's coach debt is waived.

    Cash-out fees. The fee on a cash-out is amount × payment.stripe.feeRate + payment.stripe.feeFixed. Both values are platform_config keys read through ConfigService, with seeded values 0.025 and 0.25 (there are no application.yaml equivalents). The fee comes out of the requested amount: the wallet is debited exactly the amount asked for (CASH_OUT_DEBIT = net + STRIPE_FEE_DEBIT = fee) and the card gets the net. The cash-out is refused with payment.cashOutTooSmall if the net would be ≤ 0, payment.insufficientCredit if the wallet total is short, payment.cashOutExceedsCashable if the cashable pool is short (spend-only credit never leaves as cash), and payment.cashOutBlockedByDispute while a card dispute on one of the payer's charges or an in-app dispute on one of their bookings is open. The refund goes onto the charges that funded the payer's cashable refund credit (ParentCreditLedgerRepository.findCashOutRefundTargets, newest first, up to 10), so the funding charges' disputable amount drops. fix/audit3-wallet-cashout-integrity: the cash-out is split into legs across them, each capped at its charge's refundable amount (it used to pick one charge that could take the whole amount, else put all of it on the latest charge, leaving the funding charges fully disputable — a chargeback after the cash-out paid the parent twice). Only the remainder no funding charge can take goes onto the payer's last PaymentIntent, refused with payment.cashOutExceedsRefundable if that charge has too little left. A funding charge Stripe cannot read for now (network, 5xx, 429) refuses the whole cash-out (payment.cashOutTargetUnavailable, nothing debited) rather than routing past it; one it will never return (404 / invalid request / permission: payment.refundTargetInvalid from StripePaymentGateway.getRefundableAmount) is skipped, with a BOOKING_ACTION_REQUIRED alert on that PaymentIntent (CASH_OUT_TARGET_INVALID). Each leg is a cash-out of its own downstream — its own id, debit rows, cash_out_reconciliations row and key cashout-{legId} — all debited in one transaction after one locked balance check, the fee split pro rata. The legs of one request share a note leg i/n req <8-char id> on their ledger descriptions, in cash_out_reconciliations.last_error (as a prefix) and in the CASH_OUT_UNRESOLVED alert reason, so ops can tie them together. A leg Stripe rejects restores only that leg's credit; if another leg reached the card the request fails with payment.cashOutPartiallyRefunded (the wallet page re-reads the balance after any failed cash-out). No schema change. The fee keys also produce the estimated Stripe-fee line on the admin finance overview.

    Spend-only credit (V208, owner decision 2026-10-29). A parent's refund-eligible (>24h) cancellation of a booking drawn on an expired pack is refunded at the locked price as spend-only credit. Each ledger row records its own spend_only_amount (same sign, never larger than amount, chk_ledger_spend_only). The spend-only balance is SUM(spend_only_amount) clamped to [0, total]; cashable is total − spend-only (CreditWalletService.getWalletBalance, returned by GET /credits/balance as cashableBalance / spendOnlyBalance). BOOKING_DEDUCTION consumes the spend-only pool first. Reversals and restores put money back into the pool it came from. fix/audit3-wallet-cashout-integrity: so do refunds. A BOOKING_REFUND of a card / credit booking and an admin DISPUTE_CREDIT give back to the spend-only pool the share the booking's own BOOKING_DEDUCTION (reference = booking id, single and batch settles alike) took from it, net of what a BOOKING_DEDUCTION_REVERSAL, earlier refunds / dispute credits and an UPHOLD_COACH BOOKING_REFUND_REVERSAL already returned (CreditWalletService.bookingSpendOnlyOutstanding, computed by the outbox handler at write time under the payer's wallet lock); the rest stays cashable. Lock order: the handler and DisputeCreditService lock the booking's open / lost card-dispute rows (ChargebackCreditReversalService.lockDisputesForCredit) before the wallet lock, the order ChargeDisputeWebhookService uses (dispute row, then wallet), so the two can't deadlock. Without it, spend-only credit spent on a booking came back cashable on cancellation and could be cashed out. Also spend-only (owner decision): a pause cancel of an expired-pack booking, a coach cancel of one with an excused reason (the strike logic's list, CancellationRefundService.isExcusedReason: MUTUAL_AGREEMENT, WEATHER, HEALTH_MEDICAL, FAMILY_EMERGENCY), and a parent-side restore (parent cancel, pause, excused coach cancel) that reaches a pack which expired before the restore ran. Unexcused coach cancels, admin / no-show expired-pack refunds and suspended-coach pack refunds stay cashable.

    Cancellation refund matrix. A parent cancellation is refund-eligible if the booking had been paid (CONFIRMED/UPCOMING) and the cancellation is either more than 24h before the start (measured from the earlier of the original and current start, so a reschedule cannot reopen the window) or the session is still unstarted booking.lateCancel.graceMinutes (default 30) after its start (Deferred-64, narrowed by fix/booking-cancel-policy-loopholes). A coach's excused cancellations beyond coach.excusedCancellations.maxPerWindow (3) per windowDays (90) strike as COACH_CANCELLATION_EXCUSED_LIMIT. A coach cancel at or after the start of a never-started booking (ACCEPTED / CONFIRMED / UPCOMING; a PAUSED session keeps the excused rules) (BookingCancelledByCoachEvent.cancelledAfterStart) always strikes as COACH_NO_SHOW, whatever the reason, and its history row is flagged after_start (V213) so it is not counted toward that cap. It is also never excused for the refund: CancellationRefundService.isExcusedCancellation(event) is the one classification both the strike and RefundEnqueueListener's expired-pack refund read, so that refund stays cashable; a parent's refund-eligible late cancel after the coach never started (BookingCancelledByParentEvent.coachNoShow) strikes COACH_NO_SHOW too (onBookingCancelledByParent, after commit). The count-and-strike runs under a per-coach transaction-scoped advisory lock (CoachCancellationHistoryRepository.acquireCoachCancellationLock), so two concurrent excused cancels cannot both count before the other commits. It is an advisory lock and not the coach row because ReliabilityStrikeService.issue (REQUIRES_NEW) locks that row on a second connection. That is BookingService's refundEligible flag on the event. A booking paid by card or credit is refunded as a BOOKING_REFUND wallet credit for the full session price. A pack-funded booking gets its session restored instead, unless the pack has expired: then the session's value is credited to the wallet as spend-only credit, settled on the pack ledger (PackRefundAllocationService), as for the coach, admin, no-show and pause paths (whose refunds stay cashable). Pack restores run off the outbox (PACK_SESSION_RESTORE, once per booking): a restore that lands on a pack which expired meanwhile or was revoked for a coach suspension is refunded to the wallet instead (spend-only for a parent cancel, pack pause or COACH_EXCUSED_CANCELLATION restore; cashable for an unexcused coach cancel, admin or no-show), and one that lands on a pack revoked for a chargeback or external refund gives nothing back. Refunds only ever give back money actually taken: a card booking is charged, and a pack booking's session deducted, only at settle, so nothing is owed before CONFIRMED. A coach cancellation refunds only a captured booking (CONFIRMED/UPCOMING/PAUSED; the event's paymentCaptured, decided under the booking row lock) — an ACCEPTED booking the coach drops mints no wallet credit and restores no pack session (C6; it used to do both). Coach no-shows (only from UPCOMING) always refund. An admin cancellation (coach suspension, child-safety hold, or one booking cancelled from the admin bookings page, BookingCancelledByAdminEvent) refunds only a booking whose payment was captured (CONFIRMED/UPCOMING; the event's refundEligible), never strikes the coach and writes no cancellation history. Every refund is what was actually paid (BookingService.resolveAmountPaid: the booking_payments credit + card, or the pack's per-session price), never the coach's current price, which is wrong once the coach re-prices. If the pack had already expired when a coach cancelled, no-showed, or was suspended, the refund goes to the wallet instead of the pack. Coach cancellations and no-shows also issue a reliability strike, unless the cancel reason is MUTUAL_AGREEMENT, HEALTH_MEDICAL, FAMILY_EMERGENCY or WEATHER. No-shows always get a strike. A player no-show forfeits the fee, with no refund and no strike, and the coach is paid. An admin cancellation that owes a refund whose amount is unknown enqueues nothing and raises a BOOKING_ACTION_REQUIRED admin alert (V208) for a manual refund.

    Reliability thresholds. Strikes are counted over the trailing 30 days. Reaching reliability.strike.visibilityThreshold (default 3) sets the coach's status to REDUCED. Reaching reliability.strike.suspensionThreshold (default 5) sets it to PENDING_REVIEW. Each call applies at most one of these, and suspension is checked first. The defaults live in ReliabilityStrikeConfig, which is shared with admin's AdminCoachEnforcementService. A coach who is already SUSPENDED or DEACTIVATED still gets the strike row recorded, but their status is never changed (skillars-deferred-123).

    Pack validity is set by pack size at purchase. createPurchase stamps expiresAt = now + SessionPackValidity.days(sessionCount): 1 session → pack.expiry.days.tier1 (90), 2–5 → tier2 (180), 6–10 → tier3 (365), 11+ → tier4 (548). The keys are V139 seeds registered as ConfigBounds.PACK_EXPIRY_DAYS_TIER1..4 (range 14–3650, not fail-fast) and read through the BoundedKey overload of getBoundedLong, so an absent, non-numeric or out-of-range value falls back to the seed default and can never fail a purchase that was already charged. The floor is 14 because the expiry warning and the Extend window both open 14 days before expiry. A config change affects only later purchases (no backfill), and nothing checks that the tiers ascend. SessionPackTierResponse.validityDays carries the current value to the purchase page and the coach's offer card ("Valid for {days} days from purchase"). Before #369 every pack got a flat 60 days.

    Packs can be extended once. The coach can extend a pack by 30 days, but only once (extendedAt must be null) and only within the 14 days before expiry (SessionPackPaymentService.EXTENSION_WINDOW, which CoachSoldPackQueryService also uses to compute its extendable flag). An extension that loses a race gets 409 payment.packAlreadyExtended. One outside the window gets 422 payment.packExtensionNotEligible. A daily job sends one warning email 14 days before expiry if sessions remain, and stamps expiryWarnedAt so it isn't sent again. Packs that have already been extended are not warned again. A revoked pack can't be extended (payment.packRevoked, checked before any write).

    A pack can be paused exactly once in its lifetime. pausePack refuses if pausedUntil is already set, and refuses a revoked pack (payment.packRevoked) or one frozen by an open card dispute (payment.packFrozen) ahead of the generic "not active" check, so no booking is cancelled and no expiry pushed out. The pause length must be between 1 and pack.pause.maxDays days. The pause must start strictly in the future (booking.pauseStartInPast) and before the pack expires. Pausing pushes expiresAt out by the pause length and stores both ends (paused_from, V210), so the pack counts as paused only over [paused_from, paused_until) (SessionPackPurchase.isPausedAt): a pause scheduled for later leaves the pack usable until it starts. A pre-V210 pause has no paused_from and keeps the old meaning. Only bookings drawn on this pack inside the window conflict, and they are handled by a deliberate two-step confirm flow, not an error. The first call returns them without applying the pause (PauseConflictResponse.applied = false). The client then re-submits with confirmedCancellationIds, which must cover every conflict. If it covers only some of them, the server returns the conflict list again (skillars-deferred-136). A paid (CONFIRMED/UPCOMING) booking inside the 24h notice window can't be cancelled by a pause: the pause is refused with that booking flagged not cancellable, whatever the client confirmed. A cancelled captured booking gets its pack session restored (or the amount paid as wallet credit), with no coach strike.

    A paused, dispute-frozen or revoked pack can't be spent. deductSession refuses payment.packPaused inside the pause window and payment.packFrozen while a card dispute on the pack's charge is open (dispute_frozen_at, V210). A lost dispute or a full refund made outside the app revokes the remaining sessions (revoked_reason CHARGEBACK_LOST / EXTERNAL_REFUND). A coach suspension or safety hold (CoachSuspendedEvent) enqueues PACK_SUSPENSION_REFUND: every unexpired, unfrozen pack of that coach has its unused sessions refunded to the payer's wallet at the locked price (cashable, one BOOKING_REFUND with reference = the pack id, settled on the pack ledger) and is revoked as COACH_SUSPENDED. The handler is a no-op if the coach was reinstated meanwhile. purchasePack refuses a coach who can't be booked before charging (BookingService.requireBookableCoachStatus).

    Pack status (SessionPackPaymentService.computeStatus, shared by CoachSoldPackQueryService) is evaluated in the order REVOKED → EXHAUSTED → FROZEN → PAUSED → EXPIRED → ACTIVE. Revoked outranks exhausted because a revocation also zeroes the sessions. Both the buyer's SessionPackPurchaseResponse and the coach's CoachSoldPackDto carry revokedReason (null unless revoked). Neither REVOKED nor FROZEN is ACTIVE, so every client "usable pack" filter drops them. The frontend maps the status to badges in utils/packStatus.js: the family sees Active / Paused / Frozen / Withdrawn (with the reason) / Expired / Exhausted, a scheduled future pause stays Active with a "Pause scheduled" note, and Pause is offered only on an ACTIVE pack.

    Unused sessions are forfeited at expiry, not refunded. SessionPackForfeitureScheduler stamps expiredNotifiedAt and fires SessionPackExpiredEvent carrying the remaining count. No credit is written back.

    Subscription tier changes are asymmetric. Upgrades take effect immediately with Stripe proration. Downgrades are written to *_subscription_changes and applied at the end of the billing period by a nightly job. A newer downgrade voids the older one by setting voided_at. Player SEMI_PRO/PRO tiers are billed yearly only: the tier catalogue returns no monthly price for them. A PAST_DUE, UNPAID or PAUSED subscription is cancelled once subscription.pastDue.gracePeriodDays has passed: the Stripe subscription is cancelled first (a Stripe failure leaves the row for tomorrow's run), then the tier is revoked locally and the Stripe link cleared.

    Since fix/payment-subscription-entitlements a stored paid tier is granted only while the status is ACTIVE, TRIALLING or PAST_DUE (SubscriptionEntitlement; marketplace reads it through CoachSubscriptionEntitlementPort). Tier changes need an ACTIVE/TRIALLING subscription. Upgrades charge the proration immediately (always_invoice + error_if_incomplete) and are granted only once that charge succeeds. A paid downgrade moves the Stripe price now with no proration and the local tier follows at period end; a downgrade to SCOUT is a cancel-at-period-end. Subscribe writes a short-lived subscribe_attempt_key under the row lock first (V207), so a double submit gets payment.subscription.inProgress instead of a second Stripe subscription.

Fee/net → planLegs (split across the funding charges, remainder on the payer's lastPaymentIntentId) → CreditWalletService.debitForCashOut (one locked balance check; per leg CASH_OUT_DEBIT net + STRIPE_FEE_DEBIT fee tagged with the leg's own cash-out id, and its reconciliation row) → per leg, a Stripe refund of its net under key cashout-{legId}.     CoachPayoutFreezeServiceThe safety-hold payout freeze: on AccountSuspensionRequestedEvent for a coach, activates the freeze and flips PENDING_RELEASE payouts to FROZEN; on CoachReinstatedEvent (BEFORE_COMMIT), lifts it and re-enqueues each frozen payout not before its release_after.
    BatchPaymentQuoteService / CreditCardSplitPre-submit cost preview for a multi-session request. CreditCardSplit.of(balance, amount) is the one rule ("use as much credit as covers it, charge the rest"), shared with batch settlement so the quote can't drift from the actual charge.
    PackSessionServiceThe pack credit engine.     RefundReconciliationServiceWrites or increments a stripe_refund_failures row in its own REQUIRES_NEW transaction.
    PaymentPendingSweeperRecovers bookings stuck in PAYMENT_PENDING, deciding by the payment row, not the funding type. See the "stuck payments" flow below.
    SubscriptionServiceTier catalogue, subscribe/upgrade/downgrade/cancel for coaches and players, the webhook-driven status sync, and the bodies of four nightly jobs: applyPendingChanges, checkPastDueGracePeriod, reconcileMarketplaceTiers and reconcileStripeSubscriptions.     Onboarding uses Stripe's OAuth Connect flow. Stripe redirects back to a backend callback (not the frontend) with a one-time code and the original state. Before exchanging the code, the controller checks that state equals the authenticated coach's id. This prevents CSRF account-linking. The final redirect goes through FrontendLinkBuilder.resolve(callbackSuccessUrl), producing an absolute frontend URL with the hash router's #/; on failure, ?stripe_error= is appended. The account starts as PENDING and becomes COMPLETE (or RESTRICTED) through the account.updated webhook, which Stripe sends only to the Connect endpoint. As a fallback for a missed webhook, StripeOnboardingService calls StripeClient.retrieveAccount right after the OAuth callback and, at most every 30 s per coach, when the status is read while not COMPLETE. Both paths go through CoachStripeAccountStatusApplier and respect last_event_created_at: a fetch records its snapshot as 30 s before the request, so it never overwrites a newer event. An OAuth (re)connect resets the row to PENDING and stamps last_event_created_at with the connect time, so events from an earlier connection are stale. account.application.deauthorized leaves the row RESTRICTED with the local transfers_capability sentinel deauthorized: transfers go to HOLD, later account.updated snapshots are ignored, and the status response reports disconnected: true so the coach is offered a reconnect. A charge requires COMPLETE with charges_enabled. A transfer uses StripePaymentGateway.transferReadiness: ready when transfers_capability is active (or, before any event reported it, COMPLETE with payouts enabled); pending (capability pending, or a fresh reconnect still PENDING) is retried with outbox backoff; anything else goes to HOLD. When the account becomes ready, CoachStripeOnboardingCompleteEvent re-drives the coach's held payouts.

    Booking settlement: reserve, then charge the platform account

    sequenceDiagram
  participant Booking as BookingService
  participant Life as PaymentLifecycleService
  participant Persist as BookingPaymentPersistenceService
  participant Wallet as CreditWalletService
  participant Gateway as StripePaymentGateway

  Booking-->>Life: BookingAcceptedEvent (AFTER_COMMIT)
  Life->>Life: terminal row exists? ignore. CAPTURE_PENDING row? abort (CAPTURE_UNCONFIRMED)
  alt pack-funded
    Life->>Life: PackSessionService.deductSession (row lock)
    Life->>Persist: persistPaymentSuccess(0, 0)
  else credit / card
    Life->>Wallet: lockAndGetBalance(payerId) if the payer has credit
    Life->>Life: CreditCardSplit.of(balance, price, minimumCardCharge)
    opt card > 0
      Life->>Persist: reserveCapture (REQUIRES_NEW, payment pool): CAPTURE_PENDING row + key pi-booking-id
      Life->>Gateway: chargeAndCapture (platform account, same key, transfer_group = bookingId)
      alt outcome unknown (timeout, 5xx, 409, processing)
        Life->>Life: keep CAPTURE_PENDING for ChargeOutcomeReconciler, decline nothing
      else declined (4xx)
        Life->>Persist: persistPaymentFailure
      end
      Life->>Persist: recordChargeIntent (own TX)
    end
    Life->>Persist: persistPaymentSuccess(credit, card, intentId), own TX after a charge
    Persist->>Persist: BOOKING_DEDUCTION + CAPTURED row + commission_rate
    Persist->>Booking: transition(PAYMENT_CAPTURED)
    Persist-->>Booking: BookingConfirmedEvent
    opt settle failed after the charge
      Life->>Persist: declineBookingAfterCharge: card share back as wallet credit
    end

    Credit is applied first, and only the remainder is charged to the card. A booking fully covered by credit or a pack never reserves. That keeps the UAT.3 invariant: for a booking in PAYMENT_PENDING, no booking_payments row proves no Stripe call was attempted. The Stripe call is made outside any local transaction, under a key that was committed onto the reservation before the call (ChargeKeys, V207). A Stripe rejection (4xx, or a PaymentIntent that isn't succeeded or processing) makes persistPaymentFailure mark the booking failed, with no credit debited. An unknown outcome (ChargeOutcomeUnknownException) declines nothing: the row stays CAPTURE_PENDING and ChargeOutcomeReconciler finds the PaymentIntent by its chargeKey metadata and settles or declines it. A card share below Stripe's currency minimum is re-balanced (the card takes the minimum, the credit part shrinks). While a CAPTURE_PENDING row exists, BookingService refuses a parent cancel with 409 booking.paymentInProgress. The price charged is booking.bookings.agreed_price, locked at request time (V192); a booking with no price at all is refused at accept rather than confirmed for free. A settle that fails after the charge declines the booking with its real amounts as REFUNDED_TO_CREDIT and credits the card share back as a BOOKING_REFUND (declineBookingAfterCharge); if that fails too, the reconciler retries the settle.

    For batch bookings, every card-funded booking is reserved first, then priced over the reserved set, using CreditCardSplit against the wallet. Each booking's credit and card share is decided once and committed onto the reservations with the batch key pi-batch-{batchId}-{hash of the reserved ids}, so two concurrent accepts of one batch can't share a key. There is one Stripe charge for the batch. Each booking is then confirmed in its own REQUIRES_NEW transaction that also writes its own BOOKING_DEDUCTION (spend-only credit first), so no credit leaves the wallet before the charge outcome is known. If the charge is rejected, every reserved booking is declined. A booking whose confirm fails after the charge goes through the same declineBookingAfterCharge (REFUNDED_TO_CREDIT). A reserved booking that turns out to be settled elsewhere keeps its payment row, and its charged card share is credited back as a BATCH_SHARE_REFUND (refundBatchShareToCredit).

    Coach payout lifecycle (completion-gated transfer)

    stateDiagram-v2
  [*] --> PENDING_RELEASE: completion or forfeit, card plus credit or pack share above 0
  [*] --> CANCELLED: nothing charged, pack fully allocated, or whole net lost to a chargeback
  [*] --> FROZEN: safety hold or open dispute already applies
  PENDING_RELEASE --> FROZEN: safety hold, in-app dispute or card dispute opened
  FROZEN --> PENDING_RELEASE: last freeze reason lifted, one message re-enqueued
  FROZEN --> CANCELLED: whole net refunded
  PENDING_RELEASE --> PENDING_RELEASE: partial refund cuts the net
  PENDING_RELEASE --> RELEASED: transfer ok after release_after
  PENDING_RELEASE --> HOLD: non-retryable transfer error
  PENDING_RELEASE --> CANCELLED: whole net refunded
  HOLD --> PENDING_RELEASE: account ready again or admin release-held
  HOLD --> FROZEN: re-drive finds a freeze reason
  RELEASED --> RELEASED: partial reversal, or reversal refused and booked as coach debt
  RELEASED --> REVERSED: whole net reversed
  REVERSED --> RELEASED: UPHOLD_COACH top-up transfer
  RELEASED --> REVERSAL_FAILED: no stripe_transfer_id to reverse
  HOLD --> FAILED_PERMANENT: operator SQL only
  REVERSAL_FAILED --> FAILED_PERMANENT: operator SQL only

    The row is written in the completion transaction. The outbox message is delayed to release_after. If it fires early, the handler throws so the outbox retries later. Admin's DisputeService and the card-dispute webhook lock the row with the same PESSIMISTIC_WRITE lock as the transfer handler (findByIdForUpdate, or findFundedByPaymentIntentForUpdate) before calling CoachPayoutAdjustmentService. A dispute and a transfer happening at once therefore can't both win: the handler re-reads the new status and does nothing. Raising an in-app dispute freezes the payout; resolving or dismissing it clears that freeze. After release, a refund reverses the coach's proportional share. A reversal Stripe refuses (the coach withdrew the balance) is booked as done and the amount becomes coach debt, repaid from the coach's next transfers (debt_offset_amount). Nothing moves a row to FAILED_PERMANENT automatically. An account-side HOLD recovers by itself once the account can receive transfers, or through the admin release-held endpoint; a TRANSFER_*_MISMATCH hold and REVERSAL_FAILED need an operator per docs/deployment/runbook.md. StalePayoutAlertScheduler alerts on anything stuck in FROZEN/HOLD (watch the coach.payout.held and coach.payout.reversal_failed metrics too).

    Safety-hold freeze. Only a CSAM safety hold freezes payouts (normal suspensions don't). Three gates read coach_payout_freezes: the hold itself flips pending rows to FROZEN, a completion during the hold writes the row FROZEN, and the transfer handler checks the freeze under the payout row lock right before calling Stripe. The last one is the gate the money actually passes, and it catches a row the freeze UPDATE could not see yet. A transfer that fired before the hold landed is not clawed back automatically. A retryable transfer failure is counted in coach.payout.transfer_retry{reason}. A credit-funded payout has no charge behind it and draws on the platform's available balance, so BALANCE_INSUFFICIENT is retried until charges settle.

    Stuck PAYMENT_PENDING recovery

    PaymentPendingSweeper.sweepOne takes the booking row lock (the same lock reserveCapture takes), re-checks PAYMENT_PENDING, and then decides based on the payment row:

    Payment rowAction
    NoneNo money moved, whatever the funding type. Writes CHARGE_FAILED, transitions PAYMENT_FAILED, publishes BookingDeclinedEvent, and frees the coach's slot.
    CAPTURE_PENDING, younger than booking.payment_pending.capture_pending_max_hours (default 72, bounded 6–720) or without reserved_atERROR log + booking.payment_pending.unrecoverable{reason=CAPTURE_UNCONFIRMED}. The booking is left alone. A row with a charge_idempotency_key (V207) is resolved automatically by ChargeOutcomeReconciler long before this; a row without one (pre-V207, or a credit-only batch confirm that died) needs an operator to reconcile against Stripe (runbook Scenario 4).
    CAPTURE_PENDING, older than thatMoved to CAPTURE_ABANDONED (keeping stripe_charged), the booking is released, BookingPaymentUnresolvedEvent is published, and {reason=CAPTURE_TIMEOUT} is counted. An operator still reconciles the Stripe side.

    Conventions & Gotchas

    Never skip webhook signature verification or idempotency. /api/payment/webhooks/stripe and /api/payment/webhooks/stripe/connect have to be permitAll (Stripe sends no session cookies), so Webhook.constructEvent(...) is the only protection against forged events. Any new handler must go through StripeWebhookService.handleEventAtomically (platform account) or handleConnectEventAtomically (connected accounts). Coaches are Standard accounts, so the Connect stream also carries the coach's own business events; never route those into the platform handlers.

    Stripe SDK calls stay outside @Transactional, and the split is done with a self-proxy. Services separate "call Stripe" from "persist the result". The persist half is reached through an @Autowired @Lazy self-reference so Spring's proxy applies; a direct this.foo() call skips it. SessionPackPaymentService.purchasePack is intentionally not transactional for the same reason.

    reserveCapture deadlocks with no timeout if its caller holds the booking row. It runs in REQUIRES_NEW on a second connection and locks booking.bookings. Both callers in PaymentLifecycleService carry a "DEADLOCK CONSTRAINT" comment: their own transaction must have no lock and no uncommitted write on that row. The NOWAIT hint applies only to the locked SELECT, not to a plain UPDATE. Check this before adding a write above a reservation.

    Payment's REQUIRES_NEW writes use a dedicated Hikari pool. reserveCapture, persistPaymentFailure and declineBatchBooking call RoutingDataSourceContext.set(PAYMENT_REQUIRES_NEW_DATASOURCE_KEY) in a non-transactional wrapper and then call self.xxxTransactional(...). The routing key has to be set before the proxy opens the connection, so setting it inside the @Transactional body would be too late (skillars-deferred-137). The pool is payment-requires-new-pool in infrastructure.config.DataSourceConfig.

    Stripe idempotency keys name one attempt and are persisted before the call. Booking charges use pi-booking-{bookingId}, batch charges pi-batch-{batchId}-{first 16 hex of sha256(sorted reserved ids)} (ChargeKeys), written onto the CAPTURE_PENDING rows and the PaymentIntent's chargeKey metadata, so a lost response can be looked up later. Pack purchases use pack-{parentId}-{attemptKey}. The old pi-{referenceId}-{payer}-{1-minute bucket} key survives only on the unused 4-arg chargeAndCapture overload. Transfers, reversals and refunds use deterministic keys (transfer-{bookingId}, transfer-{topUpId}, reversal-{transferId}-{reversalId}, refund-{paymentIntentId} for a pack purchase's compensating refund), so a re-run outbox row repeats the original operation instead of performing a second one. Stripe forgets keys after 24h, so transfers and reversals also look for an existing one (by transfer_group, or reversalKey metadata) before creating. A key names one logical operation: a cash-out uses cashout-{cashOutId}, because a payer can cash out several times against the same PaymentIntent and a shared key would make Stripe replay the first refund instead of issuing the second.

    The credit ledger is append-only. Rows are never updated or deleted. A failed operation is undone by writing a new row (CASH_OUT_REVERSAL, BOOKING_DEDUCTION_REVERSAL, BOOKING_REFUND_REVERSAL, CHARGEBACK_CREDIT_REVERSAL / _RESTORE), and each row carries its own spend-only split. A clawback or chargeback reversal is capped at the balance read under the wallet lock, so the wallet never goes below zero; the shortfall is absorbed by the platform. The ledger type and sign CHECKs reject anything else. Type names are constants in CreditLedgerEntryType; JPQL in ParentCreditLedgerRepository concatenates them rather than repeating literals.

    Cash-out is serialized per payer on the stripe_customers row. The ledger is append-only, so there is no balance row to lock. CreditWalletService.debitForCashOut takes StripeCustomerRepository.findByIdForUpdate (NOWAIT, inside PessimisticLockRetryer.withBoundedRetry), re-reads the balance and writes both debit rows in one transaction, so a second concurrent cash-out sees the first one's debits and fails with payment.insufficientCredit. The Stripe refund runs after that transaction commits, never inside it. CashOutConcurrencyIT pins this. Booking settlement takes the same lock: when the payer has credit, PaymentLifecycleService re-reads the balance through CreditWalletService.lockAndGetBalance (a blocking lock, not NOWAIT: settlement runs in an AFTER_COMMIT listener with no retry, so a second settlement for the same payer waits instead of being stranded in PAYMENT_PENDING) and holds the lock until its transaction writes the BOOKING_DEDUCTION, so a settlement and a cash-out (or two settlements) can't spend the same credit (SettlementCashOutRaceIT). The trade-off: on a mixed credit and card settlement the lock is held across the Stripe charge, so a cash-out at that moment may get a retryable 409. Every cash-out writes a cash_out_reconciliations row in the debit's transaction, so a crash between the debit and the refund, or an unknown refund outcome, leaves a durable record. An unknown outcome raises a CASH_OUT_UNRESOLVED admin alert (after the status commits), the re-check job retries it under the same key and the alert resolves itself when the cash-out settles, and the admin finance page shows the count and amount of unknown or stale-pending cash-outs (CashOutReconciliationIT).

    A payer with no ledger history has no balance row, so treat that as zero. Both ledgerRepository.sumByParentId and the payment.parent_credit_balance view (GROUP BY parent_id) return zero rows for that payer; the view's COALESCE only covers a null SUM. CreditWalletService.getBalance maps empty to BigDecimal.ZERO. Any new reader must do the same. CreditWalletZeroHistoryBalanceIT pins this behaviour.

    A coach can't be charged for until Stripe onboarding is COMPLETE, nor paid out until the account can receive transfers. At charge time StripePaymentGateway.resolveCoachStripeAccountId throws payment.coachStripeNotConfigured (422) unless onboarding_status = COMPLETE and charges_enabled is true (fail fast). At transfer time resolveTransferDestination applies transferReadiness: no account, an inactive capability, RESTRICTED or disconnected is a non-retryable HOLD (NO_ACCOUNT / ACCOUNT_NOT_READY), while ACCOUNT_PENDING retries. This is the most common "why did payment fail" support case.

    Pack deduction, restore, extend and pause lock the purchase row. They read with findByIdForUpdate inside PessimisticLockRetryer.withBoundedRetry. Without the lock, two devices booking against the same pack could both decrement the same remainingSessions. pausePackTransactional holds the lock for the whole method and re-runs the conflict query just before writing (D8). If a new booking appeared, it throws so the cancellations it already made roll back.

    Pack- and credit-funded sessions are paid out per completed session. Pack purchases are charged to the platform account at purchase time, and each completed pack session pays the coach its share (see the business rule above). CoachPayoutPackCreditIT pins the exact allocation under concurrent completions. A dispute on a pack session treats the pack share as the session price, so the parent is credited it and the payout is cancelled or reversed like a card payout. Transfers do not set source_transaction (card payouts never did either); they are plain transfers from the platform balance with transfer_group = booking id.

    Admin finance figures. getAdminOverview calculates:

    • gross volume as SUM(stripe_charged + credit_debited) over CAPTURED booking rows (booking value before refunds, which are a separate line; wallet credit spent on a booking counts too, so this is not cash charged to cards) plus session_pack_purchases.amount_charged (stamped at purchase, V181; legacy rows were backfilled from the tier's current price);
    • commission as the sum of coach_payouts.commission_amount over payouts RELEASED in the period (dated by released_at): what was actually deducted from coach transfers, for card, wallet-credit and pack sessions alike, at the rate stamped on each row. Pending, held and frozen payouts haven't earned it yet. Fully reversed or cancelled ones (a dispute refunded the parent) earned none. A partially reversed payout stays RELEASED and counts its commission pro rata to the net it kept;
    • refunds credited as the sum of BOOKING_REFUND, DISPUTE_CREDIT and BATCH_SHARE_REFUND rows, net of BOOKING_REFUND_REVERSALs in the period;
    • Stripe fees as an estimate from config over card money only (card-charged booking money and pack purchases), with one fixed fee per distinct charge;
    • cash-outs as CASH_OUT_DEBIT rows not reversed by a CASH_OUT_REVERSAL with the same reference_id. Rows from before the fix hold the gross requested amount and have no reference id.

    There is no subscription-revenue figure, because no subscription price amounts are stored locally. The overview also carries unresolvedCashOutCount / Amount (not period-bound). The coach summary's netPayout is the released ledger net minus reversed_amount. It carries no Stripe-fee line, because under separate charges the platform pays the processing fee, not the coach. Its refundsIssued is every BOOKING_REFUND / DISPUTE_CREDIT (net of reversals) on the coach's bookings in the period, and heldAmount / heldCount cover FROZEN and HOLD payouts.

    String status constants, not enums. BookingPaymentStatus, CoachPayoutStatus and CreditLedgerEntryType are final classes of String constants that match DB CHECK constraints. Adding a value needs a migration that widens the CHECK. For BookingPaymentStatus, also revisit isTerminal: anything that isn't CAPTURE_PENDING counts as "settled".

    Local development: Stripe webhooks without a public URL

    Stripe can only deliver webhooks to a publicly reachable URL, and localhost isn't one. For local work, use the Stripe CLI to forward test-mode events to your machine:

    stripe listen --forward-to localhost:9990/api/payment/webhooks/stripe --forward-connect-to localhost:9990/api/payment/webhooks/stripe/connect

    The first run prints a whsec_... signing secret. Set it as both APP_PAYMENT_STRIPE_WEBHOOK_SECRET and APP_PAYMENT_STRIPE_CONNECT_WEBHOOK_SECRET; the CLI signs forwarded events with its own key, which differs from any Dashboard secret. Set APP_PAYMENT_STRIPE_API_KEY to a sk_test_... key (a live key refuses to boot outside the prod profile). Set APP_PAYMENT_STRIPE_PUBLISHABLE_KEY to the matching pk_test_... key, which the frontend reads from GET /api/payment/stripe/config. The handled events are account.updated, customer.subscription.updated/deleted, invoice.payment_failed, invoice.paid, charge.dispute.created/closed and charge.refunded, so trigger those, e.g. stripe trigger customer.subscription.updated or stripe trigger charge.dispute.created. A deployed environment needs two endpoints: account.updated and account.application.deauthorized on the Connect endpoint, the rest on the account endpoint.

    Use the standard secret key (sk_test_...), not a Restricted key (rk_test_...). Stripe only accepts OAuth.token (Connect onboarding) with the account's standard secret key.

    This only works on your machine. A deployed environment such as UAT (see uat-deployment.md) needs a real webhook endpoint registered in the Stripe Dashboard.
