    Video

    Owns the full lifecycle of an uploaded video — upload, quota enforcement, three-layer content moderation, the minors-never-upload refusal, transcoding, playback authorization, and eventual archival/deletion — behind a Bunny.net provider adapter.

    Business Overview

    Skillars videos are the raw material behind drill review and skill assessment: a Coach uploads a DRILL_DEMO or COACH_REVIEW clip, and a self-registered adult Player uploads HOMEWORK footage responding to an assigned drill. Because a large share of players are minors, this module is one of the most compliance-sensitive parts of the platform: minors never upload (ADR, 2026-10-28) — a player upload whose profile is a minor, parent-managed or has no date of birth is refused at initiation with 403 VIDEO_MINOR_UPLOAD_REFUSED plus an urgent admin alert, before any quota is reserved or footage reaches the provider. Footage of a child exists only as a coach video linked to the session it reviews (V184 subject_booking_id/subject_player_id): that child's Parent, and the child themself if self-registered, may play it. Every uploaded video still passes through a three-layer moderation pipeline — CSAM matching (Arachnid), explicit-content detection (VideoIntel), and a defensive minor check that fails closed (HIDDEN + alert). Parents can also list, play and delete a linked child's own videos. Admins get a review queue (moderation-locked, failed and playback-blocked videos) plus block/restore, hard-delete and reconcile actions for when the pipeline or the Bunny.net provider gets out of sync.

    Key business rules baked into the code:

    • Coaches may only upload DRILL_DEMO or COACH_REVIEW videos; players may only upload HOMEWORK — enforced in VideoResource, not just client-side.
    • Minors never upload. MinorUploadPolicy.assertMayUpload runs on POST /player/uploads/initiate and on a player's POST /{id}/retry: a minor, parent-managed (parent_id set) or DOB-less profile gets 403 VIDEO_MINOR_UPLOAD_REFUSED and an urgent VideoModerationAdminAlertEvent (rate-limited to one per owner per hour via RateLimitingService key video.minor_upload_refused.alert — the refusal itself is never rate-limited; reaching it means an invariant broke — only adults self-register). The parental-approval flow (Story 6.6: VideoApprovalService, /api/video/approvals, /parent/approvals, the three VIDEO_APPROVAL_* emails) was removed.
    • A COACH_REVIEW must name the held session it reviews (subjectBookingId; optional for DRILL_DEMO). VideoSubjectService checks the booking is the uploading coach's own and in IN_PROGRESS/PAUSED/COMPLETED_PENDING_CONFIRMATION/COMPLETED, and takes the subject player from the booking, never from the client. Videos uploaded before V184 stay unlinked (no parent can see them).
    • Each video type has its own size/duration ceiling read from platform_config by VideoTypeConstraints (video.homework.*, video.drillDemo.*, video.coachReview.* — seeded at 250 MiB/60 s, 500 MB/120 s and 1 GiB/300 s), and storage/bandwidth are metered per owner against video.quota.<tier>.storageBytes / .bandwidthBytesMonthly. QuotaConfigService resolves the tier key from the coach's CoachSubscriptionTier (marketplace CoachProfileService.getCoachSubscriptionTier, which falls back to SCOUT unless CoachSubscriptionEntitlementPort.isPaidTierEntitled) or the player's subscription row (payment) passed through SubscriptionEntitlement.effectivePlayerTier: a paid tier counts only while the subscription is ACTIVE/TRIALLING/PAST_DUE — otherwise the player falls back to athlete, the coach to scout (#337). A missing row or unknown tier also falls back to athlete (metric recordQuotaTierFallback).
    • Storage quota is tier-gated — the Scout tier is seeded with a zero storage quota, which QuotaService.check()/reserve() reject outright.
    • A FAILED upload can be retried by its owner on the same video id (POST /api/video/{id}/retry); FAILED videos therefore stay in the owner's list — only DELETED/PURGED are hidden.
    • A coach can play a player's video only with a completed booking with that player inside platform.video.access.coach_window_days (default 90, bounded 1–3650) — see VideoAccessGuard.
    • COACH_REVIEW videos never expose a download URL (only a signed streaming URL) — download is only offered for owner-downloadable types like HOMEWORK/DRILL_DEMO (but see the download-URL gotcha below).
    • S-18, videos are uploader-only until a real scanner passes them. VideoSharingPolicy (called from VideoAccessGuard.canPlay after the admin and owner checks) denies every other viewer (parent, subject, coach) unless the video has a VIDEOINTEL scan record PASSED. VideoIntelClientImpl is a stub that now throws (fail closed) and reports isRealScanner()=false, so no video ever gets that record; the video still transcodes to READY so the uploader can watch it. app.video.moderation.requires-real-scanner (default true) is the switch. Per-video, so videos uploaded in the stub period stay private when a real scanner arrives, until they are scanned. Decision C (the real scanner) is open.
    • A CSAM match (Arachnid) immediately locks the video, alerts admins, and triggers account suspension — the most severe path in the module.
    • Subscription expiry cascades into video access: a player's READY videos get BLOCKED when their subscription lapses (unless another active subscription covers them), then ARCHIVED after 30 days, then physically purged after 90 — with a special "yearly exemption" that pauses the archival clock for YEARLY-tier subscribers.

    ServiceOrchestrates
    VideoServiceUpload initiation/retry, upload confirmation, transcoding completion/failure — the transactional core that talks to the provider adapter and the quota provider together. retryUpload 404s a caller who is not the owner (so ids can't be probed), 422s a non-FAILED video, then claims FAILED→UPLOADING atomically via VideoLifecycleService.claimFailedForRetry (a conditional UPDATE ... Each layer can be switched off by AppFeature.ARACHNID_ENABLED/VIDEOINTEL_ENABLED (recorded as SKIPPED). A FLAGGED Arachnid or VideoIntel result moves the video to LOCKED; a layer outage (FAILED) alerts admins and leaves the video in SCANNING for the SLA monitor. The minor layer is a defensive re-check that fails closed: a player video whose profile MinorUploadPolicy.refusalReason refuses (minor, managed, no DOB, profile missing) goes to HIDDEN with a FLAGGED scan and an urgent admin alert — never to transcoding.

VideoService.initializeUpload then takes FOR SHARE on the subject's player_profiles row (lockLiveProfileForShare) and refuses an erased child, so a concurrent GDPR erasure either commits first (refused) or fails its NOWAIT lock while the insert holds the share lock — it retries, or is marked FAILED and re-driven by retryFailedErasures — and its purge then sees the new row.
    VideoOwnerAccountResolverThe one mapping from a video ownerId (coach-profile UUID or PlayerProfile id) to the main."user" account behind it: resolveNotificationRecipient for owner notices (CSAM suspension resolves the owner itself, in security's AccountSuspensionEventListener) (the owner account's email; none → the notice is skipped), accountForPlayer/accountForUser. Not @Transactional, so it joins the caller's transaction or runs without one.
    PlaybackServicePlayback authorization: eligibility checks, signed URL + JWT playback token issuance, revocation-window enforcement, optional owner download URL.
    VideoAccessGuardSpEL-accessible @PreAuthorize bean (videoAccessGuard) enforcing play/delete RBAC. canPlay: PURGED gate (404 for everyone, admin included) → admin → owner → parent of owner (via ParentPlayerLink) → subject viewer (V184: a linked parent of the coach video's subjectPlayerId, or that player themself via PlayerProfile.userId) → coach with a completed booking inside platform.video.access.coach_window_days → deny. Admission is not playability: PlaybackService still refuses anything not READY/ACTIVE, HIDDEN included for everyone (the parent HIDDEN bypass went with the approval flow). canDelete: PURGED gate → admin → owner → parent of owner → deny (coaches can never delete a player's video; a subject viewer cannot delete the coach's video). isCurrentUserOwner(video) is the module's single owner check — VideoPlayResource (download URL) and VideoEventResource (/events, /status) use it.
    VideoLifecycleServiceThe operational-state machine (VALID_TRANSITIONS map — LOCKED, READY and DELETED are terminal; PROCESSING→READY is not a valid plain transition), plus reconcileToReady (the only legitimate PROCESSING→READY path, used by reconciliation), claimFailedForRetry, access-state transitions (BLOCKED/ARCHIVED, lifecycle-clock reset) and markPurged.
    VideoDeletionServiceEvery deletion path — user/parent (deleteByUser), admin (deleteByAdmin, trigger ADMIN_DELETION, also expires a PENDING upload session) and account cascade: marks PURGED, decrements storage quota, writes deletion log + outbox row. The outbox processor deletes the Bunny asset, honouring the DrillVideoRef ref-count.
    AdminVideoServiceAdmin operations: the review queue (getReviewQueue: LOCKED / FAILED / access-BLOCKED videos excluding DELETED/PURGED, 20 per page, newest updatedAt first, flagged moderation layers batch-loaded in one query), single-video lookup, force access-state changes, hard delete (VideoDeletionService.deleteByAdmin in one transaction — PURGED, quota decrement, deletion log, outbox row, pending session expired; a lost optimistic-lock race is a no-op only if the winner purged the video — then releases an ACTIVE reservation outside the transaction, retry-safe via UploadSession.quotaReleasedAt; no inline Bunny call), list upload sessions, and on-demand reconciliation returning a ReconcileResponse (state + any ReconciliationIncident written).

    WebhookEventProcessorSchedulerDrains the webhook inbox with FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED, dispatches Bunny events (upload/encoding success/failure) to state transitions, handling out-of-order delivery.
    VideoDeletionOutboxProcessorDrains the deletion outbox, calling Bunny deleteAsset; consults session.repo.DrillVideoRef ref-counting so a shared drill-demo asset isn't physically deleted while other sessions still reference it.
    QuotaReservationTimeoutService / QuotaReservationBatchExpirerReaper for the QuotaProvider contract — expires stale ACTIVE reservations under a ShedLock-guarded scheduled job.
    ModerationSlaMonitorServiceEvery app.video.moderation.sla-monitor-delay-ms (default 5 min) finds videos stuck in SCANNING longer than platform.moderation_sla_minutes; per video, in a REQUIRES_NEW transaction, it either increments the retry count and enqueues a retry, or (past platform.moderation_max_retries) marks it FAILED and enqueues an admin alert — both through ModerationOutboxSupport onto the generic platform.outbox table, so the intent commits atomically with the state change.
    ModerationRetryOutboxHandler / ModerationAdminAlertOutboxHandlerOutboxMessageHandlers for aggregate types VIDEO_MODERATION_RETRY (re-publishes VideoModerationRetryEvent; a no-op if the video is no longer SCANNING) and VIDEO_MODERATION_ADMIN_ALERT (sends synchronously via the ModerationAdminAlertSender port, implemented by notification's VideoModerationEmailListener, so a send failure retries the outbox row).
    VideoAccessCacheRequest-scoped cache for playback authorization decisions and video entity lookups, preventing redundant DB queries during access evaluation and lifecycle checks.
    VideoModerationScanPersistenceServiceThin wrapper over VideoModerationScanRepository.upsertScan — one row per video+layer, latest outcome wins.
    ReconciliationWorkerSchedulerTwo jobs. reconcile() runs every app.video.reconciliation.fixed-delay-ms (default 60 s, no ShedLock — rows are claimed with FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED), polls Bunny for non-terminal videos outside any transaction and corrects drift: provider READY + local PROCESSING → reconcileToReady (STATE_CORRECTED incident); provider DELETED → FAILED (MISSING_ASSET); provider READY + local UPLOADING → FAILED so the owner can retry. sweepOrphanedProviderAssets() (ShedLock, every 5 min) deletes Bunny assets left behind by a rolled-back initializeUpload. The admin POST .../reconcile endpoint runs the same READY/DELETED corrections for one video via AdminVideoService.triggerReconciliation.

    BandwidthResetService / BandwidthResetChunkProcessorMonthly (00:00 UTC on the 1st, ShedLock) reset of video_quotas.bandwidth_used_bytes in 500-row REQUIRES_NEW chunks — deliberately not @Transactional at the loop level so it never locks every quota row at once.

    A single upload passes through four operational states before it is playable: UPLOADING → PROCESSING → SCANNING → TRANSCODING → READY. The quota reservation made at initiate-time is only committed (storage counted against the owner) once transcoding completes, and is released on any failure path. A minor's upload never gets this far (refused at initiation); if a player video whose profile may not upload reaches the minor layer anyway, it is moved to HIDDEN and never transcoded. The external triggerTranscoding() call always happens outside a DB transaction so it never holds a connection open. Webhook delivery is asynchronous and can arrive out of order (encoding.success before upload.success, or while moderation is still SCANNING), which WebhookEventProcessorScheduler compensates for by recording Video.encodingCompletedAt and letting moderation's advance-to-TRANSCODING take a fast path to READY, rather than calling completeTranscoding() early. The webhook inbox itself is idempotent: WebhookEventOutboxService inserts with ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING on the derived event_id.

    Failed upload → owner retry

    A video lands in FAILED from an expired upload session (UploadSessionExpiryScheduler), a provider upload/encoding failure webhook, SLA-exhausted moderation, or reconciliation (provider asset missing, or provider READY while still UPLOADING). The owner sees it in GET /api/video/my (or the parent in /players/{id}/videos) and calls POST /api/video/{id}/retry: owner check (404) → state check (422) → atomic FAILED→UPLOADING claim (409 for the loser) → new quota reservation + new Bunny asset + new UploadSession, same videoId. The claim happens before the new session so ReconciliationWorkerScheduler doesn't re-fail the video mid-retry. Frontend: VideoManagementPage.vue (mounted at both player/videos and coach/videos) shows a Retry action on FAILED rows.

    Admin review queue → action

    GET /api/video/admin/videos is read-only and lock-free; every action re-reads the row through its own existing mutation. Typical paths: LOCKED (moderation flagged; flaggedLayers says which layer) → hard delete, or leave locked (LOCKED is terminal in VALID_TRANSITIONS); FAILED → reconcile against Bunny, or delete; BLOCKED → restore to ACTIVE, or leave it for the lifecycle scheduler to archive. Block/restore is PATCH .../access-state → VideoLifecycleService.setAccessState: a block from any non-BLOCKED state (or with a null clock) stamps a fresh lifecycleLockedAt; a repeat BLOCKED→BLOCKED keeps the existing clock. Frontend: src/frontend/src/pages/admin/AdminVideosPage.vue (only offers block from ACTIVE, and no block/restore on ARCHIVED — the backend itself accepts any target state except on a DELETED video).

    Quota check → reserve → commit/release

    sequenceDiagram
  participant VS as VideoService
  participant QS as QuotaService (QuotaProvider)
  participant DB as video_quota_reservations

  VS->>QS: check(ownerId, requestedBytes)
  QS-->>VS: true/false (advisory only, no lock)
  VS->>QS: reserve(ownerId, bytes, videoType)
  QS->>DB: SELECT ... FOR UPDATE (serializes per owner)
  QS->>DB: INSERT reservation status=ACTIVE, expiresAt=now+TTL
  QS-->>VS: reservationHandle (UUID)
  alt upload + transcoding succeed
    VS->>QS: commit(reservationHandle)
    QS->>DB: UPDATE ACTIVE to COMMITTED + increment storage_used_bytes (atomic CTE)
  else upload/transcoding fails or session expires
    VS->>QS: release(reservationHandle)
    QS->>DB: UPDATE ACTIVE to RELEASED (storage NOT decremented)
  end
  Note over DB: QuotaReservationTimeoutService reaps ACTIVE rows past expiresAt

    check() is advisory only — it holds no lock, so a concurrent reservation can drain quota between the check and a subsequent reserve() call; reserve() is the authoritative gate because it takes a SELECT ... FOR UPDATE row lock on the owner's quota row. commit() and release() are both single atomic SQL statements guarded by a WHERE status = 'ACTIVE' clause, which is what makes them idempotent under retries or races. Reservations that are neither committed nor released within their TTL (e.g. an abandoned upload) are cleaned up by QuotaReservationTimeoutService's ShedLock-guarded reaper — without this, quota would leak indefinitely.

    Conventions & Gotchas

    Visibility is stored here but not enforced here. The Visibility enum (PRIVATE/GROUP/UNLISTED) lives on the Video entity purely as data. Per contract/package-info.java, gating access based on Visibility is explicitly the consuming application's responsibility. Do not assume calling PlaybackService or VideoAccessGuard enforces Visibility — it doesn't. If a new caller needs Visibility-based access control, it must implement that check itself before reaching this module's playback/list APIs.

    QuotaProvider implementations must be idempotent and TTL-bounded. Per the contract's package-info.java, all methods (especially commit and release) must be safe to call more than once for the same handle, reservations must carry a TTL, and a reaper must prune orphaned reservations. QuotaService satisfies this via status-transition guards (ACTIVE→COMMITTED/ACTIVE→RELEASED) and QuotaReservationTimeoutService. Any alternative QuotaProvider implementation (e.g. for a different billing model) that skips idempotency or the reaper will leak or double-count quota under retries.

    No teamId on Video. This is deliberate per the contract package-info — the module has no concept of team ownership. Consuming applications own the linkage between teams and videos (e.g. via session's drill references). Don't add a teamId column here to "simplify" a query; join through the owning module instead.

    VideoPurgedEvent ≠ physical deletion. It fires AFTER_COMMIT when a video is marked PURGED (logical delete), but the actual Bunny.net asset removal happens later, asynchronously, via VideoDeletionOutboxProcessor. Any consumer must not treat this event as confirmation that storage/CDN cost has actually been reclaimed, and must use @TransactionalEventListener(phase = AFTER_COMMIT), never a plain @EventListener, since the publishing transaction can still roll back.

    Re-blocking must restart the lifecycle clock. lifecycleLockedAt is never cleared on BLOCKED→ACTIVE. Before the #314 fix, re-blocking a restored video kept the original block time and findBlockedExceedingThreshold archived it on the next nightly run. VideoLifecycleService.setAccessState now treats any transition into BLOCKED as a fresh block. If you add another path that sets BLOCKED, use the same rule (subscription expiry uses blockForSubscriptionExpiry(videoId, lockedAt); the YEARLY path deliberately bulk-resets the clock via VideoRepository.resetLifecycleLockedAt).

    Every delete goes through VideoDeletionService. User/parent (DELETE /api/video/{id}), admin and the account cascade all end in PURGED → quota decrement → video_deletion_log → outbox. Never call Bunny deleteAsset from a new delete path: a DRILL_DEMO asset can be shared by several sessions and only the outbox processor checks the DrillVideoRef ref-count. (Until B5 the admin path did exactly that and skipped the quota decrement and log; V182 added ADMIN_DELETION to both triggered_by CHECKs.)

    Minors never upload — and the refusal is at initiation, not after moderation. The old gate let a minor's footage reach Bunny and two moderation providers, then waited on a parent; and its approval path was unreachable anyway (it looked the parent up through PlayerProfile.parentId, but chk_pp_owner makes parent_id/user_id exclusive and only a user_id profile can upload), so a minor-owned upload would have transcoded unapproved. Now MinorUploadPolicy refuses before quota/provider work, and the moderation re-check fails closed. Do not reintroduce a "treat as adult" fallback anywhere on this path. Existing HIDDEN videos from the old gate stay HIDDEN (unplayable; owner, parent or admin can delete them).

    Retired templates stay in the enum. EmailTemplate.VIDEO_APPROVAL_* are @Deprecated, not deleted, and stay in envelope_entity_email_template_check: stored envelope/outbox/inbox rows may carry them. Their Thymeleaf files are gone, so a stale retry fails to render instead of sending an obsolete "approve this video" email. Remove the constants together with a CHECK rebuild once no stored row carries them.

    Owner identity is polymorphic and format-sniffed. ownerId is a UUID string for coach-owned videos and a decimal Long string for player-owned videos — there is no discriminator column. Guards like VideoAccessGuard and ModerationOrchestrationService distinguish the two by attempting UUID.fromString() first, falling back to Long.parseLong(). When adding new owner-aware logic, follow this same probe order rather than assuming one format. Player ownerIds are PlayerProfile ids, never user ids. Resolve the caller through PlayerProfile.userId (see VideoResource.currentPlayerProfileId, VideoAccessGuard.isOwner). Comparing the principal's businessId or username to ownerId never matches. To check ownership call VideoAccessGuard.isCurrentUserOwner; to email the account behind an ownerId use VideoOwnerAccountResolver. The account-deletion cascade keys on the same id space: AccountDeletionRequestedEvent.userId is the coach-profile UUID / player PlayerProfile id, and linkedPlayerIds are PlayerProfile ids.

    viewerId in playback is the username, not an owner id. PlaybackService uses it only for the token/revocation audit trail; the owner-only download URL is gated on the viewerIsOwner flag VideoPlayResource computes with VideoAccessGuard.isCurrentUserOwner. (B5 fixed the username-vs-ownerId comparisons that meant no download URL was ever issued, coaches got 403 on their own /events//status, and the flagged notice was addressed to the ownerId.)
