    Admin

    Admin-only tooling for trust & safety moderation, account and coach enforcement, dispute resolution, appeals, stuck-booking remediation, and GDPR data-subject requests.

    Business Overview

    Skillars is a two-sided marketplace connecting minors (Players), their Parents, and Coaches, so the platform needs a back office that lets Admins keep the marketplace safe and legally compliant without touching the consumer-facing apps. The admin module is that back office. Most of its endpoints are gated by one of two tiers (see Admin role tiers): HAS_FULL_ADMIN_ROLE (ROLE_ADMIN only) or the read/moderate tier HAS_ADMIN_ROLE (ROLE_ADMIN or ROLE_LTD_ADMIN), and are driven by the admin console pages under src/frontend/src/pages/admin/. Three controllers are end-user facing: disputes, appeals, and GDPR self-service.

    Moderation queue Account enforcement Coach enforcement Dispute resolution Appeals Booking force-complete GDPR requests

    1. Unified moderation queue. Reported messages and conversations, AI-moderation holds, flagged reviews, coach strike thresholds, raised disputes, GDPR erasure deadline overruns, orphaned Stripe subscriptions, unconfirmed credit cash-outs, safety-hold bookings and the payment-integrity alerts (chargebacks, refunds made outside the app, held or unreversed coach payouts, bookings an automatic path could not finish, sessions that ended without being started) all originate in other modules (or in admin itself). AdminAlertEventListener (plus GdprErasureService and AdminCoachEnforcementService directly) turns each into one deduplicated AdminAlert row, and AdminQueueService serves a single paginated, type-filterable queue over that table. Only status=OPEN is exposed — the API rejects any other status filter.

    2. Account enforcement (AdminUserService, V171). Admins can search any user, suspend or reactivate a non-admin account, and clear a user's failed-login rate limit. "Suspended" is the existing main."user".locked flag; suspending also revokes live sessions and, for a coach, suspends the marketplace profile in the same transaction.

    3. Coach enforcement. Coaches accumulate reliability strikes (no-shows, unexcused cancellations) via the payment module's ReliabilityStrikeService. Crossing reliability.strike.visibilityThreshold (default 3 in 30 days) sets the coach to REDUCED; crossing reliability.strike.suspensionThreshold (default 5) sets PENDING_REVIEW and publishes StrikeThresholdReachedEvent, which lands in the admin queue. There is no automatic suspension — an admin inspects the coach's strike and cancellation history, then suspends (auto-cancelling their REQUESTED bookings and, unless the admin opts out, their upcoming ones with full refunds, one transaction per booking; a booking still busy after one retry becomes a BOOKING_ACTION_REQUIRED alert), reinstates, or issues/deletes strikes. Admins also set the coach's marketplace verification tier (BASIC/TRUSTED/FEATURED).

    4. Dispute resolution. A Parent, a self-registered Player, or the booking's (non-suspended) Coach can raise a dispute on a terminal booking within disputes.submissionWindowDays (default 14); the parent/player side may also dispute a live (IN_PROGRESS/PAUSED) session, or one awaiting their confirmation (COMPLETED_PENDING_CONFIRMATION — fix/audit3-completion-dispute-integrity). Raising freezes the not-yet-released coach payout. One open dispute per booking per side (coach vs family, V213), and at most disputes.maxPerBookingPerRaiser (default 2) per side per booking — open, resolved and dismissed all count, and a parent and a child with their own login share the family's allowance (409 disputes.limitReached), so a dismissed side cannot refile to keep the payout frozen. The side, and the raised_by_role stored, come from the raiser's relationship to the booking (its coach, the player's own login, or the parent), never from their ROLE_* authorities. Raising takes the same per-booking advisory lock as resolve / dismiss, before the payout row lock. An admin reviews it alongside the payment breakdown and coach cancellation history, then resolves it with FULL_CREDIT, PARTIAL_CREDIT, NO_ACTION, COACH_WARNING or UPHOLD_COACH (V198: the payer's earlier refund was not owed — claw it back and pay the coach in full), or dismisses it. Resolution also reconciles the coach's payout and lifts the freeze — only once no other dispute on the booking is still open; the booking's single DISPUTE_RAISED alert is resolved with the last one too. Resolve and dismiss serialise per booking on a transaction-scoped advisory lock (DisputeRepository.acquireBookingDisputeLock), so the per-booking credit cap and the freeze decision see each other's outcome.

    5. Appeals (AppealService, V172, FR-ADM-004 / FR-TSC-003). A user subject to an enforcement action — a reliability strike, a coach suspension, a blocked review, or a resolved/dismissed dispute — may submit one appeal within 14 days. An admin decides it UPHELD or OVERTURNED; the decision is final, and overturning applies the reversal in the same transaction.

    6. Stuck-booking force-complete (AdminBookingCompletionService). Support can look up a booking by id and force-complete one abandoned at wrap-up with placeholder completion data, sharing BookingCompletionService.forceCompleteWithPlaceholderData with the automatic StuckCompletionSweeperService. The same page can cancel one booking (AdminBookingCancellationService, C6) with the suspension sweep's semantics: it calls BookingService.cancelBookingAsAdmin (reason ADMIN_SUPPORT), so captured money is refunded in full, the coach gets no strike, the parent gets a neutral "cancelled by the Skillars team" notice and the coach gets BOOKING_CANCELLED_BY_SKILLARS so they do not turn up (a suspension sweep sends the coach nothing per booking; they get COACH_SUSPENDED). The refund's credit-statement line reads "Cancelled by Skillars", not "Coach unavailable". Live (IN_PROGRESS/PAUSED) and in-flight-capture bookings are refused with the outcome as the reason (always a 200, like force-complete). Every attempt is audited as BOOKING_ADMIN_CANCEL (V189, which also widens the envelope template CHECK) with the outcome appended, and a cancellation resolves the booking's open SAFETY_HOLD_BOOKING alert in the same transaction. That alert's row in the moderation queue links to the booking page, which is how an admin follows up on a safety hold that could not cancel a booking at hold time.

    GDPR is a legal deadline, not a nice-to-have. Any authenticated user can request an export or erasure of their own data (Articles 15/17/20), and since V172 a Parent can do the same for a parent-owned (shadow) child profile, which has no account of its own. Erasure anonymises the user, hard-deletes messages, deletes/anonymises reviews, scrubs coach-authored narrative, cascades through every development-tracking table, and revokes refresh tokens. Exports are a ZIP behind a signed, time-limited URL. Both run after the request row commits; failed erasures are re-driven daily by GdprErasureRetryScheduler. Get the wiring wrong and you silently miss the regulatory deadline.

    Admin role tiers (S-28)

    ROLE_LTD_ADMIN is a genuinely limited back-office tier (support / moderation staff); ROLE_ADMIN is the full administrator. Two constants in SecurityConstants express it: HAS_ADMIN_ROLE = ADMIN or LTD_ADMIN (read and moderate) and HAS_FULL_ADMIN_ROLE = ADMIN only. The default for a new admin endpoint is HAS_FULL_ADMIN_ROLE; the shared tier is read-only plus plain moderation. If a case is ambiguous it is ADMIN-only — loosening later is cheap, un-leaking is not.

    AreaLTD_ADMIN (and ADMIN)ADMIN only
    Queues & alertsView the moderation queue and summary; mark safety-hold and payment-integrity alerts handledAlert-rule administration (/v1/admin/alerts), SES preflight
    Content moderationReview queue approve/block; message detail, approve/block; conversation detail; video review queue, video detail and upload sessions (read)Unblock a conversation; set video access state; delete video; reconcile video
    UsersSearch and view users (read-only)Suspend, reactivate, unlock login; any role/authority change
    CoachesEnforcement list and profile (read-only)Suspend, reinstate, issue/delete strike, verification tier
    Disputes & appealsQueue and detail (read-only)Resolve / dismiss a dispute; decide an appeal
    BookingsBooking lookup (read-only)Force-complete, admin cancel
    Money—Finance overview and coach revenue, release held payouts, refunds, clawbacks, disputes credits
    Personal data—GDPR request list (and any erasure/export action)
    Configuration—All of /api/config (read and write); actuator details (/manage/**, health component details)
    PlannedS-09 safeguarding-badge inspection: ADMIN only for now (can be loosened by switching the constant).

    Enforcement, three layers.

    • Controllers: per-method @PreAuthorize, never per class where read and write are mixed.
    • Services: the sensitive operations (user suspend/reactivate/unlock, coach suspend/reinstate/strike/tier, dispute resolve/dismiss, appeal decide, booking force-complete/cancel, ConfigService.updateConfig, payout re-drive) call AdminTierGuard.requireFullAdmin(), which refuses a caller holding ROLE_LTD_ADMIN without ROLE_ADMIN. It lets a null/system authentication through on purpose, because listeners, jobs and tests call the same services.
    • Guard test: AdminRoleTierAuditTest scans every @RestController by reflection. It fails if an admin handler (path contains /admin or a role expression names ADMIN/LTD_ADMIN) uses neither constant, if a POST/PUT/PATCH/DELETE handler uses the shared tier without being on the in-test allow-list (SHARED_TIER_WRITES, with reasons), or if a service method uses the shared constant. AdminTierWebMvcTest proves 403/200 over HTTP.

    The UI hint: the login response and the skp cookie carry limitedAdmin (role stays ADMIN for routing). The SPA hides finance/GDPR/config navigation, redirects direct visits to /admin/requires-administrator and hides action buttons on the shared pages; the server enforces the tier regardless. An LTD_ADMIN cannot grant roles (no endpoint grants roles at all today); to create one, see docs/deployment/runbook.md → "Create a limited administrator".

    UserErasedEvent, AccountDeletionRequestedEventPublished (to security and cascades)Consumed by UserErasedEventListener and AccountDeletionCascadeListener.
    CoachSuspensionNotificationEvent, CoachReinstatedEvent, CoachWarningIssuedEvent, DisputeResolvedEvent, DisputeCoachUpheldEvent, DisputeDismissedEvent, GdprExportReadyEvent, ReviewModerationResolvedEvent (class in reviews)Published (to notification)Admin-outcome notices. Each carries an AdminNotificationRecipient (email + language) resolved by the publisher in its own transaction by AdminNotificationRecipientResolver; notification.AdminOutcomeEmailListener (BEFORE_COMMIT) turns it into an outbox email + inbox row. See the gotcha below.
    CoachSuspendedEventPublishedThe always-published domain fact (also for a safety hold, adminId null). Deliberately not emailed — the "notify coach" choice lives on CoachSuspensionNotificationEvent.
    AccountSuspensionRequestedEventConsumed (from video, CSAM match)CoachSafetySuspensionListener: when ownerId is a CoachProfile UUID, suspends the marketplace profile (suspendCoachForSafetyHold). The account side is security.AccountSuspensionEventListener.

    Dependencies: security (principal, roles, user/refresh-token/login-attempt data, the playerOwnershipGuard); booking (bookings disputed, cancelled, force-completed or blocking erasure; admin implements booking's BookingDisputeLookup port); marketplace (coach profiles, pricing, verification tier; photo and gallery media scrubbed on erasure); payment (strikes, cancellation history, session packs, subscriptions, and for disputes DisputeCreditService, RefundClawbackService and CoachPayoutAdjustmentService, which own the wallet and payout writes); the booking module's no-show events (CoachNoShowEvent, a late BookingCancelledByParentEvent: the repeat-claimant alert) and payment's PaymentIntegrityAlertEvent feed the queue; messaging and reviews (moderated content); development and session (erasure cascade incl. the coach-branding logo, and child export); filestorage and the blob-deletion outbox (export ZIP, erased blobs); config (windows, thresholds, lock timeouts). From infrastructure: security constants, PessimisticLockRetryer, exceptions, RoutingDataSourceContext / DataSourceConfig.GDPR_ERASURE_DATASOURCE_KEY, and feature properties.

    Key Flows

    Coach enforcement: strike threshold to suspension

    sequenceDiagram
  participant Payment as payment.ReliabilityStrikeService
  participant Listener as AdminAlertEventListener
  participant Alerts as admin_alerts
  participant Admin as Admin (console)
  participant Enforce as AdminCoachEnforcementService
  participant Booking as booking.BookingRepository
  Payment->>Payment: count >= suspensionThreshold: status PENDING_REVIEW
  Payment->>Listener: StrikeThresholdReachedEvent(coachId)
  Listener->>Alerts: REQUIRES_NEW insert STRIKE_THRESHOLD (dedup on OPEN)
  Admin->>Enforce: GET /api/admin/coaches/{coachId}/enforcement
  Enforce-->>Admin: status, tier, strikes, cancellation history
  Admin->>Enforce: POST /api/admin/coaches/{coachId}/suspend
  Note over Enforce: TX 1 - applyAdminSuspension
  Enforce->>Enforce: findByIdForUpdate (bounded retry), no-op if already SUSPENDED
  Enforce->>Enforce: SUSPENDED, CoachSuspendedEvent (+ CoachSuspensionNotificationEvent if notifyCoach)
  Enforce->>Alerts: AdminActionLog(COACH_SUSPEND)
  Enforce->>Booking: findIdsByCoachIdAndStatusIn(REQUESTED, plus ACCEPTED to UPCOMING unless opted out)
  loop one REQUIRES_NEW TX per booking
    Enforce->>Booking: cancelBookingForSuspension - cancelBookingAsAdmin (row lock, CANCEL_ADMIN)
    Booking-->>Payment: BookingCancelledByAdminEvent (refund if captured, neutral parent notice, no strike)
  end
  Enforce->>Booking: retry busy ones once (row lock held or capture in flight)
  Enforce->>Alerts: still busy - PaymentIntegrityAlertEvent BOOKING_ACTION_REQUIRED
  Note over Enforce: notifyCoach - AdminOutcomeEmailListener enqueues COACH_SUSPENDED (BEFORE_COMMIT of TX 1)

    Safety hold. A CSAM match on a coach's video publishes AccountSuspensionRequestedEvent with the video's ownerId, which for a coach upload is the CoachProfile UUID. CoachSafetySuspensionListener (AFTER_COMMIT, fallbackExecution, never throws) calls suspendCoachForSafetyHold (REQUIRES_NEW), which runs the same locked suspension in two transactions: applySafetyHoldStatus (REQUIRES_NEW) commits the profile SUSPENDED and the COACH_SAFETY_HOLD row first, then sweepBookingsForSafetyHold (REQUIRES_NEW) cancels every not-yet-started booking (REQUESTED, ACCEPTED, PAYMENT_PENDING, CONFIRMED, UPCOMING) through the state machine (CANCEL_ADMIN, actor id 0) with full refunds - so booking-row contention can fail the sweep (the listener retries the whole, idempotent hold three times) but never undo the takedown. A coach an admin had already suspended is converted (new statusChangedAt + the COACH_SAFETY_HOLD row) and swept too; a coach already under a safety hold is only re-swept. It does not notify the coach, and is logged as COACH_SAFETY_HOLD with admin_id = 0 (AdminActionLog.SYSTEM_ACTOR_ID; the column is NOT NULL and has no FK) at the same instant as the profile's statusChangedAt. That row is what makes the hold non-appealable: AppealService answers 409 appeals.notAppealable on submit and never reverses it on overturn (an admin can still reinstate directly from the console). What the hold cannot cancel server-side - an IN_PROGRESS / PAUSED session, or a PAYMENT_PENDING booking whose Stripe capture is in flight - raises one urgent SAFETY_HOLD_BOOKING admin alert per booking (reason IN_PROGRESS / PAUSED / PAYMENT_IN_FLIGHT); the queue summary resolves the parent's email at read time so the admin can contact them (the alert row stores no contact PII). The hold never fails on those. An admin marks one handled from the moderation queue (POST /api/admin/queue/safety-hold-alerts/{alertId}/resolve, an atomic conditional UPDATE; 404 once it is no longer open). The payout freeze for a held coach belongs to the payment module and keys off the COACH_SAFETY_HOLD row. CoachSafetySuspensionListener runs at HIGHEST_PRECEDENCE because with no transaction the multicaster stops at the first listener that throws, and this one never throws.

    Suspension is not just a status flip (DECISIONS item 2): every REQUESTED booking for the coach is cancelled and, by default, so is every ACCEPTED / PAYMENT_PENDING / CONFIRMED / UPCOMING one (request flag cancelUpcomingBookings, default true, a checkbox in the suspend dialog; the response reports cancelledBookings). Each goes through the booking state machine (BookingService.cancelBookingAsAdmin, cancel reason COACH_UNAVAILABLE for both paths - neutral, because the booking row reaches the parent's GDPR export; the real reason is on the coach's admin_action_log row), so the batch roll-up, SSE and session-plan listeners see a BookingStatusChangedEvent; a booking whose fresh status (re-read under its row lock) is no longer cancellable is skipped. Its BookingCancelledByAdminEvent refunds the payer in full when payment was captured (card/credit to the credit wallet, pack session restored, expired pack refunded to the wallet), issues no reliability strike, and sends the parent a neutral "the coach is unavailable" notice. Since #361 (fix/payment-wallet-dispute-integrity) the suspension is not all-or-nothing: the profile goes SUSPENDED and commits first (so every accept path rejects from then on), then each booking is cancelled in its own REQUIRES_NEW transaction, its refund and notices committing with it. A booking whose row lock is held or whose capture is in flight is retried once after the rest; one still busy is skipped with a BOOKING_ACTION_REQUIRED alert (reason SUSPENSION_CANCEL_SKIPPED …) and counted in alertedBookings. The old 409 admin.coaches.paymentInProgress is no longer thrown (the admin pages still map it). Live sessions are left to finish. No payout freeze for an ordinary suspension. The coach-profile row lock matters: the booking accept paths re-check SUSPENDED under the same lock, so a suspension cannot slip in between their check and write. reinstateCoach runs CoachProfileService.validateReadyForActivation first and falls back to DRAFT if the profile is incomplete, ensures a SCOUT coach_subscriptions row exists, and then re-checks the in-window strike count: below the visibility threshold it resolves the STRIKE_THRESHOLD alert, otherwise it re-publishes StrikeThresholdReachedEvent so the alert stays open. Explicit admin intent wins: a reinstate proceeds even if another admin just suspended.

    sequenceDiagram
  participant Admin as Admin (console)
  participant Svc as AdminUserService
  participant Users as main.user
  participant Tokens as refresh_tokens
  participant Enforce as AdminCoachEnforcementService
  Admin->>Svc: POST /api/admin/users/{userId}/suspend {reason, cancelUpcomingBookings}
  Svc->>Svc: reject self (400)
  Svc->>Users: findByIdForUpdate (NO_WAIT, bounded retry)
  Svc->>Svc: reject ADMIN / LTD_ADMIN target (409)
  Svc->>Users: lock(), clear persistent tokens, saveAndFlush
  Svc->>Users: invalidateSessionsForUserInCurrentTransaction (stamp)
  Svc->>Tokens: markAllUsedByUserId (REQUIRES_NEW)
  Svc->>Enforce: suspendCoach(coachId, notifyCoach=false, cancelUpcomingBookings) if a coach profile exists
  Svc->>Users: AdminActionLog(USER_SUSPEND)

    The coach's status step joins this transaction (a failure there rolls the account suspension back), while each booking cancellation of the sweep still commits on its own — the sweep never writes the user or coach row this transaction holds, so the REQUIRES_NEW steps do not wait on it. Every authentication path already enforces locked. The residual window is a still-valid JWT on the filter's no-DB fast path until its next DB re-auth (5 min). Suspend is idempotent (an already-locked account writes no second audit row but still makes sure the coach profile is suspended). Reactivating the account does not reinstate the coach profile, and is refused (admin.users.erased) when a self-scoped erasure request exists for the user — a parent's erasure of a child does not block it. It is also refused (admin.users.safetyHold) for an account in verificationStatus = SUSPENDED, the child-safety suspension the CSAM scan applies (AccountSuspensionEventListener, security module).

    Dispute resolution

    sequenceDiagram
  participant Party as Parent / Player / Coach
  participant DisputeApi as DisputeResource
  participant DisputeSvc as DisputeService
  participant Listener as AdminAlertEventListener
  participant Admin as Admin (console)
  participant Wallet as payment.DisputeCreditService
  participant Payout as payment.CoachPayoutAdjustmentService
  Party->>DisputeApi: POST /api/disputes {bookingId, reason, details}
  DisputeApi->>DisputeSvc: raiseDispute(...)
  DisputeSvc->>DisputeSvc: reason, owner, eligible status, window, no open dispute
  DisputeSvc->>Payout: freezeForDispute(bookingId)
  DisputeSvc-->>Listener: DisputeRaisedEvent
  Listener->>Listener: DISPUTE_RAISED alert (referenceId = bookingId)
  Admin->>DisputeSvc: GET /api/admin/disputes/queue, then /{disputeId}
  Admin->>DisputeSvc: POST /api/admin/disputes/{disputeId}/resolve
  DisputeSvc->>DisputeSvc: 409 if booking IN_PROGRESS, PAUSED or COMPLETED_PENDING_CONFIRMATION
  alt FULL_CREDIT, PARTIAL_CREDIT, COACH_WARNING or NO_ACTION
    DisputeSvc->>Wallet: remainingRefundable, then credit(DISPUTE_CREDIT) unless NO_ACTION
    DisputeSvc->>Payout: applyRefund (reversal if RELEASED, pro-rata cut if not), lift dispute freeze
  else UPHOLD_COACH
    DisputeSvc->>Wallet: RefundClawbackService.clawBack (BOOKING_REFUND_REVERSAL)
    DisputeSvc->>Payout: payInFull(bookingId, coachId)
  end
  DisputeSvc->>DisputeSvc: RESOLVED, resolve alert, AdminActionLog(DISPUTE_RESOLVE)
  DisputeSvc-->>Party: DisputeResolvedEvent - DISPUTE_RESOLVED email + inbox row (BEFORE_COMMIT)

    Eligible reasons are COACH_NO_SHOW, SESSION_QUALITY, SAFETY_CONCERN, UNAUTHORISED_CHARGE, OTHER; eligible booking statuses are COMPLETED, CANCELLED* and NO_SHOW_*, plus IN_PROGRESS / PAUSED when the parent or player raises it (e.g. a coach who pressed Start, then Pause, and never coached; the coach cannot dispute a live session). Such a dispute can only be resolved once the booking has settled — while it is IN_PROGRESS, PAUSED or COMPLETED_PENDING_CONFIRMATION resolve answers 409 disputes.bookingNotSettled (no payout exists yet to reconcile); dismissing stays possible. The submission window is measured from Booking.getStatusAnchor() (when the booking reached that status, V178 status_changed_at; updated_at only while that is NULL), so a later unrelated write to the booking cannot reopen it. Ownership resolves the player through PlayerProfile.userId (Booking.playerId is a profile id) and the coach through CoachProfile.userId. Credit is computed only from a CAPTURED BookingPayment (credit debited + Stripe charged); otherwise FULL_CREDIT issues nothing and logs a warning. Credits are capped per booking by DisputeCreditService.remainingRefundable (price minus the cancellation refund, pending or written, minus earlier dispute credits): FULL_CREDIT credits the remainder, or answers 409 disputes.alreadyRefunded when nothing is left; PARTIAL_CREDIT / COACH_WARNING above the remainder are a 400. The credit is its own ledger kind, DISPUTE_CREDIT (V208, reference = booking id). The coach's payout is cut by its proportional share of what was actually credited — reversed if already RELEASED, cut pro rata if still PENDING_RELEASE / HOLD / FROZEN (cancelled only when the cut is the whole net) — and the dispute freeze is lifted, so the payout releases unless another freeze (safety hold, chargeback) still holds it.

    UPHOLD_COACH (V198) is for a booking whose payer was refunded (wallet BOOKING_REFUND or DISPUTE_CREDIT, or a restored pack session) when the refund was not owed. RefundClawbackService.clawBack takes it back as one BOOKING_REFUND_REVERSAL (capped at the wallet balance; the rest is absorbed by the platform, and a restored pack session may be re-deducted), the dispute is flushed RESOLVED with recovered_amount / absorbed_amount, and then CoachPayoutAdjustmentService.payInFull pays the coach (flushing first keeps the new payout from being frozen by this very dispute). Once per booking: a second one, or a booking never refunded, is 400 disputes.noRefundToReverse. The audit reason is prefixed with the amounts, and besides the raiser's DisputeResolvedEvent the other side gets DisputeCoachUpheldEvent (DISPUTE_COACH_UPHELD). Dismissal lifts the dispute freeze only, and is audited as DISPUTE_DISMISS (V183; before that it was logged as DISPUTE_RESOLVE, so older dismissal rows still carry that type — the dispute's own status tells them apart). Both outcomes notify the raiser (DISPUTE_RESOLVED / DISPUTE_DISMISSED; a minor player raiser's parent instead), and a COACH_WARNING resolution notifies the coach (COACH_WARNING_ISSUED). While a dispute is open — or after a RESOLVED outcome other than NO_ACTION — the booking's session recap is locked (DisputeRepository.existsRecapLockingDispute, exposed to booking via DisputeRecapLockLookup).

    Overturning applies the reversal only if the appealed enforcement still stands: a strike already deleted, a coach since reinstated and re-suspended (different statusChangedAt), or a review no longer BLOCKED records reversalApplied = false. Every decision is written to admin_action_log as APPEAL_UPHOLD / APPEAL_OVERTURN (V183, reference = appeal id, reason = subject + reversal outcome + note, capped at 500 chars); the reversal's own actions are logged by the services that perform them, and a reinstated coach / approved review notifies its owner.

    Self erasure is refused while a self-scoped export or erasure is active, or while a coach has REQUESTED/ACCEPTED bookings. Child erasure is refused while the child has any live booking (GdprRequestService.CHILD_ERASURE_BLOCKING_BOOKING_STATUSES), an active export, or is already erased (developmentDataErasedAt set). The child check and the request insert run under the child's player_profiles row lock, which every booking-creation path also takes before refusing a child with an in-flight (PENDING/PROCESSING/FAILED) erasure request (booking.playerNotBookable), so a new booking and a child-erasure request cannot both succeed. Self erasure anonymises the user (deleted.<id>@erased.io), hard-deletes messages, deletes or anonymises reviews (APPROVED ones are anonymised so ratings survive), revokes refresh tokens and deletes prior export blobs; a COACH additionally has profile free text, the profile photo (photo_url cleared), gallery media, the report-branding row (development.coach_branding deleted) — with the photo, gallery and logo blobs enqueued on the blob-deletion outbox, but only keys under their own upload slots (coach_profile/, coach_gallery/, coach_branding/), never a legacy URL or a server-written key — recap narrative (session completion data, timeline metadata, performance report next_steps — scrubbed, not deleted) and availability-block reasons scrubbed; a PARENT/PLAYER has each child's development data deleted under a per-child lock budget. Child erasure runs the same per-child hook and also scrubs the profile identity, committed with the developmentDataErasedAt tombstone; bookings, payment/credit rows, messages and videos are deliberately kept.

    Conventions & Gotchas

    GDPR work runs synchronously on the HTTP request thread. GdprEventListener is a plain (non-@Async) @TransactionalEventListener(phase = AFTER_COMMIT), so the export build or erasure is added to the response time of the POST. That is why erasure has a gdprEraseLockBudget, a Hikari pre-check (assertConnectionPoolNotSaturated) and its own routed connection pool (RoutingDataSourceContext.set(GDPR_ERASURE_DATASOURCE_KEY), always cleared in finally). The listener must never be @Transactional; the services open their own REQUIRES_NEW transactions via self proxies.

    A failed erasure is FAILED, never silently COMPLETED. Any skipped or contended scrub marks the request FAILED and raises a GDPR_ERASURE_DEADLINE alert (one per distinct reason). If erase() throws, markFailed commits the FAILED status in one REQUIRES_NEW transaction and then raises the alert in another — a duplicate-alert race must not roll back the status. retryFailedErasures bumps retry_count in its own transaction before each attempt so the 3-attempt cap survives a failing re-drive.

    A child-scoped GDPR request's user_id is the parent. Always branch on playerId: routing a child request to erase() would anonymise the parent's own account. Self-scoped lookups use the ...PlayerIdIsNull... repository methods for the same reason.

    Admin outcomes are notified through the outbox, with the recipient resolved by the publisher. Suspension (only when the admin ticks "notify coach"), reinstatement, dispute warning, dispute resolved / dismissed, the upheld-coach notice to the non-raising party, GDPR export ready and review approved / blocked each publish an event carrying an AdminNotificationRecipient; notification.AdminOutcomeEmailListener enqueues the email and inbox row BEFORE_COMMIT in the decision's transaction (all nine templates are transactional, never opt-out-able). The exception is GdprExportReadyEvent: handled AFTER_COMMIT in its own transaction, so a failed notice can never roll the export back to PENDING. DisputeResolvedEvent carries the amount actually credited (zero for a FULL_CREDIT on a zero-price booking), not the requested outcome. Resolve recipients with AdminNotificationRecipientResolver: a coach by CoachProfile.userId, a user id directly, a minor player with a linked parent redirected to the parent (GDPR export is the exception — it goes to the requester, whose profile page holds the download). The signed export URL is never emailed. A null recipient skips the email with a WARN.

    Coach erasure: no row lock or native statement on coach_profiles before the recap scrub. eraseTransactional's outer transaction must not hold the coach's coach_profiles row while the REQUIRES_NEW recap scrub runs — its first statement (markRecapDataErased) locks that row, so it would wait on its own caller into the 5 s lock_timeout and the erasure would end FAILED every time. The outer save(cp) is only flushed at commit, but any native query auto-flushes it: that is why the branding-logo DELETE … RETURNING runs after the scrub, and why the photo key is read from the unlocked snapshot (a photo confirmed in the same milliseconds is unlinked but its blob is not enqueued — accepted).

    Alert inserts are isolated on purpose. AdminAlertEventListener.insertAlert runs saveAndFlush inside a REQUIRES_NEW TransactionTemplate and catches DataIntegrityViolationException outside the callback. Catching inside does not work: a failed flush marks the session rollback-only and the template's commit throws UnexpectedRollbackException. saveAndFlush (not save) matters because AdminAlert.alertId is GenerationType.UUID, which defers the INSERT to commit. Several handlers are additionally annotated REQUIRES_NEW because they are published from inside messaging's moderation/retention transactions and Stripe webhook handling — a failed alert must never roll back the publisher's work.

    Adding an AdminAlertType, AdminAlertReferenceType or AdminActionType needs a migration. All three columns carry CHECK constraints; JPA will happily write a value the CHECK rejects, failing at INSERT time. V193/V208/V209 (alerts) and V183/V189 (action log) are the recent worked examples — drop and recreate the constraint as the union of every value allowed so far, with SET LOCAL lock_timeout (parallel branches widening the same CHECK conflict on merge). New alert types also need a summary case in AdminQueueService and a count in the queue summary; a workflow-less one usually just joins AdminAlertType.PAYMENT_INTEGRITY, which gives it the bucket and the resolve endpoint for free.

    Queues expose OPEN only. /api/admin/queue and /api/admin/disputes/queue return 400 for any status other than OPEN; the appeals queue accepts any AppealStatus. Alerts are deduplicated per (reference_id, type) while OPEN.

    Strike deletion can re-tier a coach. For a PENDING_REVIEW/REDUCED coach and an in-window strike, deleteStrike recounts under the coach row lock: still at or above the suspension threshold leaves the status; at or above visibility sets REDUCED; below both runs activation validation and returns the coach to ACTIVE (resolving the STRIKE_THRESHOLD alert and auditing COACH_REINSTATE). The delete is a bulk deleteByIdAndCoachId (0 rows → 404 for the losing concurrent caller). ConfigStartupAssertion refuses to boot if visibilityThreshold > suspensionThreshold.

    Enforcement reads are REPEATABLE_READ. getEnforcementProfile and getCoachesUnderEnforcement read status and strike counts in one snapshot. Spring silently ignores the isolation if an outer transaction is already open — do not call them from inside a test-level @Transactional/TransactionTemplate.

    Verification tier is written by bulk UPDATE. CoachProfile.verificationTier is updatable = false so an unlocked coach-side save can never write a stale tier back; setVerificationTier uses CoachProfileRepository.updateVerificationTier under the row lock.

    Two classes are called PlatformConfig. admin.config.PlatformConfig is a Spring @Configuration; config.repo.PlatformConfig is the main.platform_config entity. Check the import.

    ReviewModerationLog lives in the reviews schema even though the entity is in admin.repo.
