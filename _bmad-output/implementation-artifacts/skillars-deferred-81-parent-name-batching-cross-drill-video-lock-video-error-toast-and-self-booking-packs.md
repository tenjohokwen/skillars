# Story skillars-deferred-81: Parent/player name-lookup batching, cross-drill video lock, video-error toast, and self-booking session-pack purchase

Status: ready-for-dev

## Story

As the platform owner,
I want `BookingService`'s per-row name-lookup N+1 closed, `DrillUploadService`'s upload/delete lock widened to cover two drills that share one `videoId`, a visible notification when a drill/session video fails to load instead of a silent background refetch, and a self-registered (no-parent) player to be able to purchase a session pack instead of being limited to pay-per-session,
so that booking-list endpoints scale with row count instead of firing one extra query per row, a coach cloning a `PLATFORM` drill can no longer race a concurrent delete on the source drill into losing or double-publishing the shared video's deletion event, a coach or player sees a clear signal when a video genuinely fails rather than staring at a broken player, and a self-booking player gets full purchase-option parity with a parent-managed player.
